Policz pełny miesięczny i całkowity koszt najmu biura lub sklepu.

Jak działa: Kalkulator kosztów najmu lokalu komercyjnego

Szczegółowe wyjaśnienie jest po angielsku.

Landlords quote rent per square foot or square metre per year, but the real cost includes operating costs, taxes and insurance (CAM or triple net charges), fixed extras such as parking, yearly rent escalation and sometimes free months. Enter them and get the cost in year 1 per month, a year-by-year table, the total over the lease term and the true all-in cost per unit of area.

Each year the base rent and operating costs are multiplied by (1 + escalation)^(year − 1); fixed monthly extras stay constant. Free rent months are credited against the base rent of the first months. The all-in cost per unit is the average yearly total divided by the area. Use the same area definition as the lease (usable, rentable, load factor) and remember that deposits, tenant improvements, brokerage fees and sales or VAT on rent are not included here. It is an estimate for comparing offers, not a legal or tax document.

Jak z tego korzystać

  1. Enter the area and the yearly price per unit.

  2. Add operating costs, extras, escalation and term.

  3. Compare the all-in cost with other offers.

Najczęstsze pytania

What does NNN or triple net mean?

The tenant pays taxes, insurance and maintenance in addition to base rent, so the quoted rent understates the total cost.

Is rent per sq ft quoted per month or per year?

In the US usually per year, in many other markets per month. Check the quote before comparing.

Czy moje pliki są gdzieś przesyłane?

Nie. Plik otwiera i przetwarza Twoja własna przeglądarka. Możesz to sprawdzić: otwórz kartę sieci w przeglądarce podczas pracy albo odłącz internet po załadowaniu strony.

Czy to narzędzie jest darmowe?

Tak, w całości. Nie ma rejestracji, płatnej wersji ani znaku wodnego na plikach.
