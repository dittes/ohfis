Free online video tools

Trim, join, convert, compress, crop, resize and edit videos without uploading them. Every tool runs in your browser. Nothing you paste or open is uploaded.

Video Trimmer (Cut Video Online) Cut a video to the part you need, with or without re-encoding. Video Merger (Join Videos Online) Join several clips into one video, in the order you choose. Video Converter (MP4, WebM, GIF, MP3) Convert videos between MP4, WebM and GIF, or take just the sound as MP3. Video Compressor (Reduce Video File Size) Make a video file much smaller for email, chat or upload. Crop, Rotate and Flip Video Crop to an aspect ratio or area, rotate by 90° steps and mirror a video. Video Resizer (Change Video Resolution) Scale a video to 1080p, 720p, 480p or an exact size. Change Video Speed (Slow Motion and Fast Forward) Slow a video down or speed it up, with the sound following. Loop Video (Repeat a Video) Repeat a clip several times to make a longer video. Change Video Volume or Mute a Video Make the sound of a video louder or quieter, or remove it. Add Audio or Music to a Video Replace the sound of a video with music or a voice-over, or mix both. Add Text to a Video Put a title, caption or message on a video, at a position and time you choose. Add Image or Logo to a Video Place a logo or picture on a video, with size, opacity and timing. Remove Logo or Watermark from a Video Smooth over a small logo or mark in a corner of a video. Video to GIF Converter Turn a part of a video into an animated GIF with good colours. Video Stabilizer (Reduce Camera Shake) Smooth out small shakes in hand-held video.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.

Privacy Imprint Runs in your browser. No upload, no account, no tracking.
