How Add Margins or Note Space to a PDF works

Add space to the top, right, bottom or left of every page of a PDF without scaling the content. Typical uses: a wide right margin for handwritten notes (with ruled lines if you like), a left margin for binding or hole punching, or extra white space around full-bleed pages for printing.

The page is enlarged and the original content is placed unchanged at the correct offset, so text, images and links keep their size and sharpness. The added margins are blank and the ruled lines are optional. The result is a new PDF, the original is untouched. Interactive form fields and annotations of the original may not survive the embedding; flatten forms first if they matter.

How to use it

  1. Choose the PDF.

  2. Enter the margin for each side in millimetres.

  3. Download the enlarged PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Is the content scaled?

No. It keeps its size; only the page becomes larger.

Can I add a binding margin only on odd pages?

Not in this version: margins are the same on every page.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
