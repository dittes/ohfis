How Merge Two Lists into Columns works

Combine a column of names with a column of emails, or two exports that belong together, without a spreadsheet. Paste list A and list B, choose the separator and copy the aligned result. With a tab separator the result pastes straight into Excel or Google Sheets as two columns.

The lists are joined line by line. If one list is longer, the missing cells stay empty so the rows keep lining up. Padding mode adds spaces after the first column so the second column starts at the same position on every line, which gives a neat plain-text table.

How to use it

  1. Paste the first list on the left and the second on the right.

  2. Choose a separator such as Tab, comma or dash.

  3. Copy the merged lines.

Frequently asked questions

How do I paste the result into Excel as two columns?

Use the Tab separator, copy the result and paste into cell A1. Excel splits it into columns automatically.

What if the lists have different lengths?

The shorter list is padded with empty values so all rows are kept.
