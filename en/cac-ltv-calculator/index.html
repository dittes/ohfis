How CAC & LTV Calculator works

Is acquiring customers worth what it costs? Enter average revenue per account, gross margin, monthly churn and CAC to see the lifetime value, the LTV:CAC ratio and the number of months until a customer has paid back their acquisition cost.

Customer lifetime = 1 ÷ monthly churn. LTV = revenue per account × gross margin × lifetime. LTV:CAC = LTV ÷ CAC, and payback months = CAC ÷ (revenue per account × gross margin). A ratio of 3:1 or more and a payback under 12 months are the usual benchmarks. The formula assumes constant churn and revenue; cohorts with expansion revenue are worth more.

How to use it

  1. Enter revenue per account, gross margin, churn and CAC.

  2. Read LTV, ratio and payback.

  3. Test how lower churn or cheaper acquisition changes the ratio.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good LTV:CAC ratio?

Around 3:1. Much higher can mean you are under-investing in growth; below 1:1 you lose money on every customer.

Which margin should I use?

Gross margin: revenue minus the direct cost of serving the customer (hosting, support, payment fees).
