Free online text tools

Clean, convert, count, compare and extract text. Every tool runs in your browser. Nothing you paste or open is uploaded.

Zero-Width Character Remover Find and delete invisible characters such as zero-width spaces and BOMs. Case Converter Switch text to UPPER, lower, Title, Sentence, camelCase, snake_case and more. Remove Duplicate Lines Delete repeated lines and see how often each one occurred. List to Comma Separated Converter Turn lines into comma, tab or quoted lists, arrays and SQL IN (…) lists. Extract Emails, Phones & URLs from Text Pull emails, phone numbers, UUIDs, IPs, dates and more out of raw text. Text Diff Checker Compare two texts side by side and see every added and removed word or line. Table to Markdown Converter Paste cells from Excel or CSV and get a clean Markdown or HTML table. Character Counter Count characters, words and bytes against X, LinkedIn, Google Ads and SMS limits. Speech Time Calculator Estimate how long your speech or script takes at slow, natural and energetic pace. Fake Data Generator Generate realistic names, emails and addresses for test data instead of Lorem Ipsum. Smart Quotes to Straight Quotes Replace curly quotes, dashes, ellipses and ligatures with plain ASCII. Remove Line Breaks Join text copied from PDFs into flowing paragraphs and keep real paragraph breaks. Add Prefix or Suffix to Lines Add text, numbers or tags before or after every line in one step. Sort Lines Alphabetically Sort lists A–Z, Z–A, by length or naturally (v2 before v10). Email Extractor Pull every email address out of any text, remove duplicates and group by domain. URL Extractor & Tracking Parameter Remover List the links in a text and strip utm_*, fbclid and other tracking tags. URL Slug Generator Turn titles into clean, accent-free, SEO-friendly URL slugs. ROT13 Encoder & Decoder Encode or decode ROT13 and other Caesar ciphers. Emoji Remover Strip emoji and pictograms from text, or reduce everything to plain ASCII. NATO Phonetic Alphabet Translator Spell names, codes and serial numbers in Alfa, Bravo, Charlie… Random Line Shuffler Shuffle a list into random order with a fair Fisher-Yates shuffle. Filter Lines (Text Grep) Keep or drop the lines that contain, start or end with a search term. Merge Two Lists into Columns Paste two lists and join them side by side, ready for Excel. Tabs to Spaces Converter Normalise code indentation to 2, 4 or 8 spaces, or back to tabs. Word Frequency Counter Rank the most used words and phrases, ignoring common stop words. Text Splitter Cut long text into chunks of a set length without breaking words. Buzzword Detector Highlight corporate jargon and see plain-language alternatives. Indented Text to JSON, HTML & XML Convert an indented outline into nested JSON, HTML lists, XML, Markdown or YAML. Text to Hex, Binary & Base32 Converter Convert text between hex, binary, decimal, octal, Base32 and Base64. Morse Code Translator Translate text to Morse code and back, and play it as beeps. Readability Checker (Flesch Reading Ease) Measure how easy a text is to read, with scores and long sentences flagged. Text Summarizer (Shorten Text Automatically) Pick the most important sentences of a text, without any AI service. Text Similarity Checker (Compare Two Texts) Find out how much two texts overlap and which passages they share.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.

Privacy Imprint Runs in your browser. No upload, no account, no tracking.
