World Clock

A custom grid of live clocks for your offices and contacts around the world.

Loading tool…

  • No upload
  • Free
  • No account
  • Works offline once loaded

How World Clock works

Keep the current time of your offices, customers and family in one view. Add any city or IANA time zone, remove the ones you do not need, and switch between 12- and 24-hour display. Your list is remembered in your browser.

Every card shows the live local time, the date and the offset from UTC, calculated with the browser's time zone database, so daylight saving changes are applied automatically, including half-hour and 45-minute zones such as India and Nepal. The list is saved in localStorage on your device only, and nothing is sent to a server.

How to use it

  1. Pick cities or time zones to add.

  2. Remove the ones you do not need.

  3. Leave the page open for a live view; your list is saved.

Frequently asked questions

Is the time accurate?

It uses your device clock. If your computer's clock is wrong, all displayed times will be off by the same amount.

Where is my list stored?

Only in your browser (localStorage). Clearing site data removes it.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
