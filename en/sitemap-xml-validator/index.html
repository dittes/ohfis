How Sitemap XML Validator & Formatter works

Paste your sitemap and learn whether search engines will accept it: well-formed XML, correct namespace, absolute URLs, valid dates, priority and changefreq values, no duplicates and within the size limits. Each problem names the entry. The formatted XML can be copied or downloaded.

The file is parsed with the browser's XML parser (a malformed file is reported with the parser's message), the root must be urlset or sitemapindex in the sitemaps.org namespace, every url needs an absolute http(s) <loc> of at most 2,048 characters, lastmod must be in W3C datetime format, priority between 0 and 1 and changefreq one of the seven allowed values. A sitemap may hold at most 50,000 URLs and 50 MB uncompressed; larger sites need several files and an index. URLs from other hosts are flagged, because a sitemap may only list pages of its own site.

How to use it

  1. Paste your sitemap.xml.

  2. Read the list of problems and the entry table.

  3. Fix them, and copy the formatted XML.

Frequently asked questions

Do priority and changefreq matter?

Google ignores both. Accurate lastmod dates are what helps.

How many URLs can a sitemap have?

50,000 per file and 50 MB uncompressed. Use a sitemap index for more.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
