How Image Difference Checker works

Spot what changed between two design mockups, screenshots or photos. Load an original and a changed version and every differing pixel is painted in magenta (or a colour you choose); unchanged areas are faded so the differences stand out. You get the share of changed pixels and can download the diff image.

Both images are drawn on a canvas of the larger size, and the raw pixel data is compared position by position: the sum of the absolute differences of the red, green, blue and alpha channels is checked against the sensitivity threshold. A threshold of 0 flags every single change, while a higher value ignores tiny differences caused by compression noise or anti-aliasing. Images of different sizes are aligned at the top-left corner and flagged.

How to use it

  1. Load image A and image B.

  2. Adjust the sensitivity and highlight colour.

  3. Read the changed percentage and download the diff.

Frequently asked questions

Why do identical-looking images show differences?

Compression (especially JPG) changes pixel values slightly. Raise the threshold to ignore them.

Do the images need the same size?

It works best with the same size. Different sizes are compared from the top-left corner.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
