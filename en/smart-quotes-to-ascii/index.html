How Smart Quotes to Straight Quotes works

Word processors and AI chat tools replace straight quotes with curly ones and turn dashes and three dots into single special characters. Those look nice in a document but break code, JSON, shell commands, CSV files and old systems. This tool turns them back into the plain keyboard characters.

It replaces single and double typographic quotes (including German and French styles) with ' and ", en and em dashes with - and --, the ellipsis with three dots, ligatures such as ﬁ and æ with their letters, and special spaces and zero-width characters with normal spaces or nothing. Each group can be switched off separately.

How to use it

  1. Paste the text with curly quotes or special dashes.

  2. Choose which groups of characters to normalise.

  3. Copy the plain text into your code, config or spreadsheet.

Frequently asked questions

Why do curly quotes break my code?

Programming languages and shells only accept the straight quote characters. A curly quote is a different character and causes syntax errors.

Will it change my letters with accents?

No. Only punctuation, ligatures and special spaces are replaced. Letters like é or ü stay as they are.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
