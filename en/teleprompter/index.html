How Online Teleprompter works

Paste your script, set the speed and text size and start: the text scrolls smoothly in full screen while you look into the camera. A red reading line marks where to read, Space pauses, the arrow keys change the speed on the fly, and mirror mode flips the text for teleprompters that read through glass.

The script is split into paragraphs and moved upward with a CSS transform inside a requestAnimationFrame loop, using elapsed time so the speed is the same on 60 Hz and 120 Hz screens. Speed is in pixels per second; at the default setting and size a 150-word script takes about two minutes. Browsers may refuse full screen without a user action, so the prompter starts from the button. Your script stays on your device.

How to use it

  1. Paste your script and set the speed and size.

  2. Press "Start teleprompter" (full screen).

  3. Pause with Space, change speed with ↑ and ↓, close with Esc.

Frequently asked questions

How fast should a teleprompter scroll?

About 130–160 words per minute for natural speech. Rehearse once and adjust the speed.

Where should the camera be?

As close to the reading line as possible, so your eyes stay near the lens.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
