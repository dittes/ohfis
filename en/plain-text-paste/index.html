How Paste as Plain Text (Remove Formatting) works

Copy text from a web page or Word, paste it into the box and copy it out again as plain text with no fonts, colours, links or hidden styles, ready to paste into a form, CMS or document without breaking its layout. Optional cleanups trim the ends, collapse blank lines and replace curly quotes and special spaces.

When you paste, the tool reads only the text/plain version of the clipboard and inserts that, discarding the HTML version that carries the formatting. Everything stays in your browser. Most editors offer Ctrl+Shift+V (⌘⇧V) for the same effect; this page is a convenient fallback and also cleans the text.

How to use it

  1. Paste your formatted text into the box.

  2. Tick the cleanups you want.

  3. Copy the plain text.

Frequently asked questions

What is the keyboard shortcut for paste without formatting?

Ctrl+Shift+V on Windows and Linux, ⌘⇧V on Mac, in most browsers and editors.

Do images come through?

No. Only text is kept.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
