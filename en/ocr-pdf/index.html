Text recognition that stays on your device

A scan is just a picture of text: you cannot search it or copy from it. OCR (optical character recognition) reads the page and places invisible text exactly over the words. The pages look the same, but now behave like a normal document.

Text recognition usually means sending your document to a cloud service. Here it runs entirely on your device, which makes it suitable for contracts, medical letters and other sensitive scans. It takes longer than a cloud service, especially on long documents.

How to OCR a PDF

  1. Choose your scanned PDF.

  2. Tick the languages used in the document and choose the quality.

  3. Click “Make searchable”, wait for it to finish, and download your PDF.

Why people use it

Free, with no limits to work around

Works anywhere you have a browser

Frequently asked questions

Which languages are supported?

English, German, Polish and Czech. You can select several if a document mixes languages.

How accurate is it?

Clean, straight scans at normal resolution work very well. Crooked pages, handwriting and poor photos give weaker results.

Does it change how my PDF looks?

No. The page images stay untouched, and the text layer is invisible.

How long does it take?

Usually several seconds per page, depending on your device and the quality setting.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
