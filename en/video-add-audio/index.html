Add Audio or Music to a Video

Replace the sound of a video with music or a voice-over, or mix both.

Loading tool…

  • No upload

  • No account

How Add Audio or Music to a Video works

Choose a video and an audio file (MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG, FLAC) and either replace the original sound or mix both, with separate volume controls. Good for putting music under a silent clip or a voice-over onto a screen recording.

The picture is copied without re-encoding; the audio is converted to AAC. By default the result ends with the shorter of the two, so a long song does not make a short video longer; untick that option to let the audio run on over a frozen last frame. The video is processed by FFmpeg, the standard open-source video tool, compiled to WebAssembly and running inside your browser. The first use downloads the engine (about 31 MB), which your browser then keeps. Nothing is uploaded. Everything happens in your browser’s memory, so very large videos (several gigabytes) or long 4K clips can fail on a phone or an older computer; try a shorter or smaller clip in that case.

How to use it

  1. Drop the video and choose the audio file.

  2. Choose replace or mix and set the volumes.

  3. Press "Add audio" and download.

Frequently asked questions

The music is longer than the video.

Keep "end with the shorter" ticked: the result stops when the video ends. Use the Audio Cutter first if you want a specific part of the song.

My video has no sound. Does mixing work?

Yes, the audio is simply added as the only sound.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
