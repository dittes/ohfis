VAT Calculator (Net, Gross & VAT Amount)

Extract VAT from a gross price or add it to a net price.

How VAT Calculator (Net, Gross & VAT Amount) works

Split a receipt into net amount and VAT, or add VAT to a price. Pick your country to see its current standard and reduced rates, or type any rate, which works equally for GST, sales tax and MwSt.

From a gross amount: net = gross ÷ (1 + rate) and VAT = gross − net (not gross × rate, a frequent mistake that overstates the tax). From a net amount: VAT = net × rate and gross = net + VAT. The rate list is for orientation and changes over time, so confirm the rate that applies to your product with the tax authority.

How to use it

  1. Enter the amount and choose whether it includes VAT.

  2. Pick a country or enter the rate.

  3. Copy the net, VAT and gross figures.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate VAT from a gross price?

Divide the gross price by 1 + rate (1.19 for 19%) to get the net price. The difference is the VAT.

Is 19% of 119 the VAT?

No. 19% of 119 is 22.61, but the VAT inside 119 is 19.00, because 119 includes the tax.
