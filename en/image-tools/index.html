Free online image tools

Resize, convert, blur, crop and clean images without uploading them. Every tool runs in your browser. Nothing you paste or open is uploaded.

Image Converter (JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF) Convert images between JPG, PNG, WebP and AVIF in your browser, in batches. Bulk Image Resizer Resize many images to a width, height or percentage and download them as a ZIP. Image Compressor (Reduce Photo File Size) Shrink JPG, PNG and WebP files with adjustable quality and see the savings. Remove EXIF & GPS Data from Photos Strip location, camera and date metadata from JPG, PNG and WebP without re-encoding. Blur & Redact Parts of an Image Pixelate, blur or black out sensitive areas of a screenshot or photo. Crop, Rotate & Flip Image Crop to a shape or ratio, rotate by any angle and mirror images. Add Watermark to Images Stamp text across many photos with opacity, position and tiling. Favicon & App Icon Generator Create favicon.ico, PNG icons, Apple touch icon and manifest from one image. WCAG Contrast Checker Check text and background colours against WCAG AA and AAA and get a passing suggestion. Color Palette from Image Pull the dominant colours out of any picture and copy them as HEX or CSS variables. Add Padding to Fit an Aspect Ratio Add colour, transparent or blurred borders to make images 1:1, 16:9, 4:5 and more. Image to Base64 & Base64 to Image Turn an image into a data URI, HTML or CSS snippet, or render Base64 as a picture. Circular Avatar & Profile Picture Cropper Crop a headshot into a round profile picture with an optional colour ring. Screenshot Beautifier (Frame, Shadow, Gradient) Wrap screenshots in a macOS, Windows or browser frame on a gradient background. Image Difference Checker Compare two images and highlight every changed pixel in a colour of your choice. Black & White, Duotone & Invert Image Effects Turn photos black and white, duotone, sepia, inverted or dark-mode, in batch. Background Remover for Plain Backgrounds (Transparent PNG) Make a plain white, black or green background transparent in your browser. SVG Optimizer & Minifier Shrink SVG files by removing editor junk and rounding numbers, with preview. Image to Text (OCR) – Extract Text from Photos & Screenshots Copy the text out of a screenshot, scan or photo of a document, locally. Photo Collage & Split Screen Maker Combine 2–4 images side by side, stacked, in a grid or as a before/after split. Meme Generator (Top & Bottom Text) Put classic Impact-style captions on any picture and download it. PNG Color Reducer (Smaller PNG, 256 Colours or Less) Reduce an image to 2–256 colours and save a small indexed PNG-8. EXIF Viewer (Photo Metadata and GPS) See the camera, date and GPS location hidden in a photo. HEIC to JPG Converter (iPhone Photos) Turn HEIC photos from an iPhone into JPG, PNG or WebP on your device. Images to GIF (Make an Animated GIF from Pictures) Put several pictures in order and make an animated GIF slideshow.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.

Privacy Imprint Runs in your browser. No upload, no account, no tracking.
