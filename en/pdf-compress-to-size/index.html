How Compress PDF to a Target Size (2 MB, 500 KB …) works

Forms and portals often accept only PDFs up to 2 MB, 5 MB or 500 KB. Enter the size you need and the tool compresses your PDF step by step, from gentle (only the file structure) to strong (images reduced to 48 dpi), and stops at the first result that fits. Text stays selectable and sharp.

The work is done in your browser with the same engine as the Compress PDF tool: images are re-encoded at lower resolution and quality, and the file structure is packed more efficiently. If your PDF is mostly text, fonts or vector graphics, little can be saved; if even the strongest setting does not reach the target, you get the smallest possible result and an explanation instead of an empty promise. The result is always a valid PDF; very strong settings make photos visibly soft.

How to use it

  1. Drop the PDF.

  2. Enter the target size, for example 2 MB.

  3. Press "Compress to this size" and download the result.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the target not reached?

Some PDFs consist mainly of text, embedded fonts or vector drawings, which this method does not reduce. Split the PDF or remove pages and try again.

Is the text still searchable?

Yes. Only images are made smaller; the text layer stays as it is.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
