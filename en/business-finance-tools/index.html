Business and finance calculators

Margins, runway, NPV, VAT, pricing and other business maths. Every tool runs in your browser. Nothing you paste or open is uploaded.

Margin vs Markup Calculator Convert between gross margin and markup and find the price from cost. Compound Interest Calculator with Chart Growth chart for a starting amount, monthly savings, rate and time. SaaS Runway & Burn Rate Calculator How many months of cash do you have, with and without revenue growth? CAC & LTV Calculator Customer lifetime value, LTV:CAC ratio and payback period for subscription businesses. Break-Even Analysis Calculator How many units must you sell to cover fixed costs? With chart and margin of safety. VAT Calculator (Net, Gross & VAT Amount) Extract VAT from a gross price or add it to a net price. NPV & IRR Calculator Net present value, internal rate of return and discounted payback of cash flows. Rule of 72 Calculator Estimate how many years it takes for money to double at a given rate. Tiered & Volume Pricing Calculator Model graduated and volume discounts and see the price for any quantity. Sales Commission Calculator Model tiered commission payouts against a quota. Payment Processing Fee Comparator Compare Stripe, PayPal and other fees per payment, per month and per year. Effective Hourly Rate Calculator Your real hourly pay after commuting, unpaid overtime and work costs. Salary to Contractor Rate Calculator What hourly or day rate matches an employee salary once benefits, downtime and costs are included? ROI Calculator Return on investment and annualised ROI for campaigns, tools and projects. Prorated Rent Calculator Charge for a partial first month by actual days, a 30-day month or yearly rent. Group Bill Splitter by Item Split a lunch bill by who ordered what, including tax and tip. Depreciation Schedule Calculator Straight-line, declining-balance and sum-of-years depreciation schedules. Debt Payoff Planner (Snowball vs Avalanche) Compare snowball and avalanche payoff for several loans or cards. Customer Churn & Retention Calculator Project customers and revenue from monthly churn and new sign-ups. Mileage Reimbursement Calculator Calculate trip reimbursement with official per-mile or per-kilometre rates. Tip Calculator & Gratuity Table Compare tip rates for a business dinner and split the total. Working Capital & Current Ratio Calculator Compute working capital, current ratio, quick ratio and cash ratio. Price Elasticity of Demand Calculator Estimate how a price change affects quantity and revenue. Overhead Cost Allocation Calculator Allocate shared costs to departments by headcount, floor area or revenue. Startup Dilution & Cap Table Calculator See founders' ownership after seed, Series A and later rounds. Currency Conversion Fee Calculator See the real cost of an exchange rate margin and transfer fees. Commercial Lease Cost Calculator Work out the all-in monthly and total cost of an office or shop lease. Inflation Calculator (Future & Past Value) What an amount will cost in the future or is worth today, at an inflation rate you choose. Cash Drawer Counter & Float Balancer Count notes and coins and compare with the expected amount: over, short or balanced. Business Model Canvas (Interactive, with Sticky Notes) Fill in the nine blocks of the Business Model Canvas with sticky notes and export it as PNG or Markdown. Lean Canvas (Interactive, with Sticky Notes) Plan a startup on one page: problem, solution, metrics, unfair advantage and more, with sticky notes. Eisenhower Matrix with Sticky Notes (Interactive) Sort tasks as sticky notes into urgent/important quadrants by dragging, then export or print. PESTEL Analysis Board (Sticky Notes) Map political, economic, social, technological, environmental and legal factors with sticky notes. Porter's Five Forces Board (Sticky Notes) Assess an industry with the five competitive forces and collect evidence on sticky notes. Empathy Map Canvas (Sticky Notes) Understand a customer or user: what they see, hear, say, do, think and feel, with their pains and gains.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.

Privacy Imprint Runs in your browser. No upload, no account, no tracking.
