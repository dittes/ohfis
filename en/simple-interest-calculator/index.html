How Simple Interest Calculator works

Simple interest is calculated on the original amount only: interest = principal × rate × time. Enter the principal, the yearly rate and the time as years, months or days (days use a 365-day year).

For savings that earn interest on interest, use the compound interest calculator instead.

How to use it

  1. Enter principal and yearly rate.

  2. Enter the time and its unit.

  3. Read interest and total.

Frequently asked questions

Simple or compound?

Short loans and some bonds use simple interest; bank savings usually compound.

How are days counted?

Days ÷ 365 years.

Are my files uploaded?
