How BMR Calculator (Basal Metabolic Rate) works

Your basal metabolic rate is the energy you use at complete rest. Enter sex, age, weight and height. The Mifflin–St Jeor equation is: 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age + 5 (men) or − 161 (women).

It is an average for the population and can be off by 10% or more for an individual. Use the calorie calculator to turn BMR into a daily requirement.

How to use it

  1. Choose sex and units.

  2. Enter age, weight and height.

  3. Read the BMR in kcal and kJ.

Frequently asked questions

BMR or calories per day?

BMR excludes movement. Daily needs are BMR × an activity factor; see the calorie calculator.

Is the result medical advice?

No, it is an estimate for orientation.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
