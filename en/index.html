Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Every tool runs in your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. Your files are read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device.

What does it cost?

Nothing. There is no account, subscription or limit on how often you use the tools.

Which browsers and devices work?

Current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS. Very large files run best on a computer.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Once a tool has loaded, it keeps working without an internet connection. You can also install OHFIS as an app from your browser.
