How Password Generator works

Pick the length and character types and get a fresh password with a strength estimate. Options include avoiding look-alike characters (I, l, 1, O, 0) and generating several passwords at once. The password never leaves your device and is not stored.

Randomness comes from crypto.getRandomValues, the browser's cryptographically secure source, and characters are picked without bias (rejection sampling avoids the slight skew of a simple modulo). At least one character of every selected type is guaranteed, and the result is shuffled with a Fisher-Yates shuffle. Strength is entropy = length × log₂(pool size); the brute-force estimate assumes an offline attack at 10 billion guesses per second on a fast hash, which is a rough, pessimistic guide. A long random password stored in a password manager is the best practice.

How to use it

  1. Set the length and the character types.

  2. Click "Generate new" until you like the result.

  3. Copy it into your password manager.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a password be?

At least 16 random characters for important accounts. Length matters more than complexity rules.

Is it safe to generate a password in a web page?

Yes, when it runs locally like this: it is created in your browser and nothing is sent. The page makes no network request with it.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
