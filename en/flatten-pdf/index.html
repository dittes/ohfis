What does flattening do?

Filled-in forms can look different from one PDF viewer to another, and anyone can still edit the answers. Flattening turns those fields into regular page content, so what you see is what everybody gets.

That is useful before you send a completed form, submit a document to an authority, or print a file that gives you trouble. Comments and link areas can be removed at the same time.

How to flatten a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF file.

  2. Decide whether comments and link areas should be removed too.

  3. Click “Flatten PDF” and download the result.

Why people use it

Your file never leaves your device

Other online PDF tools upload your document to their servers. This one does the work inside your browser, so there is nothing to intercept, store or delete later.

Free, with no limits to work around

No account, no email address, no daily quota and no watermark on the result. The only limit is what your device can handle.

Works anywhere you have a browser

Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iPhone. Nothing to install, and once a page has loaded it keeps working without an internet connection.

Frequently asked questions

Can I undo flattening?

No, not in the new file, so keep your original if you may need to make changes later.

Is flattening the same as protecting?

No. A flattened PDF has no form fields left, but its pages can still be edited with PDF software. To restrict access, use Protect PDF.

Are my form answers kept?

Yes. They become part of the page and look just as they did.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
