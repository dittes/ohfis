A clear mark on every page

Type your text, then adjust size, color, opacity and angle while the preview updates. Place it once on each page, or repeat it across the page to make it harder to remove.

Unusual characters are not a problem, as the right font is embedded automatically. Bear in mind a watermark deters misuse but does not stop a determined person, so combine it with Protect PDF for sensitive files.

How to add a watermark to a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF file.

  2. Enter the text and adjust its look until the preview is right.

  3. Click “Add watermark” and download your PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Is the watermark on all pages?

Yes, on every page of the document.

Can the text underneath still be read?

Yes. Keep the opacity low, around 20 to 30%, so the page stays readable.

Can a watermark be removed?

With the right tools, yes, which is why it is a deterrent and not protection. Tiling it across the page makes removal harder.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
