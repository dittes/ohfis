How Trim Spaces in CSV & Spreadsheet Cells works

A lookup that "should work" fails because "Berlin" and "Berlin " are different values. Paste your table and every cell is trimmed. Non-breaking spaces, which Excel's TRIM function does not remove, and invisible zero-width characters are removed too, and repeated spaces inside cells can be collapsed.

Each cell is processed by replacing exotic spaces (U+00A0, U+2007, U+202F, U+3000) with a normal space, deleting zero-width characters (U+200B–U+200D, U+FEFF, U+2060, soft hyphens), trimming both ends and optionally collapsing runs of whitespace. The counter reports how many cells actually changed, so you know the problem was real.

How to use it

  1. Paste the CSV.

  2. Choose whether to collapse inner spaces and remove hidden characters.

  3. Copy or download the cleaned table.

Frequently asked questions

Why does TRIM in Excel not work on my data?

Data from the web often contains non-breaking spaces (code 160), which TRIM ignores. This tool replaces them first.

Will it change numbers?

Only whitespace is touched. A trimmed cell like " 5 " becomes "5".

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
