How Lean Canvas (Interactive, with Sticky Notes) works

The Lean Canvas is a one-page plan for new products and startups. Instead of partners and resources it starts from the problem: problem, customer segments, unique value proposition, solution, channels, revenue streams, cost structure, key metrics and unfair advantage. Fill the boxes with sticky notes, move them around, and keep the canvas short enough to test the riskiest assumptions first.

The board is saved in your browser and nothing is uploaded. Export it as PNG, copy it as Markdown or save it as a file. The Lean Canvas was created by Ash Maurya as an adaptation of the Business Model Canvas (CC BY-SA); this board is a re-drawn version and carries the credit.

How to use it

  1. Write the top problems and the customer segments first.

  2. Add the unique value proposition, solution, channels and the numbers that matter.

  3. Export the canvas and test the riskiest assumption with real customers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference to the Business Model Canvas?

The Lean Canvas focuses on problems, solutions, metrics and unfair advantage, which suits early startups. The Business Model Canvas fits an existing business better.

How much should I write?

One short sticky note per idea. If a box needs a paragraph, split it or move the detail into a document.

Is the board saved?

Yes, in this browser only. Save it as a file for a backup.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
