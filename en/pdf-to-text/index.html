Clean text instead of broken lines

Copying from a PDF usually leaves a line break at the end of every line. This tool puts paragraphs back together, joins hyphenated words and gives you plain text in UTF-8, including umlauts, Polish and Czech characters.

That is handy for quoting, translating, or feeding a document into another program. A scanned PDF has no text to extract, so run it through OCR PDF first.

How to extract text from a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF file.

  2. Click “Extract text”.

  3. Download the .txt file.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the result empty?

The PDF is probably a scan, which is really just images. Use OCR PDF to recognize the text first.

Is the formatting kept?

No. A .txt file holds plain text only. To keep headings and styling, use PDF to Word.

What about columns?

Text is read in position order, so multi-column pages may need some tidying afterwards.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
