How Moon Phase Calculator works

Enter a date and see the moon's phase name, how much of the disc is lit, the moon's age in days and the dates of the next new and full moons. It works for any year, so you can look up the moon on a birthday or plan a night shoot.

New and full moons are calculated with Jean Meeus's algorithm from "Astronomical Algorithms" (the mean phase plus the largest periodic corrections), which is accurate to about a minute or two; times are in UTC and ignore the small difference between terrestrial and universal time. The current phase and illumination come from the angle between sun and moon, using a short series for the moon's longitude, with an error well below one percent of illumination. The moon shows the same phase everywhere on Earth, but in the southern hemisphere it appears upside down.

How to use it

Frequently asked questions

How long is a lunar cycle?

About 29.53 days from new moon to new moon (the synodic month).

Why do the times differ from other sites?

Sites differ in time zone and rounding. These are in UTC; convert to your time zone.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
