Unpivot Table (Crosstab to List)

Turn a wide crosstab with one column per month into a tidy long list.

How Unpivot Table (Crosstab to List) works

Reports often have one column per month or year. Databases, charts and pivot tables want one row per value instead. Choose the columns that identify a row (like product or region) and every other column is turned into rows of attribute and value: Jan, Feb, Mar become a "month" column and the numbers a "amount" column.

This is the "melt" or "unpivot" operation (pandas melt, Power Query Unpivot Columns). For each input row and each non-identifier column the tool emits [identifiers…, column name, cell value]. Empty cells can be skipped to avoid rows without data. Names of the two new columns are yours to choose. A table with 5 identifiers-rows and 12 month columns becomes up to 60 tidy rows.

How to use it

  1. Paste the wide table.

  2. Tick the identifier columns and name the two new columns.

  3. Copy or download the long table.

Frequently asked questions

What is the opposite operation?

The pivot table generator turns a long list back into a crosstab.

Is this the same as transposing?

No. Transposing swaps rows and columns. Unpivoting keeps the identifiers and stacks the other columns into one.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
