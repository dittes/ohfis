How Brain Dump Organizer (Sort Thoughts into Tasks) works

Write down everything that is on your mind in any order: to-dos, worries, questions, ideas, half sentences. The organiser splits it into items and sorts them into urgent items, tasks, items with a date, questions, ideas, worries and notes, lists the people you mention and gives you a Markdown checklist to copy into your notes or task tool.

This is a transparent rule-based sorter, not an AI model: it recognises action verbs and phrases ("call", "send", "need to", "don't forget"), question marks, idea words ("what if", "maybe", "idea"), worry words ("worried", "risk", "blocked"), dates and weekdays ("tomorrow", "15 March", "Friday") and urgency words ("urgent", "asap"), and names after words like "ask", "call" or "with" and @mentions. Because it does not understand meaning it will misfile some lines, and it only knows English trigger words. In return it is instant, predictable and fully private, and the text never leaves your browser.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste your thoughts, one per line or as sentences.

  2. Check the sorted groups.

  3. Copy the Markdown checklist.

Frequently asked questions

Is this artificial intelligence?

No. It uses simple rules, so it is fast and private but not perfect. Review the result.

Does it work in other languages?

The trigger words are English; other languages will mostly end up under notes.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
