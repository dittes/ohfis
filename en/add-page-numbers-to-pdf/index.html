Number your pages in the position and style you want, and check the result before saving.

Numbering that fits your document

Choose from six positions and formats such as “1”, “1 / 10” or “Page 1 of 10”. The preview shows you exactly what you will get.

Skip a cover or contents page by starting on a later page, and choose which number to begin with. That helps when your document is one section of something larger, or when you have just merged several files.

How to add page numbers to a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF file.

  2. Choose the position and format, and where numbering starts.

  3. Click “Add page numbers” and download your PDF.

Why people use it

Your file never leaves your device

Other online PDF tools upload your document to their servers. This one does the work inside your browser, so there is nothing to intercept, store or delete later.

Free, with no limits to work around

No account, no email address, no daily quota and no watermark on the result. The only limit is what your device can handle.

Works anywhere you have a browser

Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iPhone. Nothing to install, and once a page has loaded it keeps working without an internet connection.

Frequently asked questions

Can I skip the first page?

Yes. Set “Start numbering on page” to 2, and set the first number to whatever you need.

Does it work on rotated pages?

Yes. Numbers appear upright in the chosen position, however the page is turned.

Can I remove the numbers later?

Not easily, as they become part of the page. Keep your original file in case you want to change them.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
