How Weekly Work Hours Budget Planner works

Enter your weekly hours, how much you want to keep free for meetings, email and surprises, and a list of work items with hours. The planner shows the planned hours, the usable hours after the reserve, the free capacity or the overbooking, each item's share and the hours per day.

Usable hours are the available hours minus the reserve percentage, the free capacity is the usable hours minus the planned hours, and each line's share is shown both of the plan and of the whole week. A reserve of 20–30 % is typical for people with many interruptions. The planner counts hours you enter: it does not estimate how long tasks take (for that use the PERT estimator), and nothing is stored or sent anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Enter the hours you have and the reserve.

  2. List the tasks with hours.

  3. Remove or move work until the plan fits.

Frequently asked questions

How big should the reserve be?

20 % for focused work, 30–40 % for roles with many meetings and support requests.

Why does the plan feel too full?

Planning fallacy: tasks take longer than expected. Estimate with three values using the PERT tool.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
