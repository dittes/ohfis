How Currency Conversion Fee Calculator works

Enter the amount, the mid-market rate, the provider's margin and any fixed fee, and see how much arrives, the effective rate you really get, and how much money you lose compared with the mid-market rate. Switch to "receive" mode to find out how much you must send so that an exact amount arrives, and add a safety cushion for rate moves when you budget in a foreign currency.

The mid-market rate is the midpoint between buy and sell prices. A provider lowers it by a percentage margin: effective rate = mid rate × (1 − margin). If you send, the fixed fee is deducted first and the rest is converted; if you want a target amount to arrive, you must send target ÷ effective rate plus the fee. The loss is the difference to what the same amount would have been at the mid-market rate. The calculator does not look up live rates: enter the current mid-market rate from a rate source, and treat the cushion as a budgeting aid, not a forecast.

How to use it

  1. Enter the amount and the mid-market rate.

  2. Enter the provider margin and fixed fee.

  3. Read what arrives, the effective rate and the lost amount.

Frequently asked questions

What is the mid-market rate?

The fair rate between the buy and sell prices that banks use among themselves. Compare providers against it.

Why does the effective rate differ from the quoted rate?

Because the margin and the fixed fee both reduce what arrives; the effective rate includes them.
