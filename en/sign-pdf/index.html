Sign documents without printing them

Draw your signature with a mouse or finger, type your name, or use a photo of your signature. Click to place it, then drag and resize until it fits. You can put it on as many pages as you need, along with any initials.

Contracts and personal documents are exactly the kind of file you should not upload to a stranger's server. Here the document and your signature both stay on your device, and your signature is not saved anywhere.

How to sign a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF file.

  2. Create your signature by drawing, typing or uploading it.

  3. Click on the page to place it, adjust it, then click “Finish and save”.

Frequently asked questions

Is this signature legally valid?

A visible signature like this counts as a simple electronic signature, which is enough for many everyday documents. Contracts that legally require a qualified electronic signature need a certified provider.

Is my signature stored?

No. It exists only on this page and is gone when you close it.

Can others still change the document after I sign?

A PDF signed this way can still be edited. Use Flatten PDF or Protect PDF to make changes harder.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
