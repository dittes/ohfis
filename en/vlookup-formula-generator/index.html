VLOOKUP & XLOOKUP Formula Generator

Build correct lookup formulas from column letters, in English, German, French or Spanish.

How VLOOKUP & XLOOKUP Formula Generator works

Tell the generator which cell holds the value, which column to search and which column to return, and it writes three working formulas: XLOOKUP, VLOOKUP and INDEX/MATCH. Ranges are made absolute ($D$2:$D$100) so they do not drift when you fill down, and an optional IFERROR shows your own text instead of #N/A.

VLOOKUP needs a column number counted from the first column of the table, which is the most common source of errors; the tool computes it from the column letters (D to F is column 3). It also detects when the return column is left of the search column, a case VLOOKUP cannot handle, and tells you to use XLOOKUP or INDEX/MATCH instead. You can output the formulas in the function names and separators of a German, French or Spanish Excel.

How to use it

  1. Enter the cell to look up, the column to search and the column to return.

  2. Set the first and last data row, and optionally the sheet name and the text for "not found".

  3. Copy the formula you prefer into your sheet and fill it down.

Frequently asked questions

Which should I use: VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP or INDEX/MATCH?

Use XLOOKUP if you have Excel 2021/365. INDEX/MATCH works in every version and can look left. VLOOKUP is the oldest and only looks to the right.

Why do I get #N/A?

The value is missing from the search column, has extra spaces, or is text in one place and a number in the other. Wrapping in IFERROR hides the error; the Trim tool can clean the data.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
