How Compare Two PDFs (Text and Visual Differences) works

Drop the original and the changed PDF and see which pages differ, how many words were added or removed and exactly which words, with deleted words struck through and added words underlined. A second view compares the rendered pages pixel by pixel and shows changes in images, colours and layout in magenta.

The text view compares the words of each page, so a changed number or clause is found even if the layout moved. The visual view finds what text cannot: a replaced logo, a changed chart, a moved element. Pages are matched by number, so inserting a page early in one document makes all later pages look different. Scanned PDFs have no text; run them through OCR PDF first. Both files stay on your device.

How to use it

  1. Choose PDF A (the original) and PDF B (the changed version).

  2. Read the list of pages and click one to see the differences.

  3. Switch to the visual view for layout and image changes.

Frequently asked questions

Why do all pages after an inserted page show changes?

Pages are compared by number. If a page was inserted, the following pages are shifted. Delete the inserted page in a copy and compare again.

Does it find changes in images?

Yes, in the visual view. The text view only compares text.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
