How Effective Hourly Rate Calculator works

A salary of 60,000 for 40 hours sounds like 28.85 an hour, but the hours that the job really takes are more. Enter commuting time, unpaid extra hours, weeks off and work-related costs to see what you earn for every hour you actually spend.

Nominal rate = salary ÷ (contract hours × weeks worked). Effective rate = (salary − work-related costs) ÷ (contract hours + unpaid extra hours + commuting hours) per year, with commuting counted as two trips per working day at the workplace. Weeks worked are 52 minus weeks off. The result is a way to compare job offers and remote versus on-site work, not a tax calculation.

How to use it

  1. Enter pay, contract hours and weeks off.

  2. Add commute, unpaid hours and yearly work costs.

  3. Compare the effective and nominal hourly rate.

Frequently asked questions

Should commute time count as work time?

Not legally, but it is time the job takes from your life, so it matters when comparing offers.

Is this before or after tax?

Use gross or net pay consistently; the calculator does not apply taxes.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
