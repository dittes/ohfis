Free online calculators

Percentages, fractions, averages, age, BMI, calories, pace and a scientific calculator. Every tool runs in your browser. Nothing you paste or open is uploaded.

Percentage Calculator Percent of a number, percentage change, what percent is X of Y, add or subtract a percentage. Percentage Allocation Calculator Split an amount by percentages or weights, rounded so the parts add up to the cent. Fraction Calculator Add, subtract, multiply and divide fractions and mixed numbers, always reduced. Ratio Calculator Solve A : B = C : X, and simplify a ratio such as 1920 : 1080 to 16 : 9. Average Calculator (Mean, Median, Mode) Mean, median, mode, range, sum and count of a list of numbers. Standard Deviation Calculator Sample and population standard deviation and variance of a list of numbers. Rounding Calculator Round to decimals, tens or hundreds, to the nearest 5 or 0.05, with up, down, nearest or banker's rounding. Scientific Calculator Type an expression with brackets, powers, roots, logarithms, trigonometry and factorials. Random Number Generator Draw random integers in a range, with or without repeats, using your browser's secure random source. Age Calculator Exact age in years, months and days, days lived and the countdown to the next birthday. Pregnancy Due Date Calculator Estimate the due date and current week from the first day of the last period. BMI Calculator (Body Mass Index) Body mass index, BMI prime and the healthy weight range for your height. BMR Calculator (Basal Metabolic Rate) Calories your body burns at rest, with the Mifflin–St Jeor equation. Calorie Calculator (Daily Calorie Needs) Daily calories to maintain, lose or gain weight from your activity level. Body Fat Calculator (US Navy Method) Estimate body fat percentage from neck, waist and hip measurements. Water Intake Calculator Daily water needs from body weight, exercise and hot weather. GPA Calculator Weighted grade point average from courses, credits and letter grades. Running Pace Calculator Pace, speed or finish time from distance and time, with race distance presets.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.

Privacy Imprint Runs in your browser. No upload, no account, no tracking.
