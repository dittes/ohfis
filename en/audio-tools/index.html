Free online audio tools

Tone generators, teleprompter, recorders, audio editing and display tests. Every tool runs in your browser. Nothing you paste or open is uploaded.

Online Tone Generator (Sine, Square, Sweep) Play pure tones and frequency sweeps to test speakers and find hums or rattles. Online Teleprompter A full-screen scrolling script with adjustable speed, size and mirror mode. BPM Tap Tempo Counter Tap along to find the tempo of a song in beats per minute. Audio Normalizer (Make Audio Louder) Bring a quiet recording up to a standard peak or loudness level. Audio Silence Remover & Trimmer Cut silence from the start and end of a recording, or shorten long pauses. Audio Channel Tool (Mute, Swap, Invert, Pan, Mono) Mix to mono, swap or mute left/right, invert polarity or pan a track. Audio Pitch & Speed Changer Make a recording higher, lower, faster or slower, like a tape. Online Voice Recorder (with Trim) Record from your microphone, check the level, trim and download. Online Screen Recorder Record a screen, window or tab, with optional audio, and save it as a video file. Webcam Resolution & Frame Rate Checker See what resolution and frame rate your camera really delivers. Microphone Noise Floor Test Measure the background noise of your room and microphone in dBFS. Online Soundboard (Applause, Buzzer, Drum Roll) Twelve instant sounds for meetings, quizzes and demos, plus your own. Video Speed Controller (Slow Motion & Fast Forward) Preview a video at 0.25× to 4× speed and export the sped-up version. Screen Colour Card (White Balance & Brightness) A full-screen grey, white or coloured card for webcam white balance and screen checks. Browser Hardware Acceleration Check (GPU, WebGL, Video Decode) Find out whether your browser really uses the graphics card. Screen Refresh Rate & Frame Drop Test Measure the real refresh rate, jitter and dropped frames of your browser. Aspect Ratio Calculator (16:9, 4:3, Resize) Find the ratio of a resolution, scale to a new width, and get screen PPI. Dual Monitor Size & Scaling Matcher Compare two screens and find the scaling that makes text the same size. Video Frame Capture (Screenshot from Video) Pick the exact frame in a video and save it as a full-resolution PNG or JPEG. Audio Cutter (Trim MP3 and WAV Online) Cut a part out of a recording or keep only the part you need. Audio Joiner (Merge MP3 Files Online) Join several audio files into one, in the order you choose. Change Audio Volume (Make MP3 Louder or Quieter) Make a recording louder or quieter by a number of decibels. Reverse Audio (Play Sound Backwards) Turn a recording around so that it plays backwards. Audio Equalizer (Bass and Treble Online) Shape the sound of a recording with five frequency bands and presets. Audio Converter (to MP3 or WAV) Convert M4A, OGG, FLAC, Opus or video sound to MP3 or WAV. Webcam Video Recorder Record a video from your webcam and save it, without installing anything. Text to Speech (Read Text Aloud) Have your browser read a text aloud with a voice and speed you choose. Audio Noise Reducer (Remove Background Noise) Reduce steady hiss, hum and fan noise in a recording. BPM and Key Detector (Find Tempo and Key of a Song) Find the tempo in BPM and the musical key of an audio file.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.

Privacy Imprint Runs in your browser. No upload, no account, no tracking.
