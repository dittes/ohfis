How Secret Santa Generator works

Enter the participants, add exclusions such as couples who should not draw each other, and generate the draw. Every person gets a private link that reveals only their own match when they tap a button; the organiser can hide the list of pairs to stay surprised too.

The draw is a random derangement: nobody draws themselves, every person gives and receives exactly one gift, and the exclusions are respected. The algorithm tries random assignments until it finds a valid one and tells you if the exclusions make a draw impossible. Each result is encrypted with its own AES-GCM key and put into the link (after the #), so opening the link decrypts it in the browser. There is no server, so nothing is stored; the organiser holds all the links, so the privacy is against casual peeking by other participants, not against the organiser.

How to use it

  1. Enter the participants and any exclusions.

  2. Send each person their private link.

  3. Participants open the link and tap "Reveal my match".

Frequently asked questions

Can the organiser see who got whom?

Only if they open the links or untick "Hide the pairs". The organiser generates all the links, so they could, but the default view shows no names.

What if the draw is impossible?

With too many exclusions no assignment exists. Remove some exclusions or add more people.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
