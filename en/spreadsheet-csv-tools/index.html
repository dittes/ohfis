CSV and spreadsheet tools

Fix, convert, merge, compare and reshape CSV and spreadsheet data. Every tool runs in your browser. Nothing you paste or open is uploaded.

CSV Delimiter Converter Detect the separator of a CSV and convert it to comma, semicolon, tab or pipe. Large CSV File Viewer Open CSV files of hundreds of megabytes, scroll millions of rows and search them. Restore Leading Zeros in CSV Repair zip codes and IDs that lost their leading zeros in Excel. VLOOKUP & XLOOKUP Formula Generator Build correct lookup formulas from column letters, in English, German, French or Spanish. Excel Date Serial Number Converter Turn Excel serial numbers like 45231 into dates and dates back into serial numbers. JSON to CSV Converter Flatten JSON arrays and nested objects into a CSV table for Excel. CSV to JSON Converter Turn a CSV into JSON objects, with typed values and optional nesting. Transpose Table (Rows to Columns) Swap rows and columns of pasted spreadsheet cells. Pivot Table Generator Group data and calculate sums, averages and counts without Excel. Split Column into Multiple Columns Split full names, addresses or codes into separate columns by delimiter, position or pattern. Compare Two CSV Files Find added, removed and changed rows and cells between two CSV files. CSV to SQL INSERT Converter Turn spreadsheet rows into INSERT or UPDATE statements for MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite or SQL Server. Markdown Table Formatter Align ragged Markdown tables into neat, readable grids. Excel Custom Number Format Tester Try custom format codes like #,##0.00 or [>=1000]0.0,"K" on sample values. CSV to GeoJSON Converter Turn a list of coordinates into a GeoJSON FeatureCollection of points. Reorder & Delete CSV Columns Drag columns into a new order or remove them before you share a table. Z-Score & Min-Max Normalizer Standardise numeric columns to z-scores or scale them to 0–1. Phone Number Formatter (E.164) Normalise phone numbers in a list to E.164 or a readable international style. Fill Blank Cells in Spreadsheet Data Fill empty cells with the value above, a fixed value or the column average. Excel Formula Formatter Break long nested Excel formulas into indented, readable lines. Address Parser (Split Street, City, ZIP) Split one-line addresses into street, number, unit, city, state and ZIP. Currency to Number Converter Turn "$1,234.56", "1.234,56 €" and "(500)" into plain numbers. Percentage Change Calculator for Columns Add a percent-change column comparing two numeric columns. Trim Spaces in CSV & Spreadsheet Cells Strip leading, trailing and hidden spaces from every cell. CSV Header Cleaner (snake_case) Turn messy column titles into clean, database-safe names. Merge CSV Files Combine several CSV files into one, matching columns by name. Sample Size Calculator How many respondents do you need for your confidence level and margin of error? CSV Encoding Fixer (UTF-8, Mojibake) Repair garbled characters like MÃ¼ller and convert files to UTF-8. Excel Formula Translator (EN ⇄ DE, FR, ES) Convert formulas between English, German, French and Spanish Excel, including separators. Unpivot Table (Crosstab to List) Turn a wide crosstab with one column per month into a tidy long list.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.

Privacy Imprint Runs in your browser. No upload, no account, no tracking.
