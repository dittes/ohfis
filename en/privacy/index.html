Privacy policy

Ohfis runs in your browser. Your files are not uploaded, and we do not use analytics, tracking or cookies.

What happens to your files

Files you open are processed in your browser's memory on your own device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. We do not operate a server that processes files.

The PDF tools, the home page and the PDF overview also carry a Content Security Policy, which tells your browser to block network connections to other servers. All the code and data these tools need, including text recognition, is served from ohfis.com itself.

What is stored on your device

  • Local storage: your theme and language choice and display preferences.
  • IndexedDB: a file you pass from one PDF tool to the next. It is deleted as soon as the next tool has opened it.
  • Cache storage: the app's own files, so it keeps working offline.

You can remove all of this at any time in your browser settings, under the site data for this website. We have no access to any of it.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address in order to deliver the pages. See the GitHub privacy statement. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since we do not collect personal data ourselves, we will usually have nothing on file about you. Questions are welcome at ohfis@dittes.info.
