How much can a PDF shrink?

Most of a large PDF is usually images: photos and scans stored at a higher resolution than anyone needs. Balanced compression brings them down to 150 dpi, which still looks sharp on screen and in everyday printing, and often makes files several times smaller.

Text and vector graphics are left alone, so they stay perfectly sharp and selectable, and wasted space is removed from the file as well. A PDF that is mostly text is already small and will not shrink as much.

How to compress a PDF

  1. Choose the PDF you want to make smaller.

  2. Pick a level: Light, Balanced or Strong.

  3. Click “Compress PDF”, see how much you saved, and download the file.

Why people use it

Your file never leaves your device

Other online PDF tools upload your document to their servers. This one does the work inside your browser, so there is nothing to intercept, store or delete later.

Free, with no limits to work around

No account, no email address, no daily quota and no watermark on the result. The only limit is what your device can handle.

Works anywhere you have a browser

Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iPhone. Nothing to install, and once a page has loaded it keeps working without an internet connection.

Frequently asked questions

Which level should I choose?

Balanced suits most documents. Use Strong when you need the smallest file for on-screen reading, and Light when the images must stay untouched.

Will compression make the text blurry?

No. Text stays sharp at every level, because only images are recompressed.

Why did my PDF hardly get smaller?

It probably contains mostly text, or its images are already compressed. Such files have little left to remove.

Can I get a file under 1 MB or 100 KB?

It depends on the content. Try Strong first. If it is still too large, splitting the file or removing pages can help.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
