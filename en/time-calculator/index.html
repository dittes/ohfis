Time Calculator (Add & Subtract Hours)

Add and subtract durations like 4h 12m + 1h 55m or 2:30 - 0:45.

Loading tool…

  • No upload
  • Free
  • No account
  • Works offline once loaded

How Time Calculator (Add & Subtract Hours) works

Type calculations the way you would say them: "4h 12m + 1h 55m", "2:30 - 0:45" or "1d 2h + 90m". Each line gives the result as hours and minutes, as decimal hours and as total minutes, which is useful for timesheets, video editing, flight times and cooking schedules.

Each term is read as days, hours, minutes and seconds (d, h, m, s), as clock notation (2:30 or 1:15:30) or as decimal hours (0.25). The terms are converted to seconds, added or subtracted according to their sign and the total is formatted again. Unreadable terms produce a clear message instead of a wrong sum.

How to use it

  1. Type one calculation per line, with + and - between durations.

  2. Read the result in h:mm, decimal hours and minutes.

  3. Copy the lines you need.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add 45 minutes to 2 hours 30 minutes?

Type "2h 30m + 45m" and you get 3:15, which is 3.25 hours.

Can I use clock times like 14:30?

This tool adds durations. For the time between two dates use the Date Difference Calculator.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
