How Image Compressor (Reduce Photo File Size) works

Large photos slow down websites and fill mailboxes. Choose WebP or JPG, lower the quality slider until you can just see a difference, then go one step back. Optionally limit the longest side to remove pixels you do not need. The tool shows the before and after size for every file, and keeps the original if compression would not help.

Compression happens in your browser: the image is decoded, optionally scaled down and re-encoded with the browser's own JPEG or WebP encoder at the quality you chose. Lossy formats discard detail the eye barely notices, which is why a 75–85% setting usually cuts file size by half or more. PNG stays lossless and shrinks little, so use WebP or JPG for photos. Because nothing is uploaded, private images are safe and there is no size limit apart from your device's memory.

How to use it

  1. Drop your images.

  2. Choose WebP or JPG and a quality.

  3. Check the savings and download.

Frequently asked questions

What quality setting should I use?

Start at 75–80%. Photos usually look the same to the eye down to about 70%.

Why did my PNG hardly shrink?

PNG is lossless and designed for graphics. Convert photos to WebP or JPG for real savings.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
