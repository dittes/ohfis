Team and meeting tools

Estimates, decision tools, brackets, pickers and org charts. Every tool runs in your browser. Nothing you paste or open is uploaded.

PERT Estimator (Three-Point Estimate) Combine optimistic, likely and pessimistic estimates into expected time and confidence ranges. Random Name Picker Wheel Spin a wheel to pick a name for a meeting, a giveaway or a presentation order. Secret Santa Generator Draw gift partners with exclusions and send each person a private reveal link. Tournament Bracket Maker Create printable single-elimination brackets or round-robin schedules. Org Chart Maker from Indented Text Turn an indented list of people into an organisation chart and download it as an image. Passive-Aggressive Tone Checker for Emails Highlight guilt-tripping, over-apologising and passive-aggressive phrases with kinder alternatives. Brain Dump Organizer (Sort Thoughts into Tasks) Paste everything on your mind and get tasks, questions, ideas and worries sorted. Random Team Generator (Split a Group into Teams) Split a list of people into balanced random teams, with optional skill levels and pairs to keep apart. Planning Poker for Agile Estimation (One Device) Estimate stories with secret cards on one shared screen, then reveal the votes and see median and spread. Retrospective Board (Went Well / To Improve / Actions) Collect retro cards in columns, vote with thumbs-up and copy the sorted result as Markdown. RACI Matrix Generator and Checker Build a RACI chart for tasks and roles and check that every task has exactly one accountable person. Meeting Agenda Planner with Times Turn agenda items with durations into a timed agenda and see when the meeting ends. SWOT Analysis Maker Fill in strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats and get a clean SWOT grid to copy or print. Priority Matrix Maker (Impact/Effort and Eisenhower) Sort tasks into quick wins, big bets, fill-ins and money pits, or into the Eisenhower quadrants. Rotation Schedule Generator (On-Call, Cleaning, Chair Duty) Create a fair rotation of people over days, weeks or fortnights and skip anyone who is away. Team Canvas (Interactive, with Sticky Notes) Align a team on purpose, goals, values, roles, strengths, risks and working rules, with sticky notes.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.

Privacy Imprint Runs in your browser. No upload, no account, no tracking.
