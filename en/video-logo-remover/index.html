How Remove Logo or Watermark from a Video works

Enter the position and size of the logo (the red frame on the preview shows the area) and the area is filled in from its surroundings. It works well for small, static logos on calm backgrounds, such as a corner mark on your own footage or a timestamp.

The tool uses FFmpeg’s delogo filter, which interpolates the area from the pixels around it; it cannot reconstruct a large or busy part of the picture and leaves a slight blur. Only remove logos from material you own or have the right to edit. The video is processed by FFmpeg, the standard open-source video tool, compiled to WebAssembly and running inside your browser. The first use downloads the engine (about 31 MB), which your browser then keeps. Nothing is uploaded. Everything happens in your browser’s memory, so very large videos (several gigabytes) or long 4K clips can fail on a phone or an older computer; try a shorter or smaller clip in that case.

How to use it

  1. Drop the video.

  2. Enter left, top, width and height of the logo area and check the red frame.

  3. Press "Remove logo" and download.

Frequently asked questions

The logo is still visible.

Make the frame slightly larger than the logo, including its edges and any shadow.

Does it work on a moving logo?

Only if the area covers all positions of the logo; the larger the area, the more visible the blur.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
