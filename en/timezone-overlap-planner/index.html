How Time Zone Meeting Planner works

Add the cities of your team, pick a date and your working hours, and see a bar for each time zone with the 24 hours of the day. Dark green marks the half-hours when everyone is working, and the table lists those windows in each city's local time, ready to copy into an invitation.

For every half-hour of the chosen UTC day the tool asks the browser's time zone database for the local time in each city, including daylight saving time on that date, and checks whether it falls inside the working hours and not on a weekend. Consecutive shared half-hours are merged into windows. If no window exists, you get a clear message instead of an empty chart, so you can widen the hours or accept an early or late slot for one team.

How to use it

  1. Add the time zones of all participants.

  2. Choose the date and the working hours.

  3. Pick a time from the shared windows and copy it.

Frequently asked questions

Does it handle daylight saving time?

Yes. Offsets are looked up for the chosen date, so the US and Europe's different changeover dates are respected.

Why is there no overlap?

Teams more than about 8 hours apart often have no shared 9-to-5. Widen the working hours or rotate who takes the early or late call.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
