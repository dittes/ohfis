How Excel Date Serial Number Converter works

Dates in Excel are stored as the number of days since a starting date, so exports and CSV files often show 45231 where you expect a date. Paste a list of serial numbers to get ISO dates (decimals become times), or paste dates to get serial numbers for formulas and imports.

Windows Excel counts from 1 January 1900, with a famous quirk: it wrongly treats 1900 as a leap year, so the non-existent 29 February 1900 has serial number 60. The converter reproduces this, so serials from 1 to 59 and from 61 onward match what Excel shows, and flags 60. Excel for Mac used to count from 1 January 1904 (serial 0); choose that date system if your numbers are four years and a day off. The fractional part is the time of day (0.5 = 12:00).

How to use it

  1. Paste serial numbers or ISO dates, one per line.

  2. Pick the date system (Windows 1900 is the default).

  3. Copy the converted values.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my date off by four years?

The file uses the 1904 date system from old Mac versions of Excel. Switch the date system to 1904.

What is serial number 60?

Excel treats 1900 as a leap year by mistake, so day 60 is a 29 February 1900 that never existed.
