Delete pages from a PDF

Click the pages you do not want, and download a PDF without them.

or drop it here

  • No upload
  • Free
  • No account
  • Works offline once loaded

Removed means removed

Blank scans, outdated annexes or pages that were never meant to be shared: click them and they are gone. Forms, links and bookmarks on the other pages are preserved.

Some tools only hide a removed page, leaving its content inside the file. This tool deletes the page data from the new file, so nobody can recover it from what you share.

How to delete pages from a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF file.

  2. Click every page you want to remove. They are marked in red.

  3. Click “Delete pages” and download the new PDF.

Why people use it

Your file never leaves your device

Other online PDF tools upload your document to their servers. This one does the work inside your browser, so there is nothing to intercept, store or delete later.

Free, with no limits to work around

No account, no email address, no daily quota and no watermark on the result. The only limit is what your device can handle.

Works anywhere you have a browser

Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iPhone. Nothing to install, and once a page has loaded it keeps working without an internet connection.

Frequently asked questions

Can the deleted pages be recovered from the new file?

No. Their content is removed from the new file, not just hidden. Your original file on your device is not changed.

Can I remove several pages at once?

Yes. Select as many as you like. At least one page has to remain.

Do bookmarks and forms survive?

Yes. Those on the remaining pages are kept.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
