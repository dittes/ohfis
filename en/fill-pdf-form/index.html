Forms contain personal data. Keep them private.

Tax forms, applications and medical questionnaires are full of details you would rather not hand to an online service. This tool lists every field in the form so you can fill them out in your browser, then writes your answers into the PDF.

Text fields, checkboxes, radio buttons and dropdowns all work, including German, Polish and Czech characters. You can lock the answers when you are done. If the PDF has no fillable fields, use Edit PDF to type on it directly.

How to fill out a PDF form

  1. Choose your PDF form.

  2. Fill in the fields listed on the page.

  3. Click “Save filled PDF” and download your completed form.

Why people use it

Your file never leaves your device

Other online PDF tools upload your document to their servers. This one does the work inside your browser, so there is nothing to intercept, store or delete later.

Free, with no limits to work around

No account, no email address, no daily quota and no watermark on the result. The only limit is what your device can handle.

Works anywhere you have a browser

Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iPhone. Nothing to install, and once a page has loaded it keeps working without an internet connection.

Frequently asked questions

What if my PDF has no fields?

Some forms are just flat pages. Use Edit PDF to type text wherever you need it.

Can I save and continue later?

Yes. Download the partly filled PDF and open it here again. Your answers are still in it.

Can I lock my answers?

Yes. Tick the lock option before saving, or use Flatten PDF afterwards.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
