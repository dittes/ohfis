How Sunrise & Sunset Calculator works

Choose a city or enter coordinates and a date and get sunrise, sunset, solar noon, the length of daylight, how much longer or shorter the day is than yesterday, the golden hour before sunset, and civil and nautical twilight. Times are shown in the time zone you pick, and polar day and polar night are recognised.

The calculation uses the NOAA solar equations: the position of the sun (declination and the equation of time) is computed for the date, and the hour angle at which the centre of the sun is 90.833° from the zenith (the standard value including refraction and the sun's radius) gives sunrise and sunset. Civil twilight uses 96° and nautical 102°. Accuracy is about one to two minutes in mid-latitudes and worse near the poles. Hills, buildings and weather are not considered, and the time zone you choose is only used for display. The place list is built in and the "Use my location" button reads your position only after your permission and only in your browser.

How to use it

  1. Pick a city or enter latitude and longitude.

  2. Choose the date and the time zone to display.

  3. Read sunrise, sunset, daylight and twilight times.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate are the times?

Within about 1–2 minutes at mid-latitudes, compared with official almanac values.

What is the golden hour?

The hour before sunset (and after sunrise) with low, warm light, popular for photography. Here it is shown as the last hour before sunset.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
