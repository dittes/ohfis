Save the rotation permanently

Most PDF viewers only turn a page for the moment. This tool saves the rotation in the file, so it opens correctly for everyone you send it to.

Rotate single pages or all of them at once. Nothing is re-rendered, so quality does not change, and form fields, links and bookmarks keep working.

How to rotate a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF file.

  2. Rotate individual pages, or select several and use the buttons in the side panel.

  3. Click “Save rotated PDF” and download the file.

Why people use it

Other online PDF tools upload your document to their servers. This one does the work inside your browser, so there is nothing to intercept, store or delete later.

Free, with no limits to work around

No account, no email address, no daily quota and no watermark on the result. The only limit is what your device can handle.

Frequently asked questions

Will rotating reduce the quality?

No. Only the orientation setting of the page changes, and its content is not touched.

Can I rotate just one page?

Yes. Each page has its own rotate button.

Why does my PDF viewer still show it sideways?

Close and reopen the downloaded file. The new version has the rotation saved in it.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
