How Flight Arrival Time & Jet Lag Calculator works

Enter the departure date, time and time zone, the flight time and the arrival zone and get the local arrival date and time, the time-zone shift and the total travel time. Add a layover and a second flight to see the final arrival in the last time zone, with a short jet-lag hint.

The departure wall-clock time is converted to an exact instant with the time-zone database of your browser (so daylight saving time on that date is handled, including the weeks in which the US and Europe change on different days), the flight duration is added, and the instant is shown in the arrival zone. The shift is the difference of the two UTC offsets. Flights over midnight and across the date line show the correct local date. Use the scheduled block time from your ticket; delays are not considered. The jet-lag rule of thumb (about a day per hour of shift) is an approximation, not medical advice.

How to use it

  1. Enter the departure and the flight time.

  2. Add a layover and second flight if you have one.

  3. Read the arrival time and the shift.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the arrival the next day?

Because the flight crosses midnight in the arrival zone. The date shown is the local date there.

Does it include daylight saving time?

Yes, for the dates of the trip.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
