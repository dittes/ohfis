How URL Extractor & Tracking Parameter Remover works

Shared links are often full of tracking tags such as utm_source, fbclid, gclid or mc_eid. They make links long and tell the sender who clicked. Paste a link or a whole text and get clean URLs with the tracking parameters removed, or the text back with its links cleaned in place.

URLs are found with a pattern that ignores trailing punctuation, then parsed with the browser's URL parser. Query parameters whose names match a list of known trackers (utm_*, fbclid, gclid, dclid, msclkid, mc_cid, mc_eid, igshid, yclid, _hsenc, ref, and others) are deleted; functional parameters such as ?id=7 stay. You can also remove the entire query string or the #fragment.

How to use it

  1. Paste a link or text containing links.

  2. Choose a list of clean URLs or the full text with cleaned links.

  3. Copy the result. The summary shows which parameters were removed.

Frequently asked questions

Will removing parameters break the link?

Only known tracking parameters are removed. Parameters the page needs, like ?id=7 or ?page=2, stay. Use "remove the whole query string" only if you know the page works without it.

What is fbclid?

A Facebook click identifier added to outgoing links. It lets Facebook connect a click with an account and is not needed to open the page.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
