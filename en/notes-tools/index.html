Notes, passwords and privacy tools

Scratchpads, checklists, passwords, QR codes and encrypted notes. Every tool runs in your browser. Nothing you paste or open is uploaded.

Password Generator Create strong random passwords with custom rules, right in your browser. Passphrase Generator (Diceware) Memorable passphrases like correct-horse-battery-staple from the EFF word list. JWT Decoder & Verifier Read a JSON Web Token's header, payload and expiry without sending it anywhere. Wi-Fi QR Code Generator Make a printable QR code that lets guests join your Wi-Fi with one scan. vCard QR Code Generator (Contact QR) Create a QR code that saves your contact details to a phone's address book. QR Code Generator Create QR codes for links, text, email and SMS, free and without tracking. Encrypted Note Link (Private Message) Encrypt a short message in your browser and share it as a link plus a separate key. Online Scratchpad (Auto-Saving Notepad) A plain notepad that saves automatically in your browser, with several pads. Checklist with Progress Bar Tick off items and watch the completion percentage, saved in your browser. Kanban Board (To Do, Doing, Done) A personal three-column task board with drag and drop, stored locally. Tally Counter (Click Counter) A big on-screen counter for attendees, votes or stock, with keyboard shortcuts. Markdown Table of Contents Generator Generate a linked table of contents from the headings of a Markdown document. Template Variable Filler ({{name}}) Fill {{placeholders}} in email templates, canned replies and AI prompts. Decision Matrix (Weighted Scoring) Score options against weighted criteria to decide objectively. Daily Standup Update Formatter Turn rough notes into a clean Yesterday / Today / Blockers update for Slack. Paste as Plain Text (Remove Formatting) A staging box that strips fonts, colours and links before you paste elsewhere. Markdown to Formatted Email Write in Markdown and paste rich, styled text straight into Gmail or Outlook. Snippet Vault (Click-to-Copy Text Snippets) Keep the texts you paste every day and copy them with one click. Rich Text to Clean HTML Converter Paste from Word or Google Docs and get tidy HTML without styles, fonts and junk. Braille Translator (Text to Braille) Convert text to Unicode braille and back, with capital and number signs. Emergency Contact Sheet (Printable PDF) Make a one-page sheet of phone numbers, medical info and notes for the fridge. Writing Sprint Timer with Word Count Write for a set time with a live word count, pace and goal tracking.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.

Privacy Imprint Runs in your browser. No upload, no account, no tracking.
