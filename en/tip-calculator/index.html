How Tip Calculator & Gratuity Table works

Enter the bill and the number of people to see the tip, total and amount per person for several rates side by side, so you can choose what is appropriate for the service and the country.

Tip = bill × rate; total = bill + tip; per person = total ÷ people. Values are rounded to cents. Customs differ: 15–20% is standard in the United States and Canada, 5–10% or rounding up is typical in much of Europe, and in some countries service is already included or tipping is not expected.

How to use it

  1. Enter the bill and number of people.

  2. Edit the list of tip rates if you like.

  3. Read the tip, total and share per person.

Frequently asked questions

Is the tip calculated on the pre-tax amount?

Many people tip on the pre-tax subtotal; enter that amount as the bill.

How much should I tip in Germany?

Usually round up or add 5–10% for good service.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
