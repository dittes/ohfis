How Daily Standup Update Formatter works

Write three short lists and get an update that is ready to paste into Slack, Teams or an email: bold headings, tidy bullets, empty sections filled with "Nothing to report" or "None". Choose Slack-style bold, Markdown or plain text.

Each line is trimmed, leading bullets or dashes are removed and every item is written with a bullet. Slack and Teams use single asterisks for bold, Markdown uses double, and plain text uses a colon, which the format option handles. Nothing is sent anywhere; the text is only reformatted in your browser. A good update is specific and short: outcomes, not activities.

How to use it

  1. Write what you did, what you will do and any blockers, one item per line.

  2. Choose the format.

  3. Copy the update into your chat.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a good standup update?

Outcomes instead of activities, one line each, and blockers that name who can help.

Does it work for Microsoft Teams?

Yes. Use the Slack/Teams format for bold headings.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
