How Meeting Agenda Planner with Times works

Write each agenda item with its length, for example "Status round; 15", choose the start time, and get a timed agenda with the start and end of every item, the total length and the end of the meeting. Enter how much time you have and the tool warns you when the plan is too long, or shows how many minutes are left.

Copy the agenda as plain text for an invitation or as a Markdown table for a wiki. Short, timed agendas keep meetings on track: if an item has no minutes, ask whether it needs a meeting at all.

How to use it

  1. Choose the start time and, optionally, the time you have.

  2. List the items as "title; minutes", one per line.

  3. Check the end time and copy the agenda into your invitation.

Frequently asked questions

What formats do the lines accept?

"Title; 10", "Title - 10" or "Title | 10 min" all work. Without a number an item gets 5 minutes.

Does it add breaks?

No. Add a line such as "Break; 10" where you want one.

Can I get a calendar file?

Not from this tool. Use the ICS Event Generator with the start and end time shown here.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
