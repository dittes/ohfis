Document and print tools

Booklets, labels, Bates numbers and Word conversion. Every tool runs in your browser. Nothing you paste or open is uploaded.

PDF Booklet Maker (Print as Folded Booklet) Rearrange PDF pages so a duplex printout folds into a booklet. PDF Multiple Pages per Sheet (N-up) Put 2, 4, 6 or 9 PDF pages on one sheet to save paper. Bates Numbering for PDF Stamp a unique sequential ID such as CASE-000001 on every page. Add Margins or Note Space to a PDF Enlarge each page with white space for notes, binding or printing. Paper Size Calculator (A4, Letter, Pixels & PPI) Convert paper sizes to mm, inches and pixels, or find the print size of an image. Mailing Label Printer (Avery Templates to PDF) Turn a list of addresses into a print-ready PDF for Avery label sheets. Certificate Maker (Printable PDF Certificates) Create certificates for many names at once and download them as one PDF. Markdown to Word (DOCX) Converter Convert Markdown notes into a real Word document with headings, lists and tables. Word (DOCX) to Markdown Converter Extract the structure of a Word document as clean Markdown, locally. PDF Highlights & Comments Extractor List every highlight, note and comment in a PDF and export them as Markdown or CSV. PDF to Grayscale Converter (Black & White) Convert a colour PDF to grayscale or black and white to save ink and size. Printer Test Page (Alignment, Scale & Colour) Print a sheet that checks margins, scale, grey steps, colours, lines and text. Scan Separator Sheet Maker (Batch Scanning) Print numbered separator pages with a QR code to split a stack of scans into documents. Unzip Files Online (ZIP, TAR and GZ Extractor) Open an archive, see what is inside and save single files or a selection. Create ZIP File Online (Compress Files and Folders) Pack files and whole folders into one ZIP file. PDF to HTML Converter Turn the text of a PDF into clean HTML with headings, paragraphs and lists. Word Document Word Counter (DOCX) Count words, characters, paragraphs and reading time in a .docx file. Compress PDF to a Target Size (2 MB, 500 KB …) Shrink a PDF step by step until it fits an upload limit. Compare Two PDFs (Text and Visual Differences) Find what changed between two versions of a PDF, page by page. Search Text in a PDF (Find Words and Pages) List every match of a word in a PDF with page number and context. EPUB to PDF Converter Turn the text of an EPUB e-book into a PDF you can print or read anywhere. Delivery Note Generator (PDF) Create a delivery note with items and a signature field for the recipient. Receipt Generator (PDF) Create a receipt for a payment, with VAT shown and a signature line.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.

Privacy Imprint Runs in your browser. No upload, no account, no tracking.
