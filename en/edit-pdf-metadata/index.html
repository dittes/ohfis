What your PDF gives away

PDFs carry hidden details: the author's name, the company, the software used, and when the file was created. That can be embarrassing or risky when a document is shared publicly, as the name of whoever wrote it travels along.

Here you can read and edit the title, author, subject, keywords and more. A clear title also helps, since search engines and PDF viewers may show it. “Remove all metadata” clears everything at once, including the hidden XMP data.

How to edit PDF metadata

  1. Choose your PDF file.

  2. Review the fields and edit them, or choose “Remove all metadata”.

  3. Save and download your updated PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Which metadata is removed?

Title, author, subject, keywords, creator, producer, dates and the XMP metadata stream.

Does this change the page content?

No. Only the document properties change, and your pages stay exactly the same.

Why edit a PDF title?

Browsers and search results often show the document title, so a clear one looks more professional than “Untitled-1”.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
