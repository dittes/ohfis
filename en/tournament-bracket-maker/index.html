How Tournament Bracket Maker works

List the teams or players from best to worst seed and get a bracket for single elimination, with byes where the number is not a power of two, or a round-robin schedule where everyone plays everyone. Download the bracket as PNG or SVG or print it for the hackathon, the office tournament or the school contest.

Standard seeding is used: the bracket size is the next power of two, seed 1 meets the lowest seed, seed 2 the next lowest, and so on, so the strongest seeds can only meet in the late rounds; surplus slots become byes that advance the top seeds automatically. The round robin uses the circle method: one team stays fixed while the others rotate, which gives n − 1 rounds (or n for an odd number of teams) in which every pair meets exactly once. Double elimination is not included.

How to use it

  1. Enter the entrants, best seed first.

  2. Choose single elimination or round robin.

  3. Download or print the bracket.

Frequently asked questions

How are byes assigned?

To the highest seeds, which is the usual convention.

How many matches does a round robin have?

n × (n − 1) ÷ 2, for example 15 matches for 6 teams.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
