Business and team tools

Margins, runway, VAT and pricing; estimates, decision tools, brackets, pickers and org charts. Every tool runs in your browser. Nothing you paste or open is uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.

Privacy Imprint Runs in your browser. No upload, no account, no tracking.
