How Image Converter (JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF) works

Drop one or many images and convert them to the format you need: WebP for smaller web images, JPG for maximum compatibility, PNG for lossless graphics with transparency, or AVIF where your browser can create it. Quality is adjustable and everything happens on your device, so photos stay private and there is no upload wait.

Each file is decoded by the browser (honouring the camera rotation flag), drawn onto a canvas and re-encoded with canvas.toBlob in the chosen format and quality. For JPG, transparent areas are filled with the background colour you pick because JPG has no transparency. The tool checks whether the browser really produced the requested format and says so otherwise (AVIF encoding is not available in every browser). Several results can be downloaded as one ZIP file.

How to use it

  1. Drop your images or choose them.

  2. Pick the target format and quality.

  3. Download single files or everything as a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Which format is smallest?

WebP and AVIF are usually 25–50% smaller than JPG at similar visual quality; PNG is largest for photos but lossless.

Does converting reduce quality?

Converting to JPG, WebP or AVIF is lossy, so use a high quality setting if you may edit again. PNG is lossless.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
