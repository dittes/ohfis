For files you are allowed to open

Bank statements and payslips often arrive password protected, which gets tiresome when you open them regularly. Enter the password once, and download a copy that opens without one. Restrictions on printing and copying are removed too.

This tool needs the password if the PDF requires one to open: it does not crack passwords. PDFs that open freely but restrict printing or editing can be unlocked without one. Please only unlock documents you have the right to use.

How to unlock a PDF

  1. Choose your protected PDF.

  2. Enter the password, or leave the field empty if the PDF opens without one.

  3. Click “Unlock PDF” and download the unprotected copy.

Why people use it

Your file never leaves your device

Other online PDF tools upload your document to their servers. This one does the work inside your browser, so there is nothing to intercept, store or delete later.

Free, with no limits to work around

No account, no email address, no daily quota and no watermark on the result. The only limit is what your device can handle.

Works anywhere you have a browser

Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iPhone. Nothing to install, and once a page has loaded it keeps working without an internet connection.

Frequently asked questions

Can you recover a forgotten password?

No. This tool removes a password you know, and does not try to guess it.

Is my password sent anywhere?

No. It is used in your browser to decrypt the file and is then gone.

Is the unlocked copy still safe to share?

It has no protection any more, so anyone who gets it can read it. Handle it accordingly.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
