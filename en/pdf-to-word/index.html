What to expect from the conversion

Choose Editable text to get real Word paragraphs: the tool rebuilds headings, paragraphs and bold or italic styling from the PDF. That works best for reports, letters and articles. Images and multi-column layouts are not carried over, so complex designs need some rework.

Choose Exact look if appearance matters more than editing. Every page then becomes a picture in the Word file, identical to the original. For scanned documents, run OCR PDF first, since a scan contains no text to convert.

How to convert PDF to Word

  1. Choose your PDF file.

  2. Pick Editable text or Exact look.

  3. Click “Convert to Word” and download the .docx file.

Frequently asked questions

Are tables and images converted?

In Editable text mode, the text is converted but images and table structure are not. For tables, try PDF to Excel. Exact look keeps everything visible, but not editable.

Can I convert a scanned PDF to Word?

Yes. Run OCR PDF first to recognize the text, then convert the result.

Which programs open the file?

Microsoft Word, LibreOffice, Google Docs, Pages and any other app that reads .docx.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
