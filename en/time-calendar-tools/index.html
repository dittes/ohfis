Time, date and calendar tools

Time zones, working days, timestamps, calendar files and timers. Every tool runs in your browser. Nothing you paste or open is uploaded.

Unix Timestamp Converter Convert epoch seconds or milliseconds to a date and back, in any time zone. Date Difference Calculator Exact time between two dates in years, months, days, weeks, hours and business days. Working Days Calculator Add or subtract working days and count them between dates, skipping weekends and public holidays. Calendar Event (.ics) Generator Create a calendar file for an email invitation, no Outlook or Google account needed. Time Zone Meeting Planner Find the hours when everyone is in working time across cities and time zones. Meeting Time Link Generator Create a link that shows the meeting time in each recipient's own time zone. Meeting Cost Calculator See what a meeting costs in real time from headcount and salaries. Pomodoro Timer with Focus Sounds Focus and break cycles with built-in white noise, rain and coffee-shop sounds. Hours and Minutes to Decimal Converter Convert 7:45 to 7.75 for timesheets and invoices, with rounding rules. Timecard & Overtime Calculator Total a week of punch-in and punch-out times, with breaks and overtime. Time Calculator (Add & Subtract Hours) Add and subtract durations like 4h 12m + 1h 55m or 2:30 - 0:45. ISO Week Number & Quarter Calculator Find the ISO week number, day of the year and fiscal quarter of any date. Recurring Date List Generator List the dates of an event that repeats, such as every other Tuesday. Julian Date & Ordinal Date Converter Convert calendar dates to Julian Day numbers and YYYY-DDD ordinal dates. Countdown Timer for Presentations A big countdown that turns orange and red as time runs short, with full screen mode. Stopwatch with Lap Times (CSV Export) Millisecond stopwatch that records laps and exports them as CSV. World Clock A custom grid of live clocks for your offices and contacts around the world. Sprint Schedule Generator Lay out sprint dates, planning days and reviews for a quarter or a year. Sunrise & Sunset Calculator Sunrise, sunset, daylight length and twilight for any place and date. Moon Phase Calculator See the moon phase on any date and the next new and full moons. Year Progress Bar (Days Left in the Year) How much of the year, quarter, month and week has passed, and how many days are left. Fiscal Year Calculator (Fiscal Quarter & Period) Find the fiscal year and quarter of a date and map months to fiscal quarters. Availability Formatter (Time Slots in Several Time Zones) Turn your free time ranges into a message that shows every time zone. Flight Arrival Time & Jet Lag Calculator Work out the local arrival time across time zones, with an optional layover and second flight. Weekly Work Hours Budget Planner Check whether your planned work fits into the hours you really have this week. OKR Quarter Timeline & Check-in Schedule Get the dates for kickoff, weekly check-ins, mid-quarter review and grading.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.

Privacy Imprint Runs in your browser. No upload, no account, no tracking.
