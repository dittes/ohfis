How Ratio Calculator works

Use it for recipes, scale drawings, screen sizes and mixing ratios. Fill three of the four fields of A : B = C : D and the fourth is calculated by cross-multiplication. The second part simplifies any ratio to its smallest whole numbers.

Cross-multiplication: A × D = B × C, so the unknown is the product of the opposite pair divided by the remaining value.

How to use it

  1. Fill three of the four fields.

  2. Read the missing value.

  3. Or enter two numbers to simplify their ratio.

Frequently asked questions

How do I scale a recipe?

Set A : B to old servings : new servings and C to an ingredient amount; the result X is the scaled amount.

What is a ratio of 1.5 : 3?

Multiply both sides by 10 and divide by the common factor: 1 : 2.
