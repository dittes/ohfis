How Year Progress Bar (Days Left in the Year) works

See at a glance how far the year, the quarter, the month and the week have progressed, with percentages, day numbers and days left, plus the weeks left in the year, the ISO week number and the working days remaining until 31 December (optionally minus public holidays of a country).

The year progress is the day number divided by the length of the year (365 or 366), so the 1st of July is 49.7 % in a leap year and 49.9 % otherwise; the chosen day counts as completed. The week runs Monday to Sunday. Working days are the Monday–Friday days from the chosen date to the end of the year, inclusive, minus public holidays from the built-in holiday sets (national holidays only, no regional ones). You can choose any date, not only today, for planning.

How to use it

  1. Pick a date (today by default).

  2. Choose a holiday set if you want working days.

  3. Read the progress bars and the days left.

Frequently asked questions

Does the chosen day count as done?

Yes. The day number includes the chosen day.

Are regional holidays included?

No, only national public holidays.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed by your own browser. You can check it yourself: open your browser's network tab while using the tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely. There is no sign-up, no paid tier and no watermark on your files.
