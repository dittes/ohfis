Como funciona Calculadora de ponto de equilíbrio

Enter fixed costs, the price per unit and the variable cost per unit, and the calculator shows the number of units and the revenue at which you stop losing money, with a chart of revenue against total costs. Add a target profit to see how many more units it takes, and an expected volume to see your margin of safety.

Contribution per unit = price − variable cost. Break-even units = (fixed costs + target profit) ÷ contribution, rounded up to a whole unit. The contribution margin ratio is contribution ÷ price. Margin of safety = (expected units − break-even units) ÷ expected units, the share by which sales could fall before you make a loss. The model assumes constant price and variable cost.

Como usar

  1. Enter fixed costs, price and variable cost per unit.

  2. Optionally add a profit target and expected volume.

  3. Read the break-even point and the chart.

Perguntas frequentes

What counts as a fixed cost?

Costs that do not change with volume in the short term: rent, salaries, subscriptions, insurance.

What if the price is below variable cost?

There is no break-even point: every unit sold deepens the loss.
