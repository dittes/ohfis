Como funciona Conversor de vídeo para GIF

Choose where the GIF starts, how long it runs, the frames per second and the width, and get an animated GIF. A few seconds at 10–15 frames per second and 480 px wide makes a handy reaction or demo GIF.

The colours come from a palette made for exactly this clip (FFmpeg’s palettegen and paletteuse filters), which looks far better than the default 256-colour GIF. GIFs grow fast with length, size and frame rate; for anything longer than a few seconds a short MP4 is much smaller. The video is processed by FFmpeg, the standard open-source video tool, compiled to WebAssembly and running inside your browser. The first use downloads the engine (about 31 MB), which your browser then keeps. Nothing is uploaded. Everything happens in your browser’s memory, so very large videos (several gigabytes) or long 4K clips can fail on a phone or an older computer; try a shorter or smaller clip in that case.

Como usar

  1. Drop the video and set the start with "use current position".

  2. Choose length, frame rate and width.

  3. Press "Make GIF" and download.

Perguntas frequentes

The GIF is huge.

Shorten it, lower the frame rate to 8–10 and the width to 320–480 px.

Is there sound?

No. The GIF format has no sound.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.

Esta ferramenta é gratuita?

Sim, totalmente. Não há cadastro, plano pago nem marca-d'água nos seus arquivos.
