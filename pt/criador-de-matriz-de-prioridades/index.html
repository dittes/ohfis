Como funciona Criador de matriz de prioridades (impacto/esforço e Eisenhower)

Prioritising is easier when you see the options side by side. In the impact/effort view each task gets an impact and an effort from 1 to 5; the tool puts it into quick wins (high impact, low effort), big bets, fill-ins or money pits and sorts every box by the best ratio. In the Eisenhower view you mark each task as urgent and important and get the four quadrants: do now, plan, delegate, drop.

It is a thinking aid, not a decision machine. Score together with your team, challenge the numbers that surprise you, and copy the result as Markdown into your planning document.

Como usar

  1. Choose impact/effort or the Eisenhower matrix.

  2. Enter one task per line with its two values.

  3. Read the quadrants and copy them as Markdown.

Perguntas frequentes

How are the impact and effort numbers defined?

Use any consistent 1–5 scale. A common one is: impact = value for users or the business, effort = days of work and risk.

What counts as "high"?

Impact 3 or more is high; effort 3 or less is low.

How are tasks sorted inside a box?

By impact minus effort, so the best ratio comes first.
