Como funciona Gerador de frases-senha (Diceware)

A passphrase of five or six random words is much easier to type and remember than a string of symbols, and just as strong. Choose the number of words, a separator and optional capitalisation or a digit, and see the exact entropy.

Words are picked uniformly at random from the EFF Large Wordlist, a list of 7,776 common, easy-to-spell words (one for every roll of five dice), using crypto.getRandomValues. Each word adds log₂(7776) = 12.9 bits, so five words give about 65 bits and six about 78 bits. The security comes from the randomness of the choice, not from the words being obscure: do not swap words for ones you like better. The EFF wordlist is © Electronic Frontier Foundation and licensed under CC BY 3.0 US.

Como usar

  1. Choose the number of words (six for important accounts).

  2. Pick a separator and options.

  3. Memorise or store the passphrase; never reuse it.

Perguntas frequentes

How many words do I need?

Five words (65 bits) is good; six (78 bits) is excellent for a master password.

Is it as secure as a random password?

Per character no, but a six-word passphrase has the entropy of a 12-character random password and is far easier to remember.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.
