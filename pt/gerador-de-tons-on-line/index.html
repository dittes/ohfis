Como funciona Gerador de tons on-line (senoidal, quadrado, varredura)

Play a pure tone at any frequency from 1 Hz to 20 kHz with sine, square, triangle or sawtooth waves, on both speakers or only the left or right one. A 30-second sweep slides from 20 Hz to 20 kHz so you can find the frequency at which a speaker rattles, a cabinet resonates or coil whine appears. The display shows the nearest musical note.

The tone is produced live by an oscillator in the Web Audio API and passed through a gain stage (volume follows a squared curve so the lower end is gentle) and a stereo panner for the channel choice. The frequency slider is logarithmic because pitch is perceived logarithmically: each octave takes the same slider distance. The sweep increases the frequency exponentially over 30 seconds. Listen at a low volume first: pure tones can be loud and tiring, and very high or low tones can damage small speakers if played loudly.

Como usar

  1. Set the volume low, choose a frequency and press Play.

  2. Use the channel selector to test left and right, or start the sweep to find a resonance.

  3. Stop as soon as you hear the problem frequency and note the value.

Perguntas frequentes

What is the range of human hearing?

About 20 Hz to 20 kHz in young people; most adults cannot hear above 15–17 kHz.

Why does the tone seem to vanish at low frequencies?

Small speakers and headphones cannot reproduce very low bass, so you may feel or hear only harmonics.
