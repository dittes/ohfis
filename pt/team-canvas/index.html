Como funciona Team Canvas (interativo, com post-its)

A team canvas helps a new or struggling team agree on the basics on one page: why the team exists (purpose), what it wants to achieve (goals and success criteria), what it stands for (values), who does what (roles and skills), what each person needs, where the team is strong or weak and how it works together (rules and activities).

Run it as a workshop: everybody writes sticky notes, you sort and discuss them, and the team commits to the result. The board is saved in this browser and nothing is uploaded; export it as PNG or Markdown. The Team Canvas was created by Alex Ivanov and is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0; this board is an adapted, re-drawn version and carries the credit.

Como usar

  1. Start with purpose and goals, then values and roles.

  2. Let everybody add sticky notes, then sort and discuss them together.

  3. Agree on the rules, export the canvas and review it after a few weeks.

Perguntas frequentes

When should a team use it?

At the start of a project or team, when people join or leave, or when collaboration feels stuck.

How long does a session take?

Plan about 60 to 90 minutes for a team of five to eight people.

Is it saved?

In this browser only. Save the board as a file to share it with the team.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.

Esta ferramenta é gratuita?

Sim, totalmente. Não há cadastro, plano pago nem marca-d'água nos seus arquivos.
