Como funciona Calculadora de aluguel proporcional

When a lease or license starts in the middle of the month, only part of the month is charged. Enter the full monthly amount and the start date to get the first-month charge for the days that remain, including the start day.

Three methods are in use: actual days (amount × days remaining ÷ days in that month; February and 31-day months differ), a 30-day month (a common convention in leases) and a yearly rate (monthly × 12 ÷ 365 per day). The calculator shows the formula it used. Your contract decides which method applies, so use the one that is written there.

Como usar

  1. Enter the full monthly amount and the move-in date.

  2. Choose the proration method of your contract.

  3. Copy the charge and the daily rate.

Perguntas frequentes

Is the move-in day charged?

Normally yes, the day counts as an occupied day.

Why does February give a higher daily rate?

With the actual-days method, February has fewer days, so each day costs more.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.

Esta ferramenta é gratuita?

Sim, totalmente. Não há cadastro, plano pago nem marca-d'água nos seus arquivos.
