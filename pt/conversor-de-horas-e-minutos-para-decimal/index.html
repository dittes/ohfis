Como funciona Conversor de horas e minutos para decimal

Billing systems and spreadsheets want 7.75, your timesheet says 7:45. Paste a list of times in any common form (7:45, 7h 45m, 465 minutes, 7.75) and get decimal hours, the h:mm form and a running total. Round to billing increments such as 6 minutes (0.1 h) or 15 minutes (0.25 h), up, down or to the nearest.

Minutes are divided by 60 (45 minutes = 0.75 hours, 10 minutes = 0.1667). Rounding uses the increment you pick: 7.9 h rounds to 8.00 at the nearest quarter hour, to 7.75 when rounding down. Lines can also contain sums and differences ("1:30 - 0:45"). The total is calculated from the rounded lines so that the sum matches what you will invoice.

Como usar

  1. Paste your times, one per line.

  2. Choose a rounding increment and mode, and the number of decimals.

  3. Copy the decimals and the total.

Perguntas frequentes

How do I convert 7 hours 45 minutes to decimal?

45 ÷ 60 = 0.75, so 7 h 45 min is 7.75 hours.

Should I round up when billing?

That depends on your contract. Many agencies bill in 6- or 15-minute increments rounded up; check what you agreed with the client.
