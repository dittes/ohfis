Como funciona Conversor de cores (HEX, RGB, HSL, CMYK)

Type a colour in any common notation (#2557D6, rgb(37 87 214), hsl(223 70% 48%), cmyk(83 59 0 16) or a name such as coral) and get all the others, ready to copy. Use the picker or, in Chrome and Edge, the eyedropper to sample a colour from anywhere on your screen.

RGB is the base. HSL and HSV are derived with the standard formulas, HEX is RGB in base 16 (with the alpha channel as an eighth and ninth digit when present), and CMYK uses the naive conversion K = 1 − max(R, G, B) with C, M and Y derived from it. That CMYK is a rough guide only: real print colours depend on ink, paper and colour profile. Alpha values from rgb(… / 50%) and 8-digit HEX are preserved.

Perguntas frequentes

How do I convert HEX to RGB?

Split the six digits into pairs and read each as a base-16 number: #2557D6 → 25, 57, D6 → 37, 87, 214.

Can I convert RGB to CMYK exactly?

Not exactly: screens and printers have different colour ranges. The formula is a close estimate; use a colour-managed workflow for print jobs.
