Como funciona Verificador de similaridade de textos (compare dois textos)

Paste two texts and see the share of identical word sequences, the similarity of the words used and a list of the passages that appear in both. Useful to check a rewrite against its source, to find a copied paragraph between two documents or to see how much two versions of a contract differ.

The tool ignores case and punctuation, compares three-word sequences (shingles) and lists maximal identical runs of at least the length you choose. It only compares the two texts you paste: it is not a plagiarism checker against the internet, and reworded text is not recognised. For a line-by-line comparison with insertions and deletions use the Text Diff Checker.

Como usar

  1. Paste text A and text B.

  2. Set the shortest passage to report (default 5 words).

  3. Read the percentages and the shared passages.

Perguntas frequentes

What does "word sequences in common" mean?

The share of three-word sequences that occur in both texts, compared with all sequences in either. 100 % means the texts are identical in wording.

Is this a plagiarism checker?

Not for the web. It only compares the two texts you give it.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.

Esta ferramenta é gratuita?

Sim, totalmente. Não há cadastro, plano pago nem marca-d'água nos seus arquivos.
