Como funciona Conversor de vídeo (MP4, WebM, GIF, MP3)

Drop a video and convert it to MP4 (H.264, plays on every phone, TV and browser), WebM (VP8, for websites), an animated GIF or an audio file (MP3, M4A, WAV). You can limit the width, remove the sound and choose between fast and high-quality encoding.

Converting is the usual fix when a video will not play or upload somewhere: MOV from an iPhone, MKV or AVI from older cameras and downloads all become a plain MP4. The video is processed by FFmpeg, the standard open-source video tool, compiled to WebAssembly and running inside your browser. The first use downloads the engine (about 31 MB), which your browser then keeps. Nothing is uploaded. Everything happens in your browser’s memory, so very large videos (several gigabytes) or long 4K clips can fail on a phone or an older computer; try a shorter or smaller clip in that case.

Como usar

  1. Drop the video.

  2. Choose the target format and, if you like, a maximum width.

  3. Press "Convert" and download the file.

Perguntas frequentes

Which format should I choose?

MP4 for almost everything. WebM for websites that prefer open formats. GIF only for short silent loops. MP3 if you only need the sound.

Is the quality reduced?

Re-encoding always loses a little. "High quality" keeps it visually very close to the original; it is slower and gives a larger file.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.

Esta ferramenta é gratuita?

Sim, totalmente. Não há cadastro, plano pago nem marca-d'água nos seus arquivos.
