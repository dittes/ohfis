Como funciona Redimensionador de imagens em lote

Shrink a folder of photos for email or the web: fit them inside a maximum width and height, set an exact size, or scale by a percentage. Aspect ratio is preserved unless you ask for exact dimensions, small images are not enlarged by default, and the output can keep the original format or switch to JPG, PNG or WebP.

Each image is decoded with its rotation flag applied and scaled on a canvas with high-quality smoothing. When an image is reduced by more than half, it is halved step by step first (progressive downscaling), which avoids the jagged look of one big jump. Results show the old and new dimensions and file size, and everything is zipped for download. Nothing is uploaded.

Como usar

  1. Drop your images.

  2. Choose how to resize and the output format.

  3. Download the results or the ZIP.

Perguntas frequentes

Will the picture be distorted?

No, unless you choose exact width and height that differ from the original ratio. Fit and percent keep the proportions.

What size should web images have?

Usually 1200–1600 px wide for full-width photos and 800 px for content images; 2× the displayed size serves sharp images on high-density screens.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.

Esta ferramenta é gratuita?

Sim, totalmente. Não há cadastro, plano pago nem marca-d'água nos seus arquivos.
