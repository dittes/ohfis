Como funciona Cronômetro Pomodoro com sons de foco

The Pomodoro technique alternates focused work (traditionally 25 minutes) with short breaks, and a longer break after four rounds. Set your own lengths, start the timer and the countdown also appears in the browser tab title, so you can see it while working in another tab. A soft tone marks the end of each phase.

Background sounds are not audio files: they are generated live with the Web Audio API. White noise is random samples; pink noise filters it with the Voss–McCartney method for a softer sound; brown noise integrates it for a deep rumble; rain is white noise band-limited between 1.2 and 7 kHz; the coffee-shop murmur is noise shaped by a band-pass filter around the range of human speech. The timer uses the system clock, so it stays accurate even when the tab runs in the background.

Como usar

  1. Set the focus and break lengths, and choose a background sound if you like.

  2. Press Start and work until the tone sounds.

  3. Take the break, then continue; after the set number of sessions you get a long break.

Perguntas frequentes

Does the timer keep running in a background tab?

Yes. It compares against the system time on every tick, so it catches up even when the browser slows background tabs down.

Do the sounds need an internet connection?

No. They are generated in your browser.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.

Esta ferramenta é gratuita?

Sim, totalmente. Não há cadastro, plano pago nem marca-d'água nos seus arquivos.
