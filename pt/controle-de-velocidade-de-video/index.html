Como funciona Controle de velocidade de vídeo (câmera lenta e avanço rápido)

Open a video from your device and change its speed with a slider or presets between 0.25× and 4×, with or without keeping the voice pitch natural. The tool shows the new running time and can export the result as a WebM file.

The preview sets the playbackRate of an HTML video element, and preservesPitch decides whether the audio is time-stretched (natural voice) or resampled (chipmunk effect). Export plays the video at the chosen speed in real time while recording its output stream with MediaRecorder, so exporting takes as long as the new duration and the output is re-encoded VP8/VP9 WebM with some quality loss. Formats depend on what your browser can play: MP4 (H.264), WebM and most phone videos work. For lossless or frame-exact editing a desktop editor is better.

Como usar

  1. Choose a video file.

  2. Set the speed and pitch option.

  3. Preview, then export if you need a new file.

Perguntas frequentes

Does the export reduce quality?

Yes, it is recorded and re-encoded in real time. Use it for sharing, not for archives.

Can I speed up above 4×?

Browsers limit playback speed; export twice if needed.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.

Esta ferramenta é gratuita?

Sim, totalmente. Não há cadastro, plano pago nem marca-d'água nos seus arquivos.
