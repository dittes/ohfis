Como funciona Removedor e aparador de silêncio de áudio

Drop a recording and let the tool find where the sound really starts and ends. It trims the silent edges, keeps a small padding so that nothing sounds cut off, and can also shorten long pauses inside the recording, for example in a voice memo or a lecture.

A sample counts as silent if its absolute value is below the threshold (default −45 dBFS, adjustable) in every channel. The edges are trimmed to the first and last loud sample minus the padding. With "shorten long pauses" every silent stretch longer than the chosen length is reduced to the padding on each side, so natural short pauses stay. A very low threshold keeps more noise as "sound"; a threshold that is too high cuts off quiet word endings. The result is a 44.1 kHz WAV and plays in the preview before you download it.

Como usar

  1. Drop the audio file.

  2. Set the threshold and padding.

  3. Listen and download the trimmed WAV.

Perguntas frequentes

The tool cuts off the end of my words.

Lower the threshold (for example −55 dB) or raise the padding.

Why is the result empty?

The whole recording is quieter than the threshold. Use a lower value.
