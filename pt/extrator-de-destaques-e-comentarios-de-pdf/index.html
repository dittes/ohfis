Como funciona Extrator de destaques e comentários de PDF

After reading a paper or contract with highlights and sticky notes, drop the PDF here and get a list by page: the type of annotation, the author, the highlighted text and the comment. Copy it as a Markdown list for your notes or download CSV for a spreadsheet.

The tool reads the annotation objects of each page with pdf.js: highlights, underlines, strike-outs, squiggly lines, sticky notes and text boxes. For markup annotations it takes the quadrilaterals that mark the area and collects the text items of the page whose centre lies inside, in reading order, so the highlighted words come out as text. This needs a text layer; scanned PDFs without OCR give highlights without text. Drawings, flattened annotations (burned into the page) and annotations whose app stores the text elsewhere are not found. The file is processed locally.

Como usar

  1. Drop the annotated PDF.

  2. Choose which kinds of annotations to show.

  3. Copy the Markdown or download the CSV.

Perguntas frequentes

Why is the highlighted text empty?

The PDF has no text layer (a scan) or the highlight covers only an image. Run OCR first.

Does it find annotations made in a browser or Preview?

Yes, if the app saved them as standard PDF annotations. Flattened ones are part of the page and cannot be read.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.

Esta ferramenta é gratuita?

Sim, totalmente. Não há cadastro, plano pago nem marca-d'água nos seus arquivos.
