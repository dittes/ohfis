Como funciona Dividir coluna em várias colunas

Separate "Ann Marie Lee" into first and last name, "2024-05-06" into year, month and day, or "AB1234" into letters and digits. Pick the column, choose how to split and the tool inserts the new columns in place, naming them after the original.

Three modes are available. Delimiter splits at a character or a regular expression, optionally into a maximum number of parts (the last part keeps the rest). Character positions cut at the fixed positions you list, for fixed-width codes. Pattern uses a regular expression with capture groups, and each group becomes a column. New columns are padded with empty cells for rows that have fewer parts, so the table stays rectangular.

Como usar

  1. Paste your CSV and choose the column to split.

  2. Pick the split mode and enter the delimiter, positions or pattern.

  3. Copy the new table or download it.

Perguntas frequentes

How do I split only at the first space?

Set "Maximum parts" to 2. The first space splits and the rest stays together in the second column.

Can I split by several different separators?

Yes. Tick the regular expression option and use something like [,;] or \s*[-/]\s*.

Meus arquivos são enviados?
