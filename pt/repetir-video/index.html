Como funciona Repetir vídeo (loop de vídeo)

Enter how many times the clip should play in total (2 to 200) and get one longer video: for a background loop on a screen, a meditation or ambient clip or a short animation that should run for minutes.

Fast mode repeats the clip without re-encoding, so it finishes in seconds with no loss; if a player has trouble with the result, switch it off to re-encode. Very long results get large, so check the length before you start. The video is processed by FFmpeg, the standard open-source video tool, compiled to WebAssembly and running inside your browser. The first use downloads the engine (about 31 MB), which your browser then keeps. Nothing is uploaded. Everything happens in your browser’s memory, so very large videos (several gigabytes) or long 4K clips can fail on a phone or an older computer; try a shorter or smaller clip in that case.

Como usar

  1. Drop the video.

  2. Enter how often it should play.

  3. Press "Make loop" and download.

Perguntas frequentes

Is the loop seamless?

The file simply plays the clip again from the start. If the end does not match the beginning, trim the clip so that it does.

Can I make an endless loop?

A file always has an end. Use a long repeat count, or set the "loop" option of your player.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.

Esta ferramenta é gratuita?

Sim, totalmente. Não há cadastro, plano pago nem marca-d'água nos seus arquivos.
