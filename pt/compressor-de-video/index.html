Como funciona Compressor de vídeo (reduza o tamanho do arquivo de vídeo)

Drop a video, choose how strongly to compress it and, if the picture does not need to be big, a smaller height such as 720p, and get a much smaller MP4. A phone video of 500 MB often shrinks to a fifth with no visible loss for sharing.

The tool re-encodes with H.264 and a constant-quality setting (CRF): the higher the number, the smaller the file. 23 looks almost like the original, 28 is a good compromise for chat and email, above 33 the loss is clear. A slower speed setting makes the file a bit smaller at the same quality but takes longer. The video is processed by FFmpeg, the standard open-source video tool, compiled to WebAssembly and running inside your browser. The first use downloads the engine (about 31 MB), which your browser then keeps. Nothing is uploaded. Everything happens in your browser’s memory, so very large videos (several gigabytes) or long 4K clips can fail on a phone or an older computer; try a shorter or smaller clip in that case.

Como usar

  1. Drop the video.

  2. Choose the quality (CRF) and, if you like, a maximum height.

  3. Press "Compress video" and compare the sizes.

Perguntas frequentes

How small will the file be?

It depends on the content. Compare the "Before" and "Result" numbers; try CRF 28 with 720 p first.

Can I compress to an exact size, such as 10 MB?

Not exactly. Raise CRF and lower the height until the result is below the limit.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.

Esta ferramenta é gratuita?

Sim, totalmente. Não há cadastro, plano pago nem marca-d'água nos seus arquivos.
