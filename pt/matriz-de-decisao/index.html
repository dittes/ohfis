Como funciona Matriz de decisão (pontuação ponderada)

Comparing vendors, job offers or tools? List your criteria, give each a weight from 1 to 10, score every option from 0 to 10 per criterion and see the weighted total, with the best option highlighted. A note appears when the top two are too close to call.

The weighted score of an option is Σ(score × weight) ÷ Σ(weights), which keeps the result on the same 0–10 scale. Making the weights explicit forces a conversation about what matters before the options are scored, which is where most of the value lies. The matrix is a thinking aid, not an oracle: if the result feels wrong, one of the weights probably is.

Como usar

  1. Set your criteria and their weights.

  2. Score each option per criterion.

  3. Read the weighted totals and discuss the close calls.

Perguntas frequentes

How do I choose the weights?

Give 10 to the most important criterion and scale the others against it. Agree on the weights before scoring.

Is the highest score always the right choice?

It is the best match to your stated priorities. Sensitivity matters: change the weights and see if the winner changes.
