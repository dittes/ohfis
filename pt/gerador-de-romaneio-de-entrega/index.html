Como funciona Gerador de romaneio de entrega (PDF)

Enter sender, recipient and delivery address, the items with quantity and unit, an order reference and an optional note, and download a clean one-page PDF delivery note with a signature line for the recipient. The document language can be English, German, Polish or Czech, independent of the page language.

A delivery note lists what was shipped, without prices, and lets the recipient confirm receipt. The PDF is created in your browser with a font that covers accents and umlauts, and nothing is stored or uploaded. It is a layout tool: check what your business or country requires on such documents, and keep a copy for your records.

Como usar

  1. Fill in sender, recipient, number and date.

  2. List the items, one per line: description; quantity; unit.

  3. Create the PDF and print or send it.

Perguntas frequentes

What is the difference between a delivery note and an invoice?

A delivery note documents the delivery and carries no prices; an invoice asks for payment. Many businesses send both.

Can I use my own wording?

The headings follow the chosen language. The item texts and the note are entirely yours.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.

Esta ferramenta é gratuita?

Sim, totalmente. Não há cadastro, plano pago nem marca-d'água nos seus arquivos.
