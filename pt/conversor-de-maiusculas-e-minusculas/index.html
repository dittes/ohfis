Como funciona Conversor de maiúsculas e minúsculas

Fix a headline typed in capitals, format a variable name for code or tidy a list of names. The converter handles 13 styles: UPPER, lower, Title Case, Sentence case, Capitalize Each Word, camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, CONSTANT_CASE, kebab-case, dot.case, aLtErNaTiNg and iNVERSE.

Title Case follows common editorial rules and leaves small words such as "of", "the" and "and" in lower case unless they start the text. Programming styles first split your text into words, even when it is already camelCase or contains punctuation ("HTTPServer" becomes "http_server"), and then join them with the right separator. Accented letters are handled with Unicode rules, so "straße" becomes "STRASSE".

Como usar

Perguntas frequentes

What is the difference between Title Case and Capitalize Each Word?

Title Case keeps small words like "of" and "the" in lower case, as in headlines. Capitalize Each Word capitalizes every word without exceptions.

Does it work with German umlauts and other accents?

Yes. It uses Unicode-aware rules, so ä, ö, ü, ł, é and many more are converted correctly.

Meus arquivos são enviados?
