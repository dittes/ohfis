Como funciona Removedor de emojis

Remove emoji from comments, product descriptions, chat exports or data feeds before importing them into systems that cannot store them. Skin-tone modifiers, flags, keycaps and multi-part emoji such as families are removed completely, with no stray joiner characters left behind.

The tool matches the Unicode "Extended Pictographic" property, regional indicator pairs (flags), variation selectors, zero-width joiners and skin-tone modifiers. Digits, # and * stay untouched even though they appear in keycap emoji. A second mode converts the remaining text to plain ASCII by stripping accents and dropping any other non-ASCII symbol, with an optional replacement string.

Como usar

  1. Paste the text containing emoji.

  2. Optionally enable plain ASCII mode or choose a replacement.

  3. Copy the clean text. The counter shows how many characters were removed.

Perguntas frequentes

Will it remove normal punctuation or accented letters?

No, unless you enable the plain ASCII mode, which also strips accents.

Why do some emoji leave a blank square behind elsewhere?

Older systems cannot display newer emoji. Removing them here avoids that problem.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.

Esta ferramenta é gratuita?

Sim, totalmente. Não há cadastro, plano pago nem marca-d'água nos seus arquivos.
