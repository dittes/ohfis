Como funciona Conversor de PDF para tons de cinza (preto e branco)

Drop a colour PDF and get a grayscale version for printing, or a pure black-and-white version for scans of text. Choose the resolution (100–300 dpi) and the JPEG quality, and optionally boost the contrast of faded pages. Page sizes are kept, and the before and after file sizes are shown.

Each page is rendered to a canvas with pdf.js, the pixels are converted to luminance (0.2126 R + 0.7152 G + 0.0722 B), optionally with a contrast boost or a threshold to black and white, and the result is embedded as a JPEG on a page of the original size. This is a raster conversion: the new PDF has no selectable text, no links and no form fields, and fine vector lines become pixels, so keep the original and use a high resolution for documents with small print. To get searchable text back, run the OCR tool on the result. For colour-managed print workflows a prepress tool is the better choice.

Como usar

  1. Drop the PDF.

  2. Choose grayscale or black and white, the resolution and quality.

  3. Convert and download the new PDF.

Perguntas frequentes

Will the text still be selectable?

No. Pages become images. Use the OCR PDF tool afterwards if you need searchable text.

Why is my file bigger than before?

A text-only PDF is tiny as vectors but large as images. Use 100–150 dpi or keep the original.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.

Esta ferramenta é gratuita?

Sim, totalmente. Não há cadastro, plano pago nem marca-d'água nos seus arquivos.
