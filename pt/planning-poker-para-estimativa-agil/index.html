Como funciona Planning poker para estimativa ágil (um dispositivo)

Planning poker keeps teams from anchoring on the first number they hear: everybody picks a card in secret, then all cards are revealed together. This version needs no server and no accounts. Pass the device around, each player picks a card in turn, and the tool hides the votes until you press "Reveal the cards".

After the reveal you see every vote, the median, the average, the most common card and the spread. A wide spread means the team understands the work differently: let the lowest and highest voter explain, then vote again. Choose Fibonacci, T-shirt sizes or powers of two.

Como usar

  1. Enter the players and the story you want to estimate.

  2. Pass the device: each player picks a card in secret.

  3. Reveal the cards, discuss differences and start a new round.

Perguntas frequentes

Can the team vote on their own phones?

Not in this version, because it has no server. It is made for one shared screen, in a room or a video call.

What do "?" and the coffee cup mean?

"?" means "I do not understand the task", the cup means "I need a break". They are counted but not averaged.

Which deck should I use?

Fibonacci for story points, T-shirt sizes for rough early estimates, powers of two if your team prefers them.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.

Esta ferramenta é gratuita?

Sim, totalmente. Não há cadastro, plano pago nem marca-d'água nos seus arquivos.
