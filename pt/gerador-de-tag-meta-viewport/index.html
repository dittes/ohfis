Como funciona Gerador de tag meta viewport

The viewport meta tag tells mobile browsers to use the device width instead of pretending to be a 980-pixel desktop. Choose the options and copy the tag; the generator keeps pinch-zoom enabled because disabling it fails accessibility requirements.

"width=device-width, initial-scale=1" is the recommended default. viewport-fit=cover lets content extend under the notch on iPhones (combine it with safe-area insets in CSS), and a fixed width is only for special cases. maximum-scale and user-scalable=no are intentionally not offered: they block users with low vision from zooming (WCAG 1.4.4).

Como usar

  1. Choose the width and initial scale.

  2. Add viewport-fit if you design for notched phones.

  3. Copy the tag into your <head>.

Perguntas frequentes

Do I need this tag?

Yes for every responsive website. Without it, phones show a scaled-down desktop layout.

Should I disable zooming?

No. It harms accessibility and most browsers ignore it anyway.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.
