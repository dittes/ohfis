Como funciona Remover quebras de linha

Text copied from a PDF, an email or a terminal is broken into short lines at the width of the original page. Pasting it into a document produces ragged paragraphs. This tool removes the hard line breaks inside paragraphs while keeping the blank lines that separate real paragraphs and, optionally, bullet and numbered list items.

Single line breaks are replaced with spaces, double breaks are kept as paragraph breaks, and words split by a hyphen at the end of a line ("hy-" + "phenation") are joined again. Lines that begin with a bullet or number stay on their own line so lists are not merged into one sentence.

Como usar

  1. Paste the text with the unwanted line breaks.

  2. Keep or drop paragraph breaks, hyphenation repair and list handling.

  3. Copy the flowing text.

Perguntas frequentes

Can I remove all line breaks including paragraphs?

Yes. Untick "Keep paragraph breaks" to get a single block of text.

Why are some words still hyphenated?

Only a hyphen directly before a line break, followed by a lower-case letter, is treated as hyphenation. Genuine compounds such as "well-known" are left alone.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.

Esta ferramenta é gratuita?

Sim, totalmente. Não há cadastro, plano pago nem marca-d'água nos seus arquivos.
