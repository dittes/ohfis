Como funciona Calculadora de retorno de investimento (CAGR)

Enter what you invested, what it is worth now and for how many years. You get the gain, the total return in percent and the compound annual growth rate: CAGR = (end ÷ start)^(1 ÷ years) − 1. Add contributions made later to see the gain after all money paid in.

CAGR smooths ups and downs into one yearly rate. It ignores the timing of extra deposits; for those use an IRR calculator.

Como usar

  1. Enter start value, end value and years.

  2. Optionally add later contributions.

  3. Read gain, return and CAGR.

Perguntas frequentes

What is a good annual return?

Broad stock indices have averaged roughly 5–8% after inflation over long periods, with large swings. Past returns do not guarantee future ones.

Why is CAGR lower than the average of yearly returns?

Volatility drags compound growth below the arithmetic average.
