Como funciona Removedor de fundo liso (PNG transparente)

Upload a product photo on white, a logo on a coloured square or a green-screen shot and get a PNG in which that background colour is transparent. The tool detects the background from the border of the image, or you choose white, black or any colour, and sliders set the tolerance (how different a colour may be and still count as background) and the softness of the edge.

This is colour keying, not artificial intelligence. Each pixel's distance from the background colour is measured in RGB space; pixels closer than the tolerance become fully transparent, pixels in the soft-edge band become partly transparent, everything else is kept. It works very well for evenly lit plain backgrounds, and not at all for complex scenes, shadows on the background or subjects that contain the same colour. For people or objects on busy backgrounds you need a segmentation model, which this tool deliberately does not include so that it stays small, fast and private.

Como usar

  1. Drop images with a plain background.

  2. Choose automatic detection or a colour and set tolerance and softness.

  3. Download the transparent PNGs.

Perguntas frequentes

Why are parts of my subject transparent too?

They have a similar colour to the background. Lower the tolerance or choose a different background colour.

Can it remove a photo background with trees and people?

No. It only removes a colour. Complex backgrounds need an AI model.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.

Esta ferramenta é gratuita?

Sim, totalmente. Não há cadastro, plano pago nem marca-d'água nos seus arquivos.
