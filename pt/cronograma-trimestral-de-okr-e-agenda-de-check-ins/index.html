Como funciona Cronograma trimestral de OKR e agenda de check-ins

Pick the first day of the quarter, its length in weeks and the weekday for check-ins, and get a dated schedule: kickoff, one check-in every week, a mid-quarter review, grading and the retrospective on the last day. Copy the table into your calendar or planning document.

The schedule is calculated from the start date: the first check-in is on the chosen weekday of the first full week, the mid-quarter review is at the halfway week, grading is four days before the end, and the quarter ends after the chosen number of weeks (13 by default). It is a rhythm, not a methodology: adjust it to your company. Public holidays are not considered, so move a check-in if it falls on one.

Como usar

  1. Choose the quarter start, the length and the check-in weekday.

  2. Read the dated schedule.

  3. Copy it into your calendar.

Perguntas frequentes

How often should OKRs be checked?

Weekly short check-ins on confidence and blockers, a deeper review in the middle and a scoring session at the end.

What is a good OKR score?

About 0.7 for ambitious goals; 1.0 every time suggests the goals were too easy.

Meus arquivos são enviados?

Não. O arquivo é aberto e processado pelo seu próprio navegador. Você mesmo pode conferir: abra a aba de rede do navegador enquanto usa a ferramenta ou desconecte da internet depois que a página carregar.

Esta ferramenta é gratuita?

Sim, totalmente. Não há cadastro, plano pago nem marca-d'água nos seus arquivos.
