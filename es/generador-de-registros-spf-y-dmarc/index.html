Cómo funciona Generador de registros SPF y DMARC

Choose which services may send email for your domain (Google, SendGrid, your own servers, IP ranges) and how strictly receivers should treat everything else, and get a ready SPF record. Then pick a DMARC policy, a reporting address and alignment and get the DMARC record. Both come with the exact host name to create them under and with warnings about common mistakes.

SPF lists the allowed senders in a TXT record on the domain (v=spf1 … ~all) and may cause at most 10 DNS lookups (each include, mx and a counts). DMARC (v=DMARC1; p=…) at _dmarc.your-domain tells receivers to quarantine or reject mail that fails SPF and DKIM alignment, and the rua address collects aggregate reports. A safe roll-out is p=none with reports, then quarantine (optionally with pct), then reject. The records are built locally and the tool checks syntax only: it does not look up your current DNS, and DKIM keys must come from your mail provider.

Cómo usarla

  1. Enter your sending services and choose the SPF ending.

  2. Pick the DMARC policy and a report address.

  3. Create the two TXT records at your DNS provider.

Preguntas frecuentes

Can I have two SPF records?

No. A domain must have exactly one SPF record; merge all senders into it.

Which DMARC policy should I start with?

p=none with a rua report address, then move to quarantine and reject when the reports show only legitimate senders.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
