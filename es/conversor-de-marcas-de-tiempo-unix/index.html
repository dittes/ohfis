Cómo funciona Conversor de marcas de tiempo Unix

Logs, APIs and databases store moments as a number: seconds (1700000000) or milliseconds (1700000000000) since 1 January 1970 UTC. Paste one or many timestamps to see the date in UTC and in any time zone, with a relative description such as "3 years ago", or paste a date to get its timestamp. A live counter shows the current Unix time.

The unit is detected from the size of the number: under 10¹¹ it is seconds, up to 10¹⁴ milliseconds, up to 10¹⁷ microseconds and above that nanoseconds. Dates in ISO 8601 form (with Z or an offset such as +01:00) are parsed exactly; dates without an offset are treated as UTC. Time zone names use the IANA database built into your browser, so daylight saving time is applied correctly for the date in question. Negative timestamps (before 1970) work too.

Cómo usarla

  1. Paste one or more timestamps or dates, one per line.

  2. Choose the time zone to display.

  3. Copy the table or the single value you need.

Preguntas frecuentes

How do I know if a timestamp is in seconds or milliseconds?

A current timestamp in seconds has 10 digits; in milliseconds it has 13. The tool detects the unit automatically.

What is the year 2038 problem?

Systems that store seconds in a signed 32-bit integer overflow on 19 January 2038. This converter uses 64-bit numbers and is not affected.
