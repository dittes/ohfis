Cómo funciona Imagen a texto (OCR) – extraer texto de fotos y capturas

Drop a screenshot, a photo of a page or a scan and get the text to copy or download as .txt. Languages: English, German, Polish and Czech, alone or combined with English. A progress bar shows the recognition and the result lists the word count and the engine's confidence.

The recognition runs with Tesseract (an open-source OCR engine) compiled to WebAssembly, loaded from this website together with the language data, so your image never leaves the device. Small images are enlarged twice and placed on a white background before recognition, which improves accuracy for screenshots. Quality depends on the picture: straight, sharp, high-contrast printed text works well; low resolution, skewed photos, shadows, decorative fonts and handwriting do not. Always proofread numbers and names. For multi-page PDF scans use the OCR PDF tool.

Cómo usarla

  1. Choose the language of the text.

  2. Drop or choose the image.

  3. Wait for the recognition, then copy or download the text.

Preguntas frecuentes

Is the image uploaded?

No. Recognition runs in your browser; the engine and language files come from this site.

Why are there mistakes?

Blur, low resolution, shadows or unusual fonts reduce accuracy. Retake the picture straight on and in good light.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
