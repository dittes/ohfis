Cómo funciona Creador de análisis DAFO (SWOT)

A SWOT analysis sorts what matters for a plan into four boxes: internal strengths and weaknesses, external opportunities and threats. Type one point per line in each box and the tool shows the familiar 2 × 2 grid with a title, ready to copy as Markdown or to print.

Keep each point short and concrete, and ask of every weakness and threat: what could we do about it? Use the printed grid in a workshop, or paste the Markdown into a document or slide.

Cómo usarla

  1. Enter a title for the analysis.

  2. Write one point per line in each of the four boxes.

  3. Copy the Markdown or print the grid.

Preguntas frecuentes

What is the difference between strengths and opportunities?

Strengths are inside your organisation and under your control. Opportunities come from outside, such as markets, trends or partners.

How many points should each box have?

Three to five of the most important ones work best. Long lists hide the priorities.
