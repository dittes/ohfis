Cómo funciona Paleta de colores a partir de una imagen

Build a colour scheme from a logo, photo or product shot. The tool finds the main colours of your picture, shows each with its HEX code and how much of the image it covers, and copies them as a list or as CSS custom properties. In Chrome and Edge an eyedropper can also sample any pixel on your screen.

The image is scaled down to at most 160 pixels wide, each pixel is quantised and counted, and the most representative colours are found with a weighted k-means clustering that starts from well-separated colours (farthest-point initialisation) and then moves each centre to the weighted average of its group. Share is the fraction of pixels that belong to each colour. Transparent pixels are ignored. The result depends on the number of colours you ask for.

Cómo usarla

  1. Drop an image.

  2. Choose how many colours you want.

  3. Click a colour to copy its HEX code, or copy the whole palette.

Preguntas frecuentes

How are the colours chosen?

By clustering similar pixels and averaging each group, weighted by how many pixels it holds.

Can I pick a colour from anywhere on screen?

In Chrome and Edge, yes, with the eyedropper button.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
