Cómo funciona Calculadora de diferencia entre fechas

How long until the deadline, how old is the contract, how many days since the incident? Enter two dates and see the span as "2 years, 3 months, 5 days", as total days, weeks, hours and minutes, and as business days (Monday to Friday).

Months are counted on the calendar: starting from the earlier date, whole months are added with the day clamped to the end of shorter months (31 January plus one month is 29 February in a leap year), and the remaining days are what is left over. This avoids the errors of dividing by 30. Business days count every Monday to Friday between the dates; use the Working Days Calculator when public holidays matter.

Cómo usarla

  1. Pick the start and end date.

  2. Read the difference in the units you need.

  3. For holidays and custom weekends, switch to the Working Days Calculator.

Preguntas frecuentes

Is the end date included?

No. The difference between 1 and 2 January is 1 day. Add one if you want to count both days.

Why is a month not always 30 days?

Calendar months have 28 to 31 days, so the tool counts whole calendar months and then the leftover days.
