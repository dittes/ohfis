Cómo funciona Comparador de comisiones de procesamiento de pagos

Find the cheapest way to get paid. Enter a typical payment and how many you receive per month; the table ranks processors by fee, shows what you actually receive, the effective rate and the yearly cost, and the amount you would need to charge to receive exactly 100.

Each processor charges a percentage plus a fixed fee: fee = amount × % + fixed. The effective rate is higher for small payments because the fixed part weighs more. The "charge to receive 100" column grosses the amount up: (100 + fixed) ÷ (1 − %). The prefilled rates are public list prices and are only a starting point: edit the list to match your own contract, card mix and currency.

Cómo usarla

  1. Enter a typical payment amount and monthly volume.

  2. Edit the processor list to your actual rates.

  3. Compare effective rates and yearly cost.

Preguntas frecuentes

Why is the effective rate higher on small payments?

The fixed fee (for example 0.30) is a large share of a 5 payment.

Are these the current prices?

They are list prices from 2024. Fees change; use your own contract rates.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
