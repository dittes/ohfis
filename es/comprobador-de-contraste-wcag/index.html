Cómo funciona Comprobador de contraste WCAG

Check whether your text is readable for people with low vision. Enter a text colour and a background colour in any CSS notation and see the contrast ratio, a live sample and pass or fail for AA and AAA at normal and large text size and for UI components. If the pair fails, a nearby text colour that passes AA is suggested.

The ratio follows WCAG 2.1: relative luminance L = 0.2126 R + 0.7152 G + 0.0722 B after converting each sRGB channel to linear light, and contrast = (L1 + 0.05) ÷ (L2 + 0.05) for the lighter and darker colour. AA needs 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text (24 px, or 18.66 px bold) and for graphical objects and interface components; AAA needs 7:1 and 4.5:1. The suggestion keeps your hue and saturation and moves the lightness the smallest distance that reaches 4.5:1. Colours with transparency are not blended with the background.

Cómo usarla

  1. Enter the text and background colours.

  2. Read the ratio and the five pass/fail results.

  3. Use the suggested colour if your pair fails.

Preguntas frecuentes

What contrast ratio is required?

WCAG AA requires 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text and UI components. AAA requires 7:1 and 4.5:1.

Is white text on #2557D6 accessible?

Yes: the ratio is above 6:1, so it passes AA for all text sizes.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
