Cómo funciona Contador de caja y cuadre de fondo

Enter how many of each note and coin are in the till or petty-cash box, and the tool adds them up, compares the total with the amount you expected (opening float plus sales) and shows whether the drawer is over, short or balanced. You can copy the count as a short report for your records.

Amounts are calculated in whole cents (integers), which avoids the floating-point rounding errors that make 0.1 + 0.2 differ from 0.3. Denomination lists are included for US dollar, euro, pound sterling, Czech koruna, Polish złoty and Swiss franc. The tool counts what you type: it cannot find the reason for a difference, so recount first, then check the receipts and refunds. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.

Cómo usarla

  1. Choose the currency and enter the expected amount.

  2. Enter the number of each note and coin.

  3. Read the difference and copy the report.

Preguntas frecuentes

What should I do if the drawer is short?

Recount, check the day's refunds, voids and petty-cash slips and note the difference in your cash book; repeated differences point to a process problem.

Is the count saved?

No. Copy the report before you close the page.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
