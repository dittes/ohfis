Generador de calendario de sprints

Organiza fechas de sprints, días de planificación y revisiones para un trimestre o un año.

Cómo funciona Generador de calendario de sprints

Enter the first sprint start and the sprint length in working days, and get a calendar of sprints with the planning day at the start, review and retrospective on the last day and any public holidays inside each sprint listed for reference. Copy it into a roadmap or download it as CSV.

Sprints are built from working days only (Monday to Friday): a 10-day sprint starting on a Monday ends on the Friday of the following week, and the next sprint starts on the next working day. Public holidays of the chosen country are listed per sprint so the team can adjust scope. Holidays do not automatically shorten a sprint, because teams handle them differently.

Cómo usarla

  1. Enter the start date, sprint length and number of sprints.

  2. Optionally choose a country to list public holidays.

  3. Copy the table or download it as CSV.

Preguntas frecuentes

How long should a sprint be?

Two weeks is the most common length; the Scrum Guide allows one month at most.

Can I generate a whole year?

Set the number of sprints to 26 for two-week sprints.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
