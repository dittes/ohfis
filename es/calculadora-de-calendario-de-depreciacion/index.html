Cómo funciona Calculadora de calendario de depreciación

Enter the cost, the residual value and the useful life, and get a year-by-year schedule with the expense, the accumulated depreciation and the book value, plus a chart. Choose straight line, declining balance (double or another factor) or the sum-of-the-years' digits method.

Straight line = (cost − salvage) ÷ life. Declining balance = book value × factor ÷ life each year (factor 2 = double declining), never reducing the book value below salvage and writing off the remainder in the last year. Sum-of-the-years' digits = (cost − salvage) × (life − year + 1) ÷ (life × (life + 1) ÷ 2). Tax depreciation follows national tables (MACRS, AfA, etc.) that can differ, so confirm with your accountant.

Cómo usarla

  1. Enter cost, salvage value and useful life.

  2. Choose the method.

  3. Copy the schedule or download it as CSV.

Preguntas frecuentes

Which method is best?

Straight line is simplest. Accelerated methods write off more early, matching assets that lose value quickly.

What is salvage value?

The estimated value of the asset at the end of its useful life.
