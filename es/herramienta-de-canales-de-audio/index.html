Cómo funciona Herramienta de canales de audio (silenciar, intercambiar, invertir, panoramizar, mono)

Fix common problems with stereo files in one step: mix a recording down to mono, swap left and right, silence one channel (for example when a microphone was only recorded on one side), invert the polarity of one or both channels to check phase problems, or place a mono signal anywhere between left and right.

Mono mixdown averages the channels. Swap exchanges them. Muting writes silence to one channel. Polarity inversion multiplies the samples by −1: a signal and its inverse cancel each other when played together, which is the classic test for two speakers wired out of phase. Panning uses an equal-power law (left gain = cos, right gain = sin of the pan angle), so the perceived loudness stays constant across the stereo field. Processing is lossless on the decoded audio and the result is saved as a 44.1 kHz WAV.

Preguntas frecuentes

When should I invert polarity?

To test or fix phase problems, for example two microphones on one source that cancel each other.

Why do I hear only one side?

The recording may have audio on one channel only. Use "Mix down to mono" to hear it on both sides.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
