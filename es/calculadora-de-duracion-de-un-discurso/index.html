Cómo funciona Calculadora de duración de un discurso

Paste your talk, toast or video script to see how long it takes to deliver. The calculator shows three tiers: slow (110 words per minute, for formal speeches and nervous speakers), natural (150, typical conversation and presentations) and energetic (180, fast, enthusiastic delivery). You can also enter your own pace.

The words are counted with a Unicode-aware tokenizer, divided by the words-per-minute rate, and converted into minutes and seconds. A pause setting adds a number of seconds after every paragraph break, which makes the estimate more realistic for speeches with dramatic pauses, slide changes or questions to the audience.

Cómo usarla

  1. Paste your script.

  2. Optionally set your own speaking pace and the pause after each paragraph.

  3. Use the times to trim or extend the text to fit your slot.

Preguntas frecuentes

How many words per minute is a normal speaking speed?

About 130 to 160 words per minute for English presentations. Audiobooks are around 150, auctioneers well above 250.

How many words fit into a 5-minute speech?

Roughly 750 words at a natural pace of 150 words per minute.
