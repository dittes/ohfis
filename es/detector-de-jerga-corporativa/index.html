Cómo funciona Detector de jerga corporativa

"Let's circle back and leverage our core competencies." Paste an email, report or job post and the tool highlights over 50 overused business phrases, shows how many there are per 100 words and suggests a plain replacement for each one ("circle back" → "follow up").

Matching is done with whole-word patterns that also catch simple endings, so "leverage", "leverages" and "leveraging" are all found. Multi-word phrases and hyphenated words are matched as units. The dictionary lives in your browser; nothing you paste is uploaded. It is a style aid, not a verdict: sometimes jargon is the right word for a specialist audience.

Cómo usarla

  1. Paste your text.

  2. Read the highlighted phrases and the replacement table.

  3. Rewrite the passages that sound vague.

Preguntas frecuentes

Is jargon always bad?

Not for a specialist audience that shares the vocabulary. The tool helps you notice the phrases that make general writing vague.

Can I add my own words?

Not yet. The list covers the most common business clichés.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
