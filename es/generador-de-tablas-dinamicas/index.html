Cómo funciona Generador de tablas dinámicas

Summarise a long list of records into a compact table. Choose a column to group by (rows), optionally a column for the headings across (columns) and a value column; then pick sum, average, count, distinct count, minimum, maximum or median. With both rows and columns chosen you get a cross-tab with totals.

The tool groups the rows into a map keyed by the row field (and column field), collects the values of each group and reduces them with the chosen function. Numbers are read from text, ignoring non-numeric cells for sums and averages. The groups are sorted naturally, so "Q2" comes before "Q10". The result is a normal table you can copy or download.

Cómo usarla

  1. Paste your CSV or open a file.

  2. Choose the row field, an optional column field, the value column and the calculation.

  3. Copy the pivot table or download it as CSV.

Preguntas frecuentes

Is this the same as an Excel pivot table?

It covers the most common case: grouping by one or two fields with one calculation. For slicers and calculated fields use Excel itself.

What does "count" count?

"Count rows" counts every record in the group, "Count non-empty" ignores blank cells and "Distinct count" counts unique values.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
