Cómo funciona Efectos de imagen: blanco y negro, duotono e invertido

Apply one effect to one or many images: grayscale, a duotone with two colours of your choice, sepia, an inverted negative, a dark-mode preview that keeps hues, or a hard black-and-white threshold for scans. Brightness and contrast sliders work with every effect. Results are listed with a preview and can be downloaded one by one or as a ZIP.

Each image is drawn on a canvas and the pixels are processed in your browser. Grayscale uses the luminance weights 0.2126 R + 0.7152 G + 0.0722 B; duotone maps that luminance linearly between your shadow and highlight colours; sepia uses the classic colour matrix; invert subtracts each channel from 255; the dark-mode preview inverts and then rotates the hue by 180° so reds stay red-ish. Threshold sets each pixel to black or white at the chosen level, which is useful for cleaning up text scans before OCR. Transparent areas keep their alpha channel.

Cómo usarla

  1. Drop one or more images.

  2. Choose an effect and adjust the sliders.

  3. Download the images or the ZIP.

Preguntas frecuentes

What is a duotone?

An image whose brightness range is mapped between two colours, a dark one for shadows and a light one for highlights.

Does the original stay unchanged?

Yes. Results are new files; originals are never modified or uploaded.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
