Difuminar y tapar partes de una imagen

Pixela, difumina o tapa en negro zonas sensibles de una captura de pantalla o foto.

Cómo funciona Difuminar y tapar partes de una imagen

Hide names, faces, account numbers or tokens before you share a screenshot. Drag a rectangle over the area and choose pixelate, blur or a solid black box; undo as many steps as you like and download the result as a PNG.

The effect is applied to the pixel data of a canvas: pixelation replaces each block with its average colour, blur runs three passes of a box blur (which approximates a Gaussian blur), and the black box overwrites the pixels completely. Be aware that weak blur and pixelation can sometimes be partly reversed with software, especially on text with large letters; for passwords, ID numbers and anything truly sensitive use the black box. The exported PNG is re-created from pixels and contains no metadata from the original.

Cómo usarla

  1. Drop your image.

  2. Choose the effect and strength, then drag over each area to hide.

  3. Download the redacted PNG.

Preguntas frecuentes

Is blurring safe for passwords or account numbers?

Use the black box. Light blur and pixelation can sometimes be reversed or guessed, black pixels cannot.

Is the original uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
