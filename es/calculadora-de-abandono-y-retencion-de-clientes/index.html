Cómo funciona Calculadora de abandono y retención de clientes

A monthly churn of 4% sounds small, but it means almost 40% of customers leave within a year. Enter your customers, revenue per customer, monthly churn and new customers per month to see how the base and the monthly revenue develop, and how long it takes to lose half of a cohort.

Each month customers = customers × (1 − churn) + new customers, and revenue per customer grows by the expansion rate. The survival of a cohort after 12 months is (1 − churn)^12, so annual churn = 1 − (1 − monthly churn)^12; the half-life is ln 0.5 ÷ ln(1 − churn). The chart shows whether sign-ups outpace losses.

Preguntas frecuentes

How do I convert monthly churn to annual churn?

1 − (1 − monthly)^12. For 4% monthly it is 38.8% a year.

What is a good churn rate?

For B2B SaaS under 1–2% monthly, for consumer subscriptions 5% or more is common.
