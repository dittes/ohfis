Cómo funciona Generador de favicon e iconos de aplicación

Upload one square logo and download the whole icon set a modern website needs: a multi-size favicon.ico (16, 32 and 48 px), PNG icons for browsers and Android (16 to 512 px), the 180 px Apple touch icon, a web app manifest and the HTML tags to paste into your page. A ZIP contains everything.

Each size is drawn from your image with high-quality scaling, optional padding and optional rounded corners; the Apple icon always gets a solid background because iOS does not support transparency. The .ico is a container holding PNG images of 16, 32 and 48 px (the format supported by all current browsers), assembled in your browser from the ICO structure: a header, one directory entry per image and the PNG data. For the sharpest result start from an SVG or a PNG of at least 512 × 512 px.

Cómo usarla

  1. Drop a square logo.

  2. Adjust padding, background and rounded corners.

  3. Download the ZIP and paste the HTML into your <head>.

Preguntas frecuentes

Which sizes do I really need?

favicon.ico (or a 32 px PNG), a 180 px Apple touch icon and 192 / 512 px icons for the manifest cover practically all devices.

Why is my Apple icon background filled?

iOS shows transparent areas as black, so the tool adds a solid background to that one icon.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
