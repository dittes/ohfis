Cómo funciona Generador de memes (texto arriba y abajo)

Choose a picture, type a top and a bottom line and get the familiar white text with black outline. Text size, colours and capital letters can be changed, long lines wrap automatically, and the finished meme downloads as PNG or JPEG without a watermark.

The text is drawn on a canvas with a heavy font (Impact if your system has it, otherwise Arial Black or a bold sans-serif), outlined first and then filled so that it stays readable on any background. Lines are wrapped to 94 % of the picture width and positioned from the top and the bottom edge. The picture is scaled to at most 1600 px wide for the result. Nothing is uploaded. Make sure you have the right to use and share the picture you choose, and think about the people shown in it.

Cómo usarla

  1. Choose the image.

  2. Type the top and bottom text and adjust the style.

  3. Download the meme.

Preguntas frecuentes

Is there a watermark?

No. The picture and text are processed on your device and the result is clean.

Which font is used?

Impact when available, otherwise a similar bold font. Fonts depend on your system.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
