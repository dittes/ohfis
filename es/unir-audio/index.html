Cómo funciona Unir audio (combinar archivos MP3 en línea)

Add two or more audio files, put them in the right order with the arrow buttons and download one combined file as WAV or MP3. A crossfade of a few hundred milliseconds lets one track flow into the next, for example for a mix or a podcast with intro and outro music.

All files are decoded at 44.1 kHz by your browser, so tracks with different sample rates join without changing the pitch; a mono track is widened to stereo when it is mixed with stereo tracks. The crossfade uses an equal-power curve, which keeps the loudness steady across the transition. Joining happens in memory, so very long recordings (hours) need a computer with enough RAM.

Cómo usarla

  1. Add the audio files.

  2. Order them with ↑ and ↓ and remove any you do not need.

  3. Set a crossfade if you like, listen, and download the result.

Preguntas frecuentes

How do I get a smooth transition?

Set a crossfade of 200–2000 ms. 0 ms joins the tracks without overlap.

Is there a limit on the number of files?

No fixed limit, but everything is processed in your browser, so the practical limit is your computer’s memory.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
