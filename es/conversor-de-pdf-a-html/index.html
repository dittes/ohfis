Cómo funciona Conversor de PDF a HTML

Drop a PDF and get clean, simple HTML: headings are detected from the font size, lines are joined into paragraphs, bullet and numbered lines become lists and words broken at the end of a line with a hyphen are put back together. Copy the HTML or download it as a complete page, with page breaks marked if you like.

The text and its position come from pdf.js, the same engine Firefox uses. The converter groups the text by baseline, takes the most common font size as body text and treats larger lines as headings. It does not rebuild the page layout: tables, multi-column layouts, images and exact fonts are not reproduced, and a scanned PDF has no text at all and needs OCR first. For a faithful visual copy, use PDF to JPG or PNG instead.

Cómo usarla

  1. Drop the PDF.

  2. Choose headings, page breaks and a full page or just the markup.

  3. Check the preview, then copy or download the HTML.

Preguntas frecuentes

Are images and tables kept?

No. Only the text structure is converted. Tables come out as lines of text.

The result is empty.

The PDF is probably a scan without a text layer. Run it through OCR PDF first.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
