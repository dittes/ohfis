Cómo funciona Mapa de empatía (notas adhesivas)

An empathy map puts you in the shoes of one customer or user. Write what this person sees, hears, says and does, what they think and feel, and what hurts them (pains) and what they want (gains). Use real observations from interviews instead of assumptions, and give the board the name of the persona.

The board is saved in this browser and nothing is uploaded; export it as PNG or Markdown for your product team. The empathy map goes back to Dave Gray and XPLANE; only the general idea is used here, with our own layout and wording.

Cómo usarla

  1. Name the person or persona in the board name.

  2. Fill the boxes with observations from interviews and research.

  3. Look for patterns in the pains and gains and export the board.

Preguntas frecuentes

Should I fill it alone?

Better with a team and with real interview notes. Alone it mostly records your own assumptions.

One map or many?

One per customer segment or persona. Different people have different pains.

Is it saved?

In this browser only; use "Save as file" for a backup.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
