Cómo funciona Formateador de números de teléfono (E.164)

A contact list with (415) 555-0132, 0049 30 1234567 and +49 (0)30 1234567 cannot be imported into most CRMs and SMS tools. This tool rewrites every number in a column to E.164, the international standard (+14155550132), or to a readable international, national (US) or digits-only style, and flags the numbers it cannot read.

Numbers with + or 00 keep their country code. For numbers without one, the default country you choose adds its code and removes the national trunk prefix (the leading 0 in Germany, the UK or France; not in the US, Poland or the Czech Republic). Extensions and the "(0)" convention are stripped, and the digit count must be 8–15 (US/Canada: exactly 10 after +1). The tool checks format only; it cannot tell whether a number is in service. 32 countries are supported.

Cómo usarla

  1. Paste your CSV and choose the phone column.

  2. Pick the default country and output style.

  3. Download the cleaned list; the "valid" column shows numbers that need a look.

Preguntas frecuentes

What is E.164?

The ITU standard format: a plus sign, the country code and the national number, at most 15 digits, with no spaces or punctuation. Most SMS and telephony services require it.

Why is a number marked invalid?

It has too few or too many digits for its country, or contains letters. Fix it by hand or remove it.
