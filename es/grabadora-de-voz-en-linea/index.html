Cómo funciona Grabadora de voz en línea (con recorte)

Press record, speak, press stop. A level meter shows whether the microphone picks you up, a timer shows the length and, when you are done, you can play the recording, cut it to the part you want and download it as WAV, or keep the original browser recording (WebM/OGG/M4A).

The recording uses MediaRecorder on the microphone stream with echo cancellation and noise suppression switched off so that you get the raw signal; the level meter reads an analyser node. After you stop, the file is decoded to measure its peak and to make an exact-length WAV export for the trim function. The audio exists only in memory in this tab: nothing is uploaded, and it is gone when you close the page unless you download it. The browser asks for microphone permission; the microphone is released when you press Stop.

Cómo usarla

  1. Allow the microphone and press Record.

  2. Press Stop and listen to the result.

  3. Set start and end to trim, then download.

Preguntas frecuentes

Is my recording sent anywhere?

No. It stays in your browser until you download it.

Why is the recording WebM and not MP3?

Browsers record in their own formats. The trimmed WAV works almost everywhere; MP3 encoding is not built in.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
