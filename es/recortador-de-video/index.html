Recortador de vídeo (cortar vídeo en línea)

Corta un vídeo a la parte que necesitas, con o sin recodificación.

Cómo funciona Recortador de vídeo (cortar vídeo en línea)

Drop a video, play it, pause where the part you want starts and ends, press "use current position" and save the trimmed video. Fast mode copies the streams without re-encoding, so even a long video is cut in seconds without any loss of quality; the cut then snaps to the nearest keyframe. Normal mode re-encodes and is accurate to the frame.

The video is processed by FFmpeg, the standard open-source video tool, compiled to WebAssembly and running inside your browser. The first use downloads the engine (about 31 MB), which your browser then keeps. Nothing is uploaded. Everything happens in your browser’s memory, so very large videos (several gigabytes) or long 4K clips can fail on a phone or an older computer; try a shorter or smaller clip in that case.

Cómo usarla

  1. Drop the video and play it.

  2. Set start and end (or use the current position of the player).

  3. Choose fast or accurate mode and press "Trim video".

Preguntas frecuentes

Why is the cut a bit off in fast mode?

Without re-encoding, a video can only start at a keyframe, which usually comes every one to five seconds. Switch fast mode off for frame-accurate cuts.

Which formats can I open?

MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, AVI and most phone and camera formats. The result is always MP4 (H.264).

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
