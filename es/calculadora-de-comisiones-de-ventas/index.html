Cómo funciona Calculadora de comisiones de ventas

Enter the sales achieved, the quota and your commission bands, and see the payout, the quota attainment and a band-by-band breakdown. Typical plans pay a base rate up to quota and accelerators above it.

Each band applies to the part of the sales between its lower and upper limit, expressed as a percentage of quota, like income tax brackets: with bands 0–100% at 5% and 100–150% at 8%, sales of 130% of a 100,000 quota pay 100,000 × 5% + 30,000 × 8% = 7,400. The last band can be open-ended (*). The effective rate shows the average rate on all sales.

Cómo usarla

  1. Enter sales and quota.

  2. Define the bands as "up to % of quota, rate %".

  3. Read the commission and the breakdown.

Preguntas frecuentes

What is an accelerator?

A higher commission rate on sales above quota, to reward over-performance.

Are bands applied to all sales or only the part inside?

Only to the part inside the band, like tax brackets.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
