Cómo funciona Vista previa de Open Graph y redes sociales

Fill in the title, description, URL and an image and see approximately how the shared link will look on Facebook, X (Twitter), LinkedIn and Slack or Discord. The page also writes the matching <title>, description, canonical, Open Graph and Twitter card tags, and warns when text will be cut off.

Open Graph (og:title, og:description, og:image, og:url) is read by most platforms when a link is pasted; X additionally uses twitter:card. Titles are best at 60 characters or less and descriptions at about 70–160 characters; images 1200 × 630 pixels (1.91:1). The preview image you choose here is only shown locally and never uploaded: the image URL for the tag must point to a file on your own site. Platforms cache previews, so use their debugger tools after publishing. The previews are approximations: each platform changes its layout from time to time.

Cómo usarla

  1. Enter title, description, URL and site name.

  2. Choose a preview image and enter the public image URL for the tag.

  3. Copy the HTML into your page's <head>.

Preguntas frecuentes

What size should the Open Graph image be?

1200 × 630 pixels, under about 5 MB, in JPG or PNG.

Why does my old preview still show?

Platforms cache link previews. Use the Facebook Sharing Debugger or LinkedIn Post Inspector to refresh.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
