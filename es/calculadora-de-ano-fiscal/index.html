Cómo funciona Calculadora de año fiscal (trimestre y período fiscal)

Choose the month in which your fiscal year starts and a date, and get the fiscal year, the fiscal quarter, the start and end of that fiscal year and how far into it you are. A table maps every calendar month to its fiscal quarter and period. Presets exist for the US federal government, the UK and India, Australia and the plain calendar year.

The fiscal year containing a date starts in the start month of the same year if the date is on or after it, otherwise in the previous year. The year label depends on convention: you can name it after the calendar year in which it starts (FY2025 = April 2025 to March 2026) or in which it ends (US federal FY2025 = October 2024 to September 2025). Quarters are consecutive blocks of three months from the start month. 4-4-5 retail calendars with 52-week years are a different system and are not covered here.

Cómo usarla

  1. Choose the first month of the fiscal year and the naming convention.

  2. Enter the date.

  3. Read the fiscal year, quarter and the month map.

Preguntas frecuentes

What is the US federal fiscal year?

October 1 to September 30, named after the year in which it ends.

Why does my company show a different year label?

Naming conventions vary. Use the "starts" or "ends" option to match yours.
