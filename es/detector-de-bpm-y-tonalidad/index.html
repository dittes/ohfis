Cómo funciona Detector de BPM y tonalidad (tempo y tonalidad de una canción)

Drop a song or a recording and get its tempo in beats per minute, the half and double tempo, other candidates, the musical key (such as A minor) and the Camelot code used by DJs for harmonic mixing. Useful to match tracks, to practise along or to label a sample library.

The tempo is found from the rhythm of the sound: the onsets are measured and their regular spacing is found by autocorrelation, with a preference for common tempos. The key is found from the average strength of the twelve notes over time, compared with the typical profiles of major and minor keys (Krumhansl–Schmuckler). Both work best on music with a clear steady beat and a stable key. Speech, ambient music and songs that change tempo or key give unreliable results, and half or double the tempo is a common ambiguity. For tapping the tempo yourself use the BPM Tap Counter.

Cómo usarla

  1. Drop the audio file.

  2. Wait a moment while it is analysed (up to 3 minutes from the middle of the song).

  3. Read the tempo, key and the alternatives.

Preguntas frecuentes

Why is the tempo double or half of what I expected?

Beats can be counted at different levels. Check the "half / double" values; the right one is usually obvious when you tap along.

How reliable is the key?

Good for clear tonal music. The "key certainty" line says how far the best key is ahead of the second best.

¿Se suben mis archivos?
