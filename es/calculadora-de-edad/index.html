Cómo funciona Calculadora de edad

Enter a date of birth and (optionally) another date to find the exact age, the total number of days, weeks and months lived, and the days until the next birthday. Leave the second date as today to see your age now.

Months and days are counted calendar-wise: from 15 June to 5 November is 4 months and 21 days. Nothing is stored or sent.

Cómo usarla

  1. Enter the date of birth.

  2. Leave "age at" as today or pick another date.

  3. Read the result.

Preguntas frecuentes

How is the age counted on 29 February?

Years are counted from the calendar date; in non-leap years the birthday falls on 1 March in many countries and on 28 February in others.

Can I calculate age at a past date?

Yes, set "age at" to that date.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
