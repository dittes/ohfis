Cómo funciona Cambiar la velocidad del vídeo (cámara lenta y avance rápido)

Enter a speed from 0.1× to 20×: 0.5 plays at half speed (slow motion), 2 at double speed, 8 makes a timelapse out of a long recording. The sound is adjusted to the same speed without changing its pitch.

The picture timing is changed with FFmpeg’s setpts filter and the sound with chained atempo filters (each stage only handles factors between 0.5 and 2, so extreme speeds use several). Slow motion cannot invent new frames, so a very slow clip can look choppy. The video is processed by FFmpeg, the standard open-source video tool, compiled to WebAssembly and running inside your browser. The first use downloads the engine (about 31 MB), which your browser then keeps. Nothing is uploaded. Everything happens in your browser’s memory, so very large videos (several gigabytes) or long 4K clips can fail on a phone or an older computer; try a shorter or smaller clip in that case.

Cómo usarla

  1. Drop the video.

  2. Enter the speed (below 1 = slower, above 1 = faster).

  3. Press "Change speed" and download.

Preguntas frecuentes

Does the voice sound like a chipmunk?

No. The pitch is kept, so voices stay natural at moderate speeds; at extreme speeds speech becomes hard to understand.

Can I change the speed of only part of a video?

Cut the part with the Video Trimmer, change its speed here and join the pieces with the Video Merger.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
