Cómo funciona Business Model Canvas (interactivo, con notas adhesivas)

The Business Model Canvas describes a business on one page in nine blocks: customer segments, value propositions, channels, customer relationships, revenue streams, key resources, key activities, key partners and cost structure. Add a sticky note to each block, edit it in place, change its colour and drag notes between the blocks while you discuss the model with your team.

Your board is saved in this browser and nothing is uploaded. When you are done, download the canvas as a PNG for a slide, copy it as Markdown for a document, or save it as a file to continue later. The Business Model Canvas was created by Alexander Osterwalder and Yves Pigneur (Strategyzer) and is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike; this board is an adapted, re-drawn version and says so below the canvas.

Cómo usarla

  1. Start with the customer segments and the value propositions, then work through the other blocks.

  2. Add one idea per sticky note; drag notes to other blocks, recolour or delete them.

  3. Download the PNG, copy the Markdown or save the board as a file.

Preguntas frecuentes

Is the Business Model Canvas free to use?

Yes. Strategyzer released the canvas under a Creative Commons licence that allows use and adaptation with credit and under the same licence. The credit is shown below the board and inside the PNG.

Where is my work stored?

Only in this browser (local storage). Use "Save as file" to keep a copy or to move it to another device.

Can my team edit it together?

Not live, because there is no server. Work on one screen in the workshop, or send the saved file around.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
