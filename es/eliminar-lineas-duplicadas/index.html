Cómo funciona Eliminar líneas duplicadas

Clean a mailing list, a keyword export or a log file by keeping only the first occurrence of every line. Options let you ignore upper and lower case, trim stray spaces and drop empty lines, so "Apple", "apple " and "APPLE" can count as one entry.

Each line is normalised according to your options and stored in a Map keyed by that value, which is why even lists with hundreds of thousands of lines finish instantly. The tool can keep the original order, sort by how often a line occurred, or sort A–Z, and it can print the count next to each line, turning the page into a frequency counter for lists.

Cómo usarla

  1. Paste your list, one item per line.

  2. Choose whether case and spaces should be ignored, and how to order the result.

  3. Copy the unique lines. Tick "Show how often" for counts.

Preguntas frecuentes

Does it keep the first or the last duplicate?

The first occurrence is kept, in its original spelling, so the order of your list is preserved.

How many lines can it handle?

Hundreds of thousands of lines work well in a modern browser. Everything runs on your device.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
