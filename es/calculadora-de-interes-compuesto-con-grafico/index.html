Cómo funciona Calculadora de interés compuesto con gráfico

See how a starting amount and regular deposits grow over time and how much of the final balance is interest. The chart compares your balance with the money you paid in, and the table gives the figures for every year, ready to copy as CSV.

Each month the balance grows by the monthly equivalent of the annual rate, (1 + r/n)^(n/12) − 1, where n is the number of compounding periods per year, and the monthly contribution is added at the end (or start) of the month. For a single lump sum this equals the textbook formula A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt). Results are nominal: taxes, fees and inflation are not deducted.

Cómo usarla

  1. Enter the starting amount, monthly contribution, rate and years.

  2. Choose how often interest is compounded.

  3. Read the final balance and chart, and copy the table.

Preguntas frecuentes

How much is 10,000 at 7% after 30 years?

About 76,123 with yearly compounding, 81,165 with monthly compounding.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Real investments fluctuate; use a conservative rate and see the effect of fees.
