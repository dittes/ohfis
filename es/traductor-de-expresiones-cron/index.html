Cómo funciona Traductor de expresiones cron

What does "30 4 1,15 * 5" do? Paste a crontab schedule and read it in plain English, then check the next dates it will run. Presets cover common schedules such as every 5 minutes, weekdays at 9:00 or the first of every month.

The five fields (minute, hour, day of month, month, day of week) are parsed with support for lists (1,15), ranges (1-5), steps (*/10, 1-30/5), month and weekday names and the @daily-style aliases. Invalid values are reported with the reason. Next runs are found by stepping forward minute by minute and testing each field. Like real cron, when both the day-of-month and the day-of-week field are restricted, a job runs when either matches, a frequent source of surprises that the description points out. Times are shown in UTC or your local time, so you can check them against your server's time zone.

Cómo usarla

  1. Type or pick a cron expression.

  2. Read the plain-English description.

  3. Check the list of upcoming runs, in UTC or local time.

Preguntas frecuentes

What do the five fields mean?

Minute (0–59), hour (0–23), day of month (1–31), month (1–12) and day of week (0–6, Sunday = 0).

Why does "0 0 13 * 5" run on the 13th AND on Fridays?

When both day fields are restricted, standard cron runs the job if either one matches.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
