Comprobador de tono pasivo-agresivo para correos

Resalta frases que culpabilizan, piden disculpas en exceso o son pasivo-agresivas, con alternativas más amables.

Cómo funciona Comprobador de tono pasivo-agresivo para correos

Before you press send, paste your email or chat message and see which phrases may sound passive-aggressive ("as per my last email"), blaming ("you never"), guilt-tripping, condescending ("obviously"), vague ("ASAP") or over-apologetic. Each flagged phrase comes with a plain suggestion, and messages with too many apologies, exclamation marks or capitals get a gentle note.

This is a transparent rule-based checker, not an AI model: it matches a built-in list of about 40 phrases (whole words, case-insensitive) and counts a few signals such as the number of "sorry", exclamation marks and ALL-CAPS words. Because nothing is sent to a server, the message stays private, but the tool cannot understand context, sarcasm or relationships, and it will miss anything that is not on its list. Use the highlights as prompts to reread the message from the other person's point of view.

Cómo usarla

  1. Paste your draft message.

  2. Read the highlighted phrases and the suggestions.

  3. Rewrite what could land badly, then send.

Preguntas frecuentes

Is this an AI that understands my message?

No. It is a fixed list of phrases and simple counts, which makes it fast and private but limited.

Is every flagged phrase bad?

No. Context matters: "friendly reminder" can be perfectly fine. The tool only points them out.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
