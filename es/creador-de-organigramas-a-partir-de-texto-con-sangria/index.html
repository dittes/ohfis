Cómo funciona Creador de organigramas a partir de texto con sangría

Type who reports to whom with indentation, optionally as "Name, Title", and get a tidy organisation chart you can download as PNG or SVG for a slide, a wiki page or an onboarding document.

The text is turned into a tree by comparing indentation levels. A tidy-tree layout then places leaves side by side, centres every parent above its children and keeps equal spacing between boxes on the same level, with right-angled connector lines. The chart is drawn as SVG, which stays sharp at any size, and the PNG export renders that SVG at double resolution. Several top-level lines produce a forest of independent trees. Everything is created in your browser, so staff lists stay private.

Cómo usarla

  1. Type the structure; indent with spaces or a tab for direct reports.

  2. Adjust the box width and colour.

  3. Download the chart as PNG or SVG.

Preguntas frecuentes

How do I show two lines in a box?

Write "Name, Title". The part before the first comma is the bold first line.

Can I have several top managers?

Yes. Start several lines at the left margin; each becomes the root of its own tree.

¿Se suben mis archivos?

No. El archivo lo abre y procesa tu propio navegador. Puedes comprobarlo tú mismo: abre la pestaña de red de tu navegador mientras usas la herramienta, o desconéctate de internet después de cargar la página.

¿Es gratis esta herramienta?

Sí, por completo. No hay registro, ni plan de pago, ni marca de agua en tus archivos.
