Divide la cuenta de una comida según quién pidió qué, con impuestos y propina.

Cómo funciona Divisor de cuenta de grupo por artículo

Add the people, list each item with its price and tick who shared it (no one ticked means everyone). Tax and tip are added in proportion to what each person ordered, and you get a clear amount per person to copy into the group chat.

Each item is divided equally between the people who shared it. Tip is a percentage of the items; tax is a fixed amount entered from the receipt. Both are distributed in proportion to each person's share of the subtotal. Shares are rounded to the cent and any leftover cent is handed out so the total of all shares equals the bill exactly.

Cómo usarla

  1. Enter the names of the people.

  2. Add the items and tick who shared each one.

  3. Enter tax and tip and copy the amounts.

Preguntas frecuentes

How are shared dishes handled?

Tick every person who shared the item; its price is divided equally between them.

Why is tax split proportionally?

Because someone who ordered more also caused more tax; you can still enter the tax as one amount.
