Sintesi vocale (leggi un testo ad alta voce)

Fai leggere un testo ad alta voce dal browser con la voce e la velocità che scegli.

Come funziona Sintesi vocale (leggi un testo ad alta voce)

Paste a text, choose one of the voices on your device, set speed, pitch and volume and press play. Useful for proofreading (mistakes are easy to hear), for learning pronunciation or for listening to an article instead of reading it. Pause, resume and stop at any time.

The tool uses the speech synthesis built into your browser and operating system. That means no text leaves your device, but also that the available voices and their quality depend on your computer or phone; install more voices in the system settings if the list is short. Long texts are split at sentence ends, because browsers cut off long utterances. Browsers do not allow pages to save the speech as an audio file, so there is no download; to capture it, record the tab or system audio with the Screen Recorder.

Come si usa

  1. Type or paste the text.

  2. Choose the voice (it should match the language of the text) and the speed.

  3. Press "Read aloud".

Domande frequenti

Why is my language missing?

Voices come from your system. Add the language in your operating system’s speech settings and reload the page.

Can I save the speech as MP3?

Not from this page, because browsers do not expose the audio. Use the Screen Recorder with system audio, or a dedicated text-to-speech service.

I miei file vengono caricati?

No. Il file viene aperto ed elaborato dal tuo browser. Puoi verificarlo tu stesso: apri la scheda Rete del browser mentre usi lo strumento oppure disconnettiti da internet dopo il caricamento della pagina.

Questo strumento è gratuito?

Sì, completamente. Nessuna registrazione, nessun piano a pagamento e nessuna filigrana sui tuoi file.
