Come funziona Ritaglia, ruota e capovolgi video

Fix a sideways phone video, cut a landscape clip to a vertical 9:16 format for Stories and Reels, make a square 1:1 version or mirror a selfie video. Crop to a standard aspect ratio around the centre or draw an exact rectangle (the red frame on the preview shows what stays), then rotate in 90° steps and flip horizontally or vertically.

Cropping is applied first, then rotation and flip. The result is re-encoded as MP4; dimensions are rounded to even numbers, which the video format requires. The video is processed by FFmpeg, the standard open-source video tool, compiled to WebAssembly and running inside your browser. The first use downloads the engine (about 31 MB), which your browser then keeps. Nothing is uploaded. Everything happens in your browser’s memory, so very large videos (several gigabytes) or long 4K clips can fail on a phone or an older computer; try a shorter or smaller clip in that case.

Come si usa

  1. Drop the video.

  2. Choose the crop (ratio or rectangle), rotation and flips.

  3. Press "Apply" and download.

Domande frequenti

My phone video plays sideways.

Choose a rotation of 90° or 270° until it looks right; the result is saved with the correct orientation.

How do I make a vertical video from a horizontal one?

Choose "To an aspect ratio" with 9:16. The centre of the picture is kept; use the rectangle mode to pick another part.

I miei file vengono caricati?

No. Il file viene aperto ed elaborato dal tuo browser. Puoi verificarlo tu stesso: apri la scheda Rete del browser mentre usi lo strumento oppure disconnettiti da internet dopo il caricamento della pagina.

Questo strumento è gratuito?

Sì, completamente. Nessuna registrazione, nessun piano a pagamento e nessuna filigrana sui tuoi file.
