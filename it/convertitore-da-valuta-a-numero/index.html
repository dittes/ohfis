Come funziona Convertitore da valuta a numero

Money in exported data arrives as text with symbols and mixed conventions: $1,234.56, 1.234,56 €, CHF 1'234.50, (500.00) or 12,5 zł. Excel cannot calculate with these. This tool converts selected columns to plain numbers with a dot as the decimal mark, and can add a column with the detected currency.

It strips symbols and letters, treats parentheses and minus signs as negatives, and then decides which character is the decimal mark: when both a dot and a comma appear the last one is the decimal mark; when only one appears several times it is a thousands separator. A lone separator followed by exactly three digits (1,234) is ambiguous, so you choose: thousands separator (default), US style or European style. Cells that cannot be read are listed instead of guessed.

Come si usa

  1. Paste your CSV and tick the amount columns.

  2. Choose how ambiguous values like 1,234 should be read.

  3. Download the numeric table.

Domande frequenti

How is "1,234" read?

By default as one thousand two hundred thirty-four. If your data is European, choose "European style" to read it as 1.234.

Are exchange rates applied?

No. Only the text is converted to numbers; the currencies are not converted.

I miei file vengono caricati?

No. Il file viene aperto ed elaborato dal tuo browser. Puoi verificarlo tu stesso: apri la scheda Rete del browser mentre usi lo strumento oppure disconnettiti da internet dopo il caricamento della pagina.

Questo strumento è gratuito?

Sì, completamente. Nessuna registrazione, nessun piano a pagamento e nessuna filigrana sui tuoi file.
