Come funziona Calcolatore da stipendio a tariffa da freelance

Freelancers who simply divide a salary by 2,080 hours undercharge by a third. This calculator adds what an employer pays for you (benefits, pension, insurance), your own business costs, unpaid weeks off and the non-billable time spent on admin and finding clients.

Rate = (salary × (1 + benefits %) + own costs) ÷ billable hours ÷ (1 − tax margin). Billable hours = (52 − weeks off) × hours per week × billable share. A 70% share is realistic for many freelancers. The tax margin lets you gross the rate up for taxes or risk. The day rate assumes 8 billable hours.

Come si usa

  1. Enter the salary you want to match and the benefits percentage.

  2. Add your costs, weeks off and billable share.

  3. Use the hourly and day rate as the floor for your pricing.

Domande frequenti

Why can't I just divide salary by 2,080?

Because you cannot bill every hour: admin, sales and holidays are unpaid, and you pay for your own insurance and equipment.

What billable share is realistic?

60–80% for consultants, lower while building a client base.
