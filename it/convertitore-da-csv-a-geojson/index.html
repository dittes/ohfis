Come funziona Convertitore da CSV a GeoJSON

Put a spreadsheet of places on a map. Choose the latitude and longitude columns (they are detected from names like lat, lon, latitude) and get a standard GeoJSON FeatureCollection; every other column becomes a property of the point. The result opens in geojson.io, QGIS, Mapbox, Leaflet and most GIS software.

Coordinates are parsed strictly: 52.5 and 52,5 are accepted, but text such as "52,5abc" or values outside −90…90 and −180…180 are skipped and listed, rather than silently producing a wrong location. GeoJSON stores positions as [longitude, latitude], the reverse of how people write them, and the tool takes care of the order. Everything is converted in your browser.

Come si usa

  1. Paste the CSV and check the latitude and longitude columns.

  2. Review the list of skipped rows, if any.

  3. Copy or download the .geojson file.

Domande frequenti

Why are my points in the ocean?

Latitude and longitude are probably swapped. GeoJSON uses [longitude, latitude]; select the columns in the right order.

Can it geocode addresses?

No. That needs an online service. This tool converts coordinates you already have.

I miei file vengono caricati?

No. Il file viene aperto ed elaborato dal tuo browser. Puoi verificarlo tu stesso: apri la scheda Rete del browser mentre usi lo strumento oppure disconnettiti da internet dopo il caricamento della pagina.

Questo strumento è gratuito?

Sì, completamente. Nessuna registrazione, nessun piano a pagamento e nessuna filigrana sui tuoi file.
