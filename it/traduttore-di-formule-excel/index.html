Come funziona Traduttore di formule Excel (EN ⇄ DE, FR, ES)

A formula from an English tutorial does not work in a German Excel: it wants SUMME instead of SUM, WENN instead of IF and semicolons instead of commas. Paste the formula, pick the languages and get a version your Excel accepts. It works the other way round, too, which helps when you share files with colleagues abroad.

About 90 common functions are mapped between English, German, French and Spanish, including lookup, logic, text, date, statistics and finance functions. The formula is tokenised, so function names are replaced only where a name is followed by an opening bracket; text inside quotes, sheet names in apostrophes and array constants are never touched. Argument separators change between "," and ";" and decimal marks between "." and ",", following each language's convention. Unknown or custom functions are listed instead of being guessed.

Come si usa

  1. Paste one formula per line.

  2. Choose the language it is written in and the language you need.

  3. Copy the translated formulas.

Domande frequenti

Why does Excel use semicolons in some countries?

Where the comma is the decimal mark (Germany, France, Spain and many more), the comma cannot separate arguments, so the semicolon is used.

What about Google Sheets?

Google Sheets uses the English function names in every language; only the separators follow your locale. Translate to the language that matches your separators.
