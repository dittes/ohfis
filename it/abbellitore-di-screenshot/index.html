Abbellitore di screenshot (cornice, ombra, sfumatura)

Racchiudi gli screenshot in una cornice macOS, Windows o browser su uno sfondo sfumato.

Caricamento dello strumento…

  • Nessun upload
  • Gratis
  • Nessun account

Come funziona Abbellitore di screenshot (cornice, ombra, sfumatura)

Turn a plain screenshot into a polished image for documentation, a blog or social media: choose a macOS, Windows or browser window frame, a gradient background, padding, rounded corners and a soft shadow. Paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V and download or copy the result as PNG.

The result is composed on a canvas: a linear gradient with your two colours and angle as background, a rounded rectangle with a blurred drop shadow as the window, a title bar drawn with vector shapes (traffic-light buttons, window controls, an address bar) and your screenshot clipped into the window. Everything is rendered at the screenshot's full resolution, so text stays sharp.

Come si usa

  1. Drop or paste a screenshot.

  2. Pick a frame, colours, padding and shadow.

  3. Download the PNG or copy it to the clipboard.

Domande frequenti

Can I paste a screenshot directly?

Yes: take it with your system shortcut and press Ctrl+V (⌘V on Mac) on this page.

Is the picture uploaded?

No. The image is composed locally in your browser.

I miei file vengono caricati?

No. Il file viene aperto ed elaborato dal tuo browser. Puoi verificarlo tu stesso: apri la scheda Rete del browser mentre usi lo strumento oppure disconnettiti da internet dopo il caricamento della pagina.

Questo strumento è gratuito?

Sì, completamente. Nessuna registrazione, nessun piano a pagamento e nessuna filigrana sui tuoi file.
