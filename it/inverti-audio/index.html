Come funziona Inverti audio (riproduci il suono al contrario)

Drop a recording and get it back reversed, ready to play or download as WAV or MP3. Reversed sound is used for effects in music and video, for puzzles and for fun with spoken words.

The samples of every channel are simply read from the end to the start, so nothing is lost and reversing twice gives back the original exactly. Long recordings are reversed in memory, which is instant for minutes of audio.

Come si usa

  1. Drop the audio file.

  2. Listen to the reversed version.

  3. Download it as WAV or MP3.

Domande frequenti

Can I reverse part of a recording?

Cut the part out first with the Audio Cutter, reverse it here and join the pieces with the Audio Joiner.

Is reversing lossless?

Yes for WAV. MP3 output is compressed once more.

I miei file vengono caricati?

No. Il file viene aperto ed elaborato dal tuo browser. Puoi verificarlo tu stesso: apri la scheda Rete del browser mentre usi lo strumento oppure disconnettiti da internet dopo il caricamento della pagina.

Questo strumento è gratuito?

Sì, completamente. Nessuna registrazione, nessun piano a pagamento e nessuna filigrana sui tuoi file.
