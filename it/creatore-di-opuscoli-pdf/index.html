Creatore di opuscoli PDF (stampa come opuscolo piegato)

Riordina le pagine del PDF in modo che una stampa fronte-retro si pieghi in un opuscolo.

Come funziona Creatore di opuscoli PDF (stampa come opuscolo piegato)

Upload a PDF and get a new PDF in which two pages sit side by side on each landscape sheet, in the order that makes a stack of double-sided sheets fold into a booklet. Print it duplex, flip on the short edge, fold the stack in half and staple the fold.

The tool uses saddle-stitch imposition. The page count is padded with blank pages up to a multiple of 4; for an 8-page document the sheet sides are 8|1, 2|7, 6|3 and 4|5, so that after folding the pages follow each other correctly. Pages are scaled to fit half a sheet while keeping their proportions; you can choose automatic size (two pages side by side), A4, A3, Letter or Tabloid sheets, and right-to-left binding for Arabic, Hebrew or manga. Processing is done with pdf-lib in your browser, so the document is not uploaded. Print at 100% (actual size), choose "flip on short edge" for duplex and test with a 4-page file first. Very thick booklets need "creep" compensation which is not included.

Come si usa

  1. Choose the PDF; pages are read locally.

  2. Pick the sheet size and binding direction.

  3. Download, print double-sided (flip on the short edge) and fold.

Domande frequenti

What page count works best?

A multiple of 4 (8, 12, 16 …). Other counts are padded with blank pages at the end.

Which printer setting do I need?

Two-sided, "flip on short edge", scale 100% / actual size, landscape.

I miei file vengono caricati?

No. Il file viene aperto ed elaborato dal tuo browser. Puoi verificarlo tu stesso: apri la scheda Rete del browser mentre usi lo strumento oppure disconnettiti da internet dopo il caricamento della pagina.

Questo strumento è gratuito?

Sì, completamente. Nessuna registrazione, nessun piano a pagamento e nessuna filigrana sui tuoi file.
