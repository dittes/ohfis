Scheda colore dello schermo (bilanciamento del bianco e luminosità)

Una scheda grigia, bianca o colorata a schermo intero per il bilanciamento del bianco della webcam e i controlli dello schermo.

Come funziona Scheda colore dello schermo (bilanciamento del bianco e luminosità)

Show a neutral grey (18 %), daylight white (6500 K), warm (3200 K) or cool (7500 K) card, any colour you pick, or a grey scale with 256 steps, and go fullscreen. Hold it in front of a webcam to set white balance, use it as a soft light for video calls, or check a display for tint, uneven brightness and stuck pixels.

The colour temperatures are approximations of the usual sRGB values for the named white points; real calibration needs a measuring device (colorimeter) because every display is different. The card is a plain element filled with a CSS colour, and fullscreen uses the Fullscreen API, so nothing is uploaded or recorded. For white balance, point the camera at the card so it fills the frame and use your camera app's "set white balance" or "click to white balance" function.

Come si usa

  1. Choose a preset or pick a colour.

  2. Press Fullscreen.

  3. Hold it in front of the camera or inspect the screen.

Domande frequenti

Which card should I use for white balance?

The neutral grey card is the best reference; a white card works if it is not overexposed.

Can this calibrate my monitor?

No. It helps to spot problems; calibration needs a hardware colorimeter.

I miei file vengono caricati?

No. Il file viene aperto ed elaborato dal tuo browser. Puoi verificarlo tu stesso: apri la scheda Rete del browser mentre usi lo strumento oppure disconnettiti da internet dopo il caricamento della pagina.

Questo strumento è gratuito?

Sì, completamente. Nessuna registrazione, nessun piano a pagamento e nessuna filigrana sui tuoi file.
