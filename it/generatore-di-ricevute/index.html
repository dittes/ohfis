Crea una ricevuta di pagamento, con IVA indicata e riga per la firma.

Come funziona Generatore di ricevute (PDF)

Enter who paid, who received the money, the amount, what it was for, the payment method and, if you like, the VAT rate included in the amount. The tool splits the gross amount into net and VAT and creates a one-page PDF receipt with a signature line. The document language can be English, German, Polish or Czech.

A receipt confirms that a payment was made, for example for a cash sale, a deposit or rent. It does not replace an invoice where one is required, and rules for what a receipt must contain differ between countries and between businesses and private persons. The tool lays out the document; it does not give tax or legal advice. The PDF is made in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

Come si usa

  1. Enter issuer, payer, number, date and amount.

  2. Set the VAT rate included in the amount (0 for none) and what the payment was for.

  3. Create the PDF, print it and sign it.

Domande frequenti

Is a receipt the same as an invoice?

No. A receipt proves a payment; an invoice requests it and usually carries more mandatory details. Check what you need.

How is the VAT calculated?

The amount is treated as gross: net = amount ÷ (1 + rate), VAT = amount − net.

I miei file vengono caricati?

No. Il file viene aperto ed elaborato dal tuo browser. Puoi verificarlo tu stesso: apri la scheda Rete del browser mentre usi lo strumento oppure disconnettiti da internet dopo il caricamento della pagina.

Questo strumento è gratuito?

Sì, completamente. Nessuna registrazione, nessun piano a pagamento e nessuna filigrana sui tuoi file.
