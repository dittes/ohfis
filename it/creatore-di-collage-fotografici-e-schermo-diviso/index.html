Combina 2–4 immagini affiancate, impilate, in griglia o come confronto prima/dopo.

Come funziona Creatore di collage fotografici e schermo diviso

Add several images and place them side by side, stacked, in a 2 × 2 grid or as a split screen with a divider for before-and-after comparisons. Choose the canvas shape (16:9, 4:3, square, vertical), the width, gap, rounded corners and background colour, then download the result as PNG or JPEG.

Images are scaled to cover their cell and centred, so edges may be cropped but nothing is stretched. The canvas size is calculated from the chosen width and aspect ratio (a width of 1600 px at 16:9 gives 1600 × 900). The split screen uses the full canvas for both images and cuts the second one at the middle, so the same part of each picture lines up, which is what a before/after needs. All drawing is done on a canvas in your browser. Up to 4 images (6 for the row and column layouts) are used.

Come si usa

  1. Add the images in order.

  2. Choose the layout, size, gap and colours.

  3. Download the collage.

Domande frequenti

How do I make a before/after picture?

Add the two images and choose "Split screen with divider".

Why is my image cropped?

Each image fills its cell without distortion. Choose another canvas shape to see more of it.

I miei file vengono caricati?

No. Il file viene aperto ed elaborato dal tuo browser. Puoi verificarlo tu stesso: apri la scheda Rete del browser mentre usi lo strumento oppure disconnettiti da internet dopo il caricamento della pagina.

Questo strumento è gratuito?

Sì, completamente. Nessuna registrazione, nessun piano a pagamento e nessuna filigrana sui tuoi file.
