Come funziona Taglia audio (taglia MP3 e WAV online)

Drop an audio file, enter where the part you want starts and ends, and save it as WAV or MP3. You can keep only that part (to make a ringtone, a sound bite or a trimmed interview) or cut it out and join the rest (to remove a cough or a mistake). Optional fade in and fade out avoid clicks at the cut points.

The file is decoded by your browser, the samples are cut with exact sample accuracy and the result is encoded again, all on your device. MP3 output uses the LAME encoder; WAV keeps every detail. The tool works with times in seconds, which is precise but needs you to listen first: play the original, note the times, and check the result in the preview player before you download it.

Come si usa

  1. Drop the audio file.

  2. Choose "keep" or "remove", then enter start and end in seconds.

  3. Listen to the preview and download the WAV or MP3.

Domande frequenti

Which formats can I open?

Anything your browser can play: MP3, M4A/AAC, OGG, Opus, FLAC, WAV and the sound of most video files.

Does cutting reduce the quality?

Not with WAV. If you save as MP3, the audio is compressed again once, which costs a little quality; use 256 or 320 kbps to keep it small.

I miei file vengono caricati?

No. Il file viene aperto ed elaborato dal tuo browser. Puoi verificarlo tu stesso: apri la scheda Rete del browser mentre usi lo strumento oppure disconnettiti da internet dopo il caricamento della pagina.

Questo strumento è gratuito?

Sì, completamente. Nessuna registrazione, nessun piano a pagamento e nessuna filigrana sui tuoi file.
