Come funziona Lavagna delle cinque forze di Porter (post-it)

Porter's Five Forces explain how attractive an industry is: the threat of new entrants, the bargaining power of suppliers and of buyers, the threat of substitutes and the rivalry between existing competitors. Write the facts that support each force on sticky notes and see where the pressure on your margins comes from.

The board is saved in this browser and nothing is uploaded. Export it as PNG or Markdown for a strategy document. Use it before you enter a market, set prices or look for a niche.

Come si usa

  1. Name the industry or market in the board name.

  2. Add evidence for each of the five forces on sticky notes.

  3. Discuss which forces are strongest and export the board.

Domande frequenti

Which force matters most?

It depends on the industry. Look for the force with the most and strongest notes: that is where profits are squeezed.

Is this a score?

No, it is a structured collection of evidence. Judging low, medium or high is up to your team.

Is my data uploaded?

No. Everything stays in your browser.

I miei file vengono caricati?

No. Il file viene aperto ed elaborato dal tuo browser. Puoi verificarlo tu stesso: apri la scheda Rete del browser mentre usi lo strumento oppure disconnettiti da internet dopo il caricamento della pagina.

Questo strumento è gratuito?

Sì, completamente. Nessuna registrazione, nessun piano a pagamento e nessuna filigrana sui tuoi file.
