Come funziona Calcolatore di capitale circolante e indice di liquidità

Check whether a business can pay its short-term bills. Enter current assets, current liabilities and the inventory and cash included in the assets to get net working capital and the three standard liquidity ratios.

Working capital = current assets − current liabilities. Current ratio = current assets ÷ current liabilities. Quick (acid-test) ratio = (current assets − inventory) ÷ current liabilities, which ignores stock that is hard to sell quickly. Cash ratio = cash ÷ current liabilities, the strictest test. A current ratio of about 1.2–2 is considered comfortable, but norms vary a lot by industry (retailers with fast-moving stock run lower).

Come si usa

  1. Enter current assets and liabilities.

  2. Add the inventory and cash portions.

  3. Read the ratios against your industry's norm.

Domande frequenti

Is a high current ratio always good?

Not always: it can mean idle cash or slow-moving inventory.

What counts as a current asset?

Cash, receivables, inventory and anything expected to turn into cash within a year.
