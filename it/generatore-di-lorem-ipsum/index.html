Come funziona Generatore di Lorem Ipsum

Choose how many paragraphs, sentences or words you need, whether to start with the classic "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet", and optionally wrap the paragraphs in <p> tags. Copy or download the text for a mock-up, a template or a layout test.

The words come from a Latin-looking list and are put together by a seeded random generator, so the same settings always produce the same text until you ask for a new one. The text has no meaning, which is the point: it lets you judge the look of a layout without being distracted by the content. For realistic data such as names and addresses use the Fake Data Generator.

Come si usa

  1. Choose the amount and the unit.

  2. Decide on the classic start and HTML tags.

  3. Copy the text, or press "Generate new text" for a different one.

Domande frequenti

Is Lorem Ipsum real Latin?

It is scrambled from a text by Cicero, so it looks like Latin but has no meaning.

Can I get text in another language?

Not here. For names, addresses and numbers in German or English see the Fake Data Generator.

I miei file vengono caricati?

No. Il file viene aperto ed elaborato dal tuo browser. Puoi verificarlo tu stesso: apri la scheda Rete del browser mentre usi lo strumento oppure disconnettiti da internet dopo il caricamento della pagina.

Questo strumento è gratuito?

Sì, completamente. Nessuna registrazione, nessun piano a pagamento e nessuna filigrana sui tuoi file.
