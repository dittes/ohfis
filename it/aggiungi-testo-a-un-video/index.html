Aggiungi testo a un video

Metti un titolo, una didascalia o un messaggio su un video, nella posizione e nel momento che scegli.

Caricamento dello strumento…

  • Nessun upload
  • Gratis
  • Nessun account
  • Funziona offline dopo il caricamento

Come funziona Aggiungi testo a un video

Type a text, choose size, colour and one of nine positions, optionally a dark box behind it for legibility and a start and end time, and get the video with the text burned in. Good for titles, captions, a name, a date or a call to action.

The text is drawn with the DejaVu Sans font, which covers Latin (including umlauts and accents), Greek and Cyrillic letters; there is no emoji or Asian script support. For subtitles that follow speech, use a dedicated subtitle editor. The video is processed by FFmpeg, the standard open-source video tool, compiled to WebAssembly and running inside your browser. The first use downloads the engine (about 31 MB), which your browser then keeps. Nothing is uploaded. Everything happens in your browser’s memory, so very large videos (several gigabytes) or long 4K clips can fail on a phone or an older computer; try a shorter or smaller clip in that case.

Come si usa

  1. Drop the video.

  2. Type the text and choose size, colour and position.

  3. Set the time range if needed, press "Add text" and download.

Domande frequenti

Can I show the text only for a few seconds?

Yes. Enter "from" and "until" in seconds; use the "current position" buttons while the video is paused at the right moment.

Can I add several texts?

One per run. Run the result through the tool again to add another.

I miei file vengono caricati?

No. Il file viene aperto ed elaborato dal tuo browser. Puoi verificarlo tu stesso: apri la scheda Rete del browser mentre usi lo strumento oppure disconnettiti da internet dopo il caricamento della pagina.

Questo strumento è gratuito?

Sì, completamente. Nessuna registrazione, nessun piano a pagamento e nessuna filigrana sui tuoi file.
