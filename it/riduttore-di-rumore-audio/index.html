Come funziona Riduttore di rumore audio (rimuovi il rumore di fondo)

Drop a recording and reduce steady background noise such as hiss, hum, fan or traffic rumble, by 6 to 40 dB. The noise level is learned automatically from the quietest tenth of the recording, or from a stretch you mark as noise-only, and then pushed down wherever the sound is not clearly louder than the noise. Listen to the result, compare, and download WAV or MP3.

The method is spectral gating with soft gains: the sound is split into frequencies over short time slices, each frequency is compared with its learned noise level and attenuated if it does not stand out. It works well on constant noise and poorly on changing noise such as voices in the background or clattering, where no clean noise sample exists. Strong settings can make speech sound thin or watery; start low. It does not use AI, runs entirely in your browser and your file is never uploaded.

Come si usa

  1. Drop the recording.

  2. Set the reduction and, if you know a noise-only stretch, its start and end.

  3. Listen to the preview and download the result.

Domande frequenti

Why does the voice sound watery?

The setting is too strong. Lower the reduction (dB) or the sensitivity.

Can it remove music or voices in the background?

No. It only reduces steady noise. Changing sounds cannot be separated this way.

I miei file vengono caricati?

No. Il file viene aperto ed elaborato dal tuo browser. Puoi verificarlo tu stesso: apri la scheda Rete del browser mentre usi lo strumento oppure disconnettiti da internet dopo il caricamento della pagina.

Questo strumento è gratuito?

Sì, completamente. Nessuna registrazione, nessun piano a pagamento e nessuna filigrana sui tuoi file.
