Cambia volume audio (rendi MP3 più forti o più bassi)

Rendi una registrazione più forte o più bassa di un certo numero di decibel.

Come funziona Cambia volume audio (rendi MP3 più forti o più bassi)

Enter how many decibels louder (+) or quieter (−) the recording should be, listen to the result and save it as WAV or MP3. +6 dB is roughly twice as loud, −6 dB half. Fade in and out can be added in the same step.

The gain is applied to every sample. Making a recording louder can push the loudest peaks over the maximum and cause harsh distortion (clipping), so by default the tool scales the result back just enough to avoid it and tells you how much it reduced. If you want the loudest point to reach an exact level automatically, use the Audio Normalizer instead.

Come si usa

  1. Drop the audio file.

  2. Enter the change in dB (positive = louder).

  3. Check the preview and the clipping note, then download.

Domande frequenti

How many dB is twice as loud?

About +6 dB doubles the signal level; people usually hear +10 dB as twice as loud.

Why is the result not as loud as I asked?

The clipping guard limited it. Turn the option off to allow distortion, or use a compressor-like normalisation for very quiet recordings.

I miei file vengono caricati?

No. Il file viene aperto ed elaborato dal tuo browser. Puoi verificarlo tu stesso: apri la scheda Rete del browser mentre usi lo strumento oppure disconnettiti da internet dopo il caricamento della pagina.

Questo strumento è gratuito?

Sì, completamente. Nessuna registrazione, nessun piano a pagamento e nessuna filigrana sui tuoi file.
