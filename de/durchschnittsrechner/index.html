So funktioniert: Durchschnittsrechner (Mittelwert, Median, Modus)

Die ausführliche Erklärung ist auf Englisch.

Paste numbers separated by spaces, commas, semicolons or new lines. You get the arithmetic mean, the median, the mode(s), the minimum, maximum and range, the sum and the count.

The median is the middle value of the sorted list, or the mean of the two middle values for an even count; it is less sensitive to outliers than the mean.

So benutzt du es

  1. Paste or type your numbers.

  2. Read the statistics.

  3. Copy the result.

Häufige Fragen

What if there are several modes?

All of them are listed. If no value repeats there is no mode.

Mean or median?

Use the median when a few extreme values would distort the mean, for example incomes or house prices.
