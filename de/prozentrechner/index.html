So funktioniert: Prozentrechner

Die ausführliche Erklärung ist auf Englisch.

Six everyday percentage questions in one place: what is 15% of 200, what percent is 30 of 200, by how much did a value change from 80 to 100, which number is 15% of what, and what is 100 plus or minus 19%.

Percent change = (new − old) ÷ |old| × 100. Adding a percentage multiplies by 1 + p ÷ 100, so 100 plus 19% is 119, but taking 19% off 119 does not return to 100.

So benutzt du es

  1. Choose the question you want answered.

  2. Enter the two numbers.

  3. Read the result and the formula.

Häufige Fragen

How do I calculate a percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new one, divide by the old value and multiply by 100.

Why is +20% then −20% not back to the start?

The second percentage is taken from a larger (or smaller) base. 100 → 120 → 96.
