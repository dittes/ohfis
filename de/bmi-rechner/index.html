So funktioniert: BMI-Rechner (Body-Mass-Index)

Die ausführliche Erklärung ist auf Englisch.

Enter weight and height in metric or imperial units. BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². The result is placed in the WHO categories from underweight to obesity, with BMI Prime (BMI ÷ 25) and the weight range that corresponds to a BMI of 18.5–24.9 for your height.

BMI is a rough screening number. It ignores muscle mass, age, sex and fat distribution, so an athlete can have a high BMI and be healthy. It is not a diagnosis; ask a doctor about your health.

So benutzt du es

  1. Choose metric or imperial.

  2. Enter weight and height.

  3. Read BMI, category and healthy range.

Häufige Fragen

What is a normal BMI?

18.5 to 24.9 for adults, according to the WHO.

Does it work for children?

No. Children use age- and sex-specific percentiles.
