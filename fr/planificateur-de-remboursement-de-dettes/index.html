Comment fonctionne Planificateur de remboursement de dettes (boule de neige ou avalanche)

List your debts with balance, interest rate and minimum payment, enter what you can pay in total each month, and compare the two classic strategies: avalanche (highest interest first) and snowball (smallest balance first). You see the months to debt-free, the total interest and the order in which debts disappear.

Each month interest is added to every balance, the minimum is paid on each debt, and everything left of your budget goes to the target debt of the chosen strategy; when a debt is paid off its minimum payment rolls into the next target. Avalanche always pays the least interest; snowball often feels better because small debts vanish early. The budget must at least cover all minimums.

Mode d'emploi

  1. Enter each debt on its own line.

  2. Enter your monthly payment budget.

  3. Compare the two strategies and choose one.

Questions fréquentes

Which method saves more money?

Avalanche, because it pays down the most expensive debt first. Snowball can be better if motivation is the problem.

What if my budget is below the minimums?

The calculator tells you; you cannot pay off debts that grow faster than you pay.
