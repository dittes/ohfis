Comment fonctionne Formateur de formules Excel

A formula with five nested IFs in the formula bar is nearly impossible to debug. Paste it here and get it back with one argument per line, indented by nesting level, and with spaces around operators. Short parts stay on one line. In Excel, press Alt+Enter in the formula bar to add such line breaks; they do not change the result.

The formula is tokenised (function names, strings, references, numbers and operators) and parsed into a tree of function calls. A call that fits within your width limit is printed on one line; a longer call is expanded with each argument on its own line. Text in quotes and sheet references are preserved exactly, and the formatted formula contains the same characters as the original apart from spaces and line breaks.

Mode d'emploi

  1. Paste the formula, with or without the leading =.

  2. Set the maximum line width and indent size.

  3. Copy the formatted formula back into the formula bar.

Questions fréquentes

Do line breaks change what the formula does?

No. Excel ignores spaces and line breaks outside of text in quotes.

Does it check the formula for errors?

It does not evaluate formulas. If parentheses do not match, you will see it in the indentation.

Mes fichiers sont-ils envoyés ?

Non. Le fichier est ouvert et traité par votre propre navigateur. Vous pouvez le vérifier vous-même : ouvrez l'onglet réseau de votre navigateur pendant l'utilisation de l'outil, ou coupez internet après le chargement de la page.
