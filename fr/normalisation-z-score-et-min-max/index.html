Normalisation z-score et min-max

Standardisez des colonnes numériques en z-scores ou ramenez-les à l'échelle 0–1.

Comment fonctionne Normalisation z-score et min-max

Put columns with different units on the same scale before comparing or modelling them. Choose z-score (how many standard deviations from the mean), min–max scaling (0 to 1) or max-abs scaling (−1 to 1), pick the numeric columns and download the table with the new columns. The mean, standard deviation, minimum and maximum of every column are shown.

Z-score is (x − mean) ÷ standard deviation; the standard deviation can be the sample version (divide by n − 1, the default, like Excel's STDEV.S) or the population version (n). Min–max is (x − min) ÷ (max − min); if all values are equal, the result is 0 instead of a division by zero. Non-numeric cells are left untouched and ignored in the statistics, and the new header is the old one plus the method.

Mode d'emploi

  1. Paste your CSV and tick the numeric columns.

  2. Choose z-score, min–max or max-abs and the decimals.

  3. Copy or download the normalised table.

Questions fréquentes

Sample or population standard deviation?

Use sample (n−1) when your rows are a sample of a larger group, which is the usual case. Population (n) when you have every member.

What is a z-score of 2?

The value is two standard deviations above the column mean. In a normal distribution about 2.3% of values are that high.
