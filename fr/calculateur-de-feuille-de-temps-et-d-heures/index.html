Comment fonctionne Calculateur de feuille de temps et d'heures supplémentaires

Enter the start time, end time and unpaid break for each day, and the calculator adds up the hours worked, the overtime and, with an hourly rate, the gross pay. Times can be typed as 9:00, 0900 or 9am; shifts that cross midnight are handled.

For each day the break is subtracted from the time between start and end (adding 24 hours if the end is earlier than the start). Weekly overtime is every hour above the threshold you set (40 by default); an optional daily threshold counts hours above it per day, and the larger of the daily and weekly overtime is used so hours are never counted twice. Overtime pay uses your multiplier (1.5 by default). Laws and contracts differ by country, so treat the result as a calculation aid, not payroll advice.

Mode d'emploi

  1. Fill in start, end and break for each day worked.

  2. Set the overtime thresholds, and optionally the rate and multiplier.

  3. Read the totals and copy them into your payroll or invoice.

Questions fréquentes

How are night shifts handled?

If the end time is earlier than the start time, the shift is assumed to end the next day.

Does it follow my country's overtime law?

It applies the thresholds you enter. Check local rules for daily limits, rest periods and premiums.
