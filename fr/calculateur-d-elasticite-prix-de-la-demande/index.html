Comment fonctionne Calculateur d'élasticité-prix de la demande

Enter two price points and the units sold at each and learn whether demand is elastic (customers react strongly) or inelastic (they barely react), and what the change did to revenue.

The midpoint formula divides the percentage change in quantity by the percentage change in price, each measured against the average of old and new value, so the answer is the same whichever point you start from. An elasticity below −1 is elastic: raising the price loses more in volume than it gains per unit, so revenue falls. Between −1 and 0 it is inelastic and revenue rises with the price. Real demand is affected by many things besides price, so treat the figure as one data point.

Mode d'emploi

  1. Enter the old and new price and the units sold at each.

  2. Read the elasticity and its type.

  3. Compare revenue before and after.

Questions fréquentes

What does an elasticity of −2 mean?

A 1% price increase reduces quantity by about 2%.

Which products are inelastic?

Necessities and products without close substitutes, such as medicines or fuel.
