Comment fonctionne Calculateur de runway et de burn rate SaaS

Enter your cash, monthly costs and current revenue. The calculator shows your net burn, the runway if nothing changes, and the runway if revenue keeps growing at a monthly rate, plus a chart of the cash balance month by month.

Net burn is costs − revenue. The simple runway is cash ÷ net burn. For the growth scenario revenue is multiplied by (1 + growth) every month while costs stay constant, and the cash balance is simulated until it reaches zero (the exact month is interpolated) or 60 months pass. If revenue overtakes costs the company becomes cash-flow positive and the runway is not limited. The model keeps costs flat, so add planned hires to the monthly costs.

Mode d'emploi

  1. Enter cash, monthly costs, revenue and growth.

  2. Read the runway with and without growth.

  3. Adjust costs or growth to see how many months you gain.

Questions fréquentes

How much runway should a startup have?

Most investors suggest 18–24 months after a raise, and treat under 6–9 months as urgent because fundraising takes time.

Is growth modelled as compounding?

Yes, revenue grows by the same percentage every month.
