Comment fonctionne Page de test d'imprimante (alignement, échelle et couleur)

Print this one-page PDF to find out whether your printer scales the page, cuts off the edges, prints colours and greys evenly and keeps thin lines and small text sharp. It has a 100 mm bar to measure with a ruler, a frame 5 mm from every edge with corner crosshairs, 11 grey steps, colour blocks, lines from 0.1 to 1 pt, five text sizes and a solid black bar for banding. An optional second page lets you check front and back alignment against the light.

The sheet is created in your browser with exact millimetre positions on A4, Letter or another size. Print it at 100 % ("Actual size") because "Fit to page" would falsify the scale test. If the bar is not exactly 100 mm or the frame is cut off, the printer driver is scaling or the printer has an unprintable margin. The colour blocks are RGB values and show how the printer renders them; they are not a calibration. For a quick check of the whole print chain and for screen tests see the print test page on test.institute.

Mode d'emploi

  1. Choose the paper size.

  2. Create and print the PDF at 100 % scale.

  3. Measure the bar and check the frame, greys and text.

Questions fréquentes

The 100 mm bar prints too short. Why?

The printer or the PDF viewer is scaling the page. Select "Actual size" or "100 %" in the print dialog.

What causes light stripes in the black bar?

Clogged inkjet nozzles or a low toner level. Run the printer's cleaning cycle.

Mes fichiers sont-ils envoyés ?

Non. Le fichier est ouvert et traité par votre propre navigateur. Vous pouvez le vérifier vous-même : ouvrez l'onglet réseau de votre navigateur pendant l'utilisation de l'outil, ou coupez internet après le chargement de la page.
