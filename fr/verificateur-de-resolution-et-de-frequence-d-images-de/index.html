Comment fonctionne Vérificateur de résolution et de fréquence d'images de webcam

Start the camera and read its real resolution, the frame rate it delivers right now (measured live), the maximum resolution and frame rate the camera reports, and its aspect ratio. Request 720p, 1080p, 4K or 60 fps and see what you actually get. Switch between several cameras.

The values come from the MediaStreamTrack: getSettings() returns what the camera currently does, getCapabilities() what the driver offers. The measured frame rate counts the frames that arrive in the video element over the last 90 frames (requestVideoFrameCallback), which reveals cameras that drop to 15 fps in low light or over a slow USB port. Requested values are only "ideal": the browser picks the closest supported mode, so the numbers tell you the truth. Video is displayed locally only.

Mode d'emploi

  1. Press Start camera and allow access.

  2. Choose a camera, resolution and frame rate to request.

  3. Read the real values and the measured frame rate.

Questions fréquentes

Why is my 1080p camera showing a lower resolution?

The USB port, the cable, the video call app or low light can limit the mode. Try another port and request the resolution here.

Why does the frame rate drop in a dark room?

Many cameras lengthen the exposure time in low light, which lowers the frame rate.

Mes fichiers sont-ils envoyés ?

Non. Le fichier est ouvert et traité par votre propre navigateur. Vous pouvez le vérifier vous-même : ouvrez l'onglet réseau de votre navigateur pendant l'utilisation de l'outil, ou coupez internet après le chargement de la page.

Cet outil est-il gratuit ?

Oui, entièrement. Pas d'inscription, pas d'offre payante et pas de filigrane sur vos fichiers.
