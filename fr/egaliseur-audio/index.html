Comment fonctionne Égaliseur audio (graves et aigus en ligne)

Pick a preset (bass boost, treble boost, voice clarity, warm and soft) or set five bands yourself: 60 Hz, 250 Hz, 1 kHz, 4 kHz and 12 kHz, each from −12 to +12 dB. The result is rendered, played back for you to check and saved as WAV or MP3.

Each band is a standard biquad filter from the Audio EQ Cookbook: shelving filters for the lowest and highest band and peaking filters with a moderate width in between. Boosting bands can raise the overall level, so the tool scales the result down if it would clip. An equaliser cannot repair a bad recording, but a few dB at the right place makes speech clearer or music warmer.

Mode d'emploi

  1. Drop the audio file.

  2. Choose a preset or move the five bands.

  3. Listen and download.

Questions fréquentes

Which band improves speech?

A little at 4 kHz (+3 to +5 dB) makes speech clearer; cutting 60–250 Hz reduces rumble and boominess.

Why is the result quieter than expected?

When boosts would make the signal clip, the tool lowers the whole result by the needed amount.
