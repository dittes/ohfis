Supprimer les données EXIF et GPS des photos

Retirez localisation, appareil et date des métadonnées de JPG, PNG et WebP sans réencodage.

Comment fonctionne Supprimer les données EXIF et GPS des photos

Photos from phones carry a hidden label: where they were taken, the exact time, the camera or phone model and sometimes a serial number. Drop your pictures to see what was found and get clean copies for sharing. The picture itself is not touched, so quality stays exactly the same.

For JPEG the tool walks through the file's segments and drops the APP1 (EXIF and XMP), APP13 (IPTC) and comment segments; for PNG it removes the tEXt, iTXt, zTXt, eXIf and tIME chunks; for WebP it removes the EXIF and XMP chunks and clears their flags. The compressed image data is copied byte for byte. By default the colour profile and the rotation flag are kept so that colours and orientation do not change; a minimal orientation tag is re-added for JPEGs. GPS coordinates, device and other findings are listed per file. Other formats are re-saved as PNG.

Mode d'emploi

  1. Drop your photos.

  2. Read what was removed for each file.

  3. Download the clean copies (or a ZIP).

Questions fréquentes

Does removing metadata reduce quality?

No. For JPG, PNG and WebP the image data is copied unchanged; only the metadata blocks are cut out.

Do social networks remove metadata?

Many do on upload, but not all, and not when you send a photo by email or chat as a file. Clean it before sharing.

Mes fichiers sont-ils envoyés ?

Non. Le fichier est ouvert et traité par votre propre navigateur. Vous pouvez le vérifier vous-même : ouvrez l'onglet réseau de votre navigateur pendant l'utilisation de l'outil, ou coupez internet après le chargement de la page.

Oui, entièrement. Pas d'inscription, pas d'offre payante et pas de filigrane sur vos fichiers.
